# OSRS Path 2.79.0

Smithing, Cooking and Crafting get the same engine as combat and gathering.

- **One answer with a real rate for the processing skills.** The Skills page now works out XP per hour for your level in Smithing (bronze items, platebodies at the Varrock anvils), Cooking (meat, trout and tuna at the Barbarian Village fire), and Crafting (leather by a bank, gold jewellery at a furnace, gems by a bank). Each rate counts a full bag of materials, the wiki's own action time, the walk to the bank and back, and the chance to burn.
- **The best station is picked for you.** For example Edgeville, Al Kharid or Falador furnace, by the wiki's distance from each to its bank.
- **Costs are counted.** Materials are a cost at their price and the product a gain at its sale price after tax. When either price is unknown, no profit is claimed.
- **Checked against the wiki.** The model's range contains the wiki guide's own rates, e.g. steel platebodies 150k XP/h at the Varrock West anvil, gold items 1,350 an hour at Edgeville.
- **Supplies you already own come first when speeds are close.** Taking them from the bank costs less time than buying new ones.

No gameplay automation. Saves, route IDs, adopted plans and progress are unchanged.
