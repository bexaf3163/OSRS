# OSRS Path 2.89.0

- Gear data now includes the free bows (shortbows and the oak, willow and maple longbows) and the ranged armour that fits the app's six gear slots: studded body and chaps, green d'hide body and chaps, the leather cowl. Each comes from the live Wiki with its bonuses and requirements (the green d'hide body needs Dragon Slayer I). The plain Longbow is left out because the Wiki states no requirements for it.
- A bow has no melee hit, so no melee estimate is made with one worn: the Money page times a bow user in their own style, and a bow on a melee step is named as not a melee weapon instead of "no stats". With the leather cowl's defence known, Ranged 20 now trains at the giant frogs (slightly faster than the barbarians).
- Drops: a raw catch is cooked and sold only when the cooked sale beats the raw one after the burns at your Cooking level (the Wiki's success chances per fish). With today's prices raw fish usually sells for more, so the advice stays "sell raw".
- The data check counts every NPC the guide looks up (quest-stage lines, stage targets, recipe makers, not only step lists). Six NPC locations that nothing looks up are removed (Fishing tutor, Bob, Diango, Thessalia, Mazchna, Grace); the check now has no warnings.
- The route gate runs in CI and a release waits for it. It also fails if more turns than today's 51 end with an unknown remaining time.

Not done: the Stronghold of Security's one-time 10,000 coins are still not on the Money page, because no source gives a time for it and the Money engine has no way to show a one-time method without guessing one. Quest times stay Wiki bands: replacing them needs measured times from many players, which a single account cannot produce.

Verified before delivery: 2,641 unit tests, the route gate (23 of 23 quests, no check, 51 turns with an unknown time) and 769 browser checks; typecheck/build, data/fixtures (no warnings), structure, unused-code, dead-CSS and Wiki-link (633 links) gates. The RuneLite compatibility check needs a locally installed RuneLite and did not run here; the plugin code is unchanged since 2.87.0 (its 640 tests passed then). Timing against 2.88.1, two paired runs in opposite orders: mean non-XP update script time +10% and −5% (about +2% together), cold-start script time between −16% and +11% per page; the one consistent rise is the Gear page's gear update, 1 to 2 ms, from the larger gear table. Native Electron checks and the isolated Portable EXE run in the release workflow. No real-game testing is claimed.
