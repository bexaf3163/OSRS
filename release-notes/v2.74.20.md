# OSRS Path 2.74.20

The skill guides now follow the OSRS Wiki's free-to-play training guides, with other options for every skill.

## Melee

- **Giant frogs from 20 to 40.** Level 13 frogs in Lumbridge Swamp: about 90 XP a kill and Big bones on every kill. Before, the guide kept you on chickens, cows and Al Kharid warriors.
- Then Hill Giants (40–60), Moss giants (60–80) and Lesser demons (80+).
- **Other options** under each step: monks (free healing), giant rats, minotaurs, Brutus, barbarians, Al Kharid warriors, hobgoblins, Flesh Crawlers, ice giants, body golems, giant spiders and ogresses.

## Every other free skill

- **Ranged:** seagulls that never hit back (1–20), Giant frogs (20–57), then moss giants from a safespot.
- **Magic:** enchanting sapphire, emerald and ruby amulets. Lvl-1 Enchant had been marked members-only, but it is free. Splashing, teleports, Superheat, Telekinetic Grab and Fire Blast are listed as other options.
- **Prayer:** Big bones from giant frogs, plus fiendish ashes, vile ashes and Brutus's bull bones.
- **Cooking:** jugs of wine from 35 at any bank, with no range needed. The old plan stopped at 50.
- **Runecraft and Crafting** now go to 99 for free accounts, with body runes and cutting diamonds.
- **Smithing** adds adamant platebodies from 88.
- **Mining, Woodcutting and Fishing** each gain an "Other options" list.
- **Corrections:** Crafting level 9 makes a Leather cowl, not a coif. Pies, pizza and wine are free, not members-only.

In the roadmap, "Other options" folds open under the step it belongs to. The other options never change your path.

No gameplay automation or route reordering. Saves, route IDs and progress are unchanged.
