# OSRS Path 2.110.0

- New Members quest: Creature of Fenkenstrain (2 QP, 1,000 Thieving XP; needs Priest in Peril, The Restless Ghost, Crafting 20 and Thieving 25). It joins the Morytania stage of a Members account's route after Priest in Peril and never shows in free-to-play mode. Every stage is on the card, from the pickled brain and the graves to the lightning conductor and the monster. The level 51 experiment that carries the key is estimated with your kit, the stronger monsters are run past, and Lobster x5 (or similar food) is a required item.
- A quest that asks for part of another quest now waits for that quest, so the Ghostspeak amulet from The Restless Ghost counts as kept for Fenkenstrain.

Verified before delivery: 2,640 unit tests, the F2P route gate (23 of 23 quests, 0 of 211 turns with an unknown time), the Members route gate (49 of 49 quests, no check, 6 of 368 turns with an unknown time), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
