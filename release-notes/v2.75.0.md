# OSRS Path 2.75.0

- **Fight plans: where to train, in one answer.** The combat step now compares every training spot the route can reach and says why it picked one, for example "The route as written banks about 83,000 gp of Big bones: training on Brutus is about 85 min faster but brings about 83,000 gp less." The plan line is shown on the step even when the route's own plan wins.
- **New spots:** the barbarians of Barbarian Village, the Al Kharid warriors (with a warning that the others join in), the Edgeville monks (they heal you, so no food) and Brutus after The Ides of Milk (double combat XP, his 19-damage charge kept in mind). Each place is a spawn from the OSRS Wiki next to a route step.
- **The walk to the bank counts**, from each spot to the bank the player really uses (Lumbridge Castle for the frogs, Edgeville for the Minotaurs and giants), instead of a flat 3 to 6 minutes.
- **Loot is counted when it pays:** Big bones, cowhides and bones are picked up and banked only where they bring more than the time they cost, at Grand Exchange prices after tax. A new account that cannot sell cowhides yet does not count them.
- **"Why this plan?" is smaller:** one sentence, at most four rows (time, food, coins, Use), and one line for the rest and for what opens later ("Not yet: Brutus (after The Ides of Milk)").
- The training spots are data (scripts/trainers.defs.json, checked against the wiki and the route), so new spots, members ones too, need no code.

No gameplay automation or route reordering. Saves, route IDs and progress are unchanged; an older saved "Train on …" choice maps to the best spot.
