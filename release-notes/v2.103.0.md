# OSRS Path 2.103.0

- Hunter training for Members accounts, from the OSRS Wiki's Hunter training guide: the Natural History Quiz in the Varrock Museum (1,000 XP once, levels 1 to 9), Feldip weasels (9-15, about 13,000 XP/h), ruby harvests with copper longtails (15-25, about 20,000), sapphire glacialis (25-43, about 28,500), falconry (43-49, 60,000-70,000) and razor-backed kebbits (49 on, 100,000-130,000). The rates are the Wiki's, labelled as such. Methods that need a quest the route does not do yet (red crabs, embertailed jerboas, black chinchompas) are left out, and their notes say where the Wiki moves on.
- Five Hunter places are on the map: the Varrock Museum, the Feldip, Rellekka and Piscatoris Hunter areas and the Piscatoris falconry area.
- Places that the Wiki now draws as an area outline are placed in the middle of the area, not on its first corner (Lumbridge Swamp would otherwise have moved 58 tiles onto its edge).
- The Grand Exchange bank and Lumbridge Castle's top-floor bank are now part of the location builder, so rebuilding the map data can no longer drop them.

Verified before delivery: 2,630 unit tests (new: an area outline is placed in its middle), the F2P route gate (23 of 23 quests, 0 of 211 turns with an unknown time), the Members route gate (37 of 37 quests, no check, 87 of 302 turns with an unknown time), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
