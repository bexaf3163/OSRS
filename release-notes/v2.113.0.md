# OSRS Path 2.113.0

- Members accounts now earn with Members money methods. The Money section reads the OSRS Wiki's Members money-making guides as well as the free-to-play ones: 146 Members methods are in the catalogue, from tanning dragonhide, enchanting jewellery and steel cannonballs to bullseye lanterns and pyre logs. When a step is short of coins, the planner ranks every method your levels, quests and membership allow and picks the fastest dependable one. A Members account no longer falls back on cowhides when something better is open.
- Free-to-play mode shows only free-to-play methods. Members methods are left out of the Money list completely, not shown as locked.
- Guides the app cannot judge yet are held back with the reason: 128 need a quest that is not on the route yet (they come back as quests are added), 104 are Members fights that need a hand-made combat profile, and 11 use wiki formulas for their inputs or outputs that the build cannot read. A guide is never built with an input left out, so no method looks cheaper than it is.
- A guide's optional skills no longer lock it (steel cannonballs list Sailing 87 as optional), and combat-level requirements are checked against your combat level.

The Members methods are built from the wiki guides and marked low confidence until each one is reviewed by hand like the free-to-play catalogue; that review continues in the next releases.

Verified before delivery: 2,642 unit tests, the F2P route gate (23 of 23 quests, 0 of 211 turns with an unknown time, still earning with cowhides as its fixed baseline), the Members route gate (51 of 51 quests, no check, 6 of 379 turns with an unknown time, earning with the ranked methods: collecting ruby rings and grinding goat horns), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
