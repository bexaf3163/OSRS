# OSRS Path 2.55.0 — training methods drive Fishing, four more ways to travel, a stock-up trip that adds up

## Fishing 1-50 (S6-02) follows the method for your real level

- The step asks for the tools of the method the "What to train with" card recommends for your level and what you carry: a net (or a rod and bait) at the start, a fly fishing rod and 100 feathers from 20, a lobster pot from 40. A harpoon or a lobster pot no longer makes net fishing "ready".
- The arrow follows the method's place: Draynor Village, then Barbarian Village, then Musa Point. When the level moves you to the next method, the game gets the new list and place without a press.
- While your level is not known, the step keeps its own list (the net). The game is asked to count every fishing tool, so the choice cannot flip back and forth for lack of data.
- An arrow the preparation queue set for a task that changed (for example "buy a lobster pot" after the method changed) is moved or taken down, so the next task gets the arrow.

## Travel: River Dougne canoes, spirit trees, gnome gliders, the Cowbell amulet

Each was checked on the OSRS Wiki on 15 October 2026.

- **River Dougne canoes** (members, five stations since 1 April 2026: Castle Wars, Tree Gnome Village, Ardougne Zoo, Chaos Druid Tower, Combat Training Camp): the same canoe types and levels as the River Lum.
- **Spirit trees** (members, after Tree Gnome Village): the Tree Gnome Village, Gnome Stronghold (leaving it needs The Grand Tree), Battlefield of Khazard, Grand Exchange and Feldip Hills trees. The completed quests come from the game; when the game has not said, the way is "check", never "locked".
- **Gnome gliders** (members, after The Grand Tree): only the Grand Tree glider flies everywhere, so two outer stations are two flights. The stairs to the Grand Tree's top floor count. Ape Atoll needs Monkey Madness II. The Feldip glider stays "check" because One Small Favour can only be read as done or not.
- **Cowbell amulet** (free-to-play, from The Ides of Milk): the game reports one name for the empty and the charged amulet, so the item id tells them apart. Charged means ready. Empty with an Air rune in the bag means "use an Air rune on it first". It is not offered above level 20 Wilderness, or when you are not carrying it (it cannot be bought).
- **Left out on purpose:** planted spirit trees (the game does not say whether one is grown), Poison Waste (part of The Path of Glouphrie), Prifddinas and Laguna Aurorae (not on the main map).

## Stocking up for the road

- One trip gets the whole kit: what the bank holds and the bag does not is taken from the nearest bank, and the rest is bought where it is sold. A teleport is only counted when everything it lacks is on the trip.
- The saving shown is the road's saving minus the detour of the stops. A trip that does not pay is not offered.
- If your coins are known and too few, there is no trip. If the coins are short in the bag but in the bank, they are withdrawn on the way.
- Anything not confirmed is named instead of hidden: "check your bank for Law rune", "check you have the coins".
