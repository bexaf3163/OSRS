# OSRS Path 2.185.0

## What a processing loop made pays for its next batch

At Crafting 57 with 1,185 coins and 220 diamond necklaces in the bank, the Crafting plan stopped making diamond necklaces and asked for 4 sapphires instead. The training engine checked whether the next batch was affordable with coins alone, so a cheaper method that 1,185 coins could start won, although the necklaces sell for about 430,000 coins.

A processing method's own products that you hold, in the bag or the bank, now count as coins for its next batch at their sale price after the exchange tax. When the step is short of coins and those products cover it, the plan earns by selling them ("Sell 220 Diamond necklace"), a trip to the exchange, instead of sending you to another money method. A product the same skill uses next, such as bronze bars for smithing, stays a material and is not counted. Items a new account cannot trade are not counted either.

## The Money list ranks for an hour of income

With nothing short, "Best for you" ranked every method by the time to make 10,000 more coins. At that size the walk decides, so garlic, shrimp and air talismans nearby came first for a Combat 51, Crafting 57 account, ahead of methods paying several times more an hour. The list now ranks for an hour of your best steady method, rounded: 44,000 coins for that account, where spades, plain pizzas, iron bars, tanning and giant frogs lead and garlic and shrimp fall to the bottom. Methods that need full attention or carry Wilderness risk stay in their own group, as before.
