# OSRS Path 2.101.0

- New Members quest: Witch's House (4 QP). It joins the early Members stage of a Members account's route and never shows in free-to-play mode. Its stages, tiles and texts come from Quest Helper and the Wiki through the quest pipeline.
- The Witch's experiment is fought the way the quest does it: all four forms in a row, without a break (level 19 skavid, 30 spider, 42 bear and 53 wolf). The app estimates every form and adds up the food, so Trout x5 (or similar food) is a required item that preparation sizes for your levels and armour.
- Better time estimates in Members mode. When a quest's item use is not reviewed, or a fight or training uses up an unknown amount, the planner used to forget everything you own, including the armour and weapon you wear. Now a single worn piece survives as long as the quest does not ask for it, so later training keeps its time. Turns with an unknown remaining time dropped from 253 to 87 in the Members route gate.
- Every page is now checked in both game modes: free-to-play pages name no Members quest, Members pages show them.

Verified before delivery: 2,629 unit tests (new: the four forms are four fights in order, a worn piece survives unmodelled consumption unless the quest names it), the F2P route gate (23 of 23 quests, 0 of 211 turns with an unknown time), the Members route gate (36 of 36 quests, no check, 87 of 301 turns with an unknown time), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
