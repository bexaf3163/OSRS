# OSRS Path 2.162.0

Members Magic now enchants crossbow bolts. From level 4 the plan casts Enchant Crossbow Bolt on opal, sapphire and pearl bolts, 10 bolts a cast, until level 27. It then enchants amulets as before, ending with Amulet of strength from 55 to 99 at 81,000 to 111,000 XP an hour, which makes about 124,000 coins an hour. A Members Magic plan to 99 takes about 140 hours.

Bolt enchanting costs about 290,000 to 770,000 coins an hour at current Grand Exchange prices, so the plan switches to amulets at 27 because they make money.

The planner also knows the Members amulet enchants for Amulet of glory and Amulet of fury. It does not pick them at current prices, because a dragonstone amulet costs more than a glory sells for and an onyx cast loses about 60,000 coins.

Verified before delivery: the unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 132 Members-route quests), the browser checks, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
