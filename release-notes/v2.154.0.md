# OSRS Path 2.154.0

A Tail of Two Cats, Contact! and the Evil Dave part of Recipe for Disaster are on the route.

A Tail of Two Cats plans your cat or kitten, 5 death runes for Hild to enchant the catspeak amulet, and the items for Unferth's chores: a chocolate cake, a bucket of milk, logs, a tinderbox, shears, 4 potato seeds, a rake and a seed dibber. It also plans a vial of water and a desert shirt and robe for the doctor's visit. The route now knows that Icthlarin's Little Helper rewards the catspeak amulet and uses up its embalming items, which its reviewed item list had missed because of a mistyped Wiki revision. It also knows that the Sphinx in Sophanem replaces a lost one while a cat follows you.

Contact! plans a bullseye lantern with a tinderbox to relight it, and 15 lobsters for the level 191 Giant Scarab (130 hitpoints, max hit 20). The scarab summons mages, rangers and lancers in turn, and the fight estimate does not count their hits yet, so the combat audit says so.

Freeing Evil Dave plans your cat or kitten and a bowl of stew. Your cat kills the hell-rats in Evil Dave's basement for the spices.

Heroes' Quest and Fairytale I - Growing Pains stay off the route for now. Heroes' Quest needs a partner from the other Shield of Arrav gang and gets a batch of its own, and Fairytale I asks for 3 random ingredients the route cannot know in advance.

Verified before delivery: 2,688 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 124 Members-route quests), the browser checks on every page, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
