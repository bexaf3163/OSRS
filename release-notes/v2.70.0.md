# OSRS Path 2.70.0

**Skills: one plan per skill, honest labels, and a followed plan that drives the whole guide.**

- **Roadmap filters say one thing each.** The three mixed filters ("Fastest / Quest-Driven", "AFK / Budget", "F2P Only") are gone. There is a **priority** (Fastest, with quest XP skips first, or Low effort), and for a free account a **Show members steps (preview)** switch. The priority starts from Settings → Training choices (Relaxed opens on Low effort).
- **Level groups are labelled by what they cover.** A group no longer says "Levels 30–40" over a method that runs from 30 to 60.
- **Follow this plan** (was "Track Skill Path") leads the skill in the game instead of the quest route. Its tools and materials now appear first in Shopping (for example a needle and 50 thread for leather crafting). **Back to the quest route** stops it; route progress is untouched.
- **A met goal is said plainly** on the skill page ("Stage 4 goal reached: Woodcutting 15"), and the next goal follows.
- **Tool wording:** an axe works from the bag at your Woodcutting level; wielding it needs an Attack level (the old text said "the best axe you can wield").
- **Coverage ledger** of every skill: account access, guide and catalogue coverage, route requirements and gaps (`docs/SKILLS_COVERAGE.md`, checked by a test).

Known gaps (in the ledger): the method catalogue has no free melee methods above 20 (combat training is the route's combat steps), no smithing method before 18 (The Knight's Sword covers it) and none for Woodcutting after 60. Cost per hour is not modelled per method.
