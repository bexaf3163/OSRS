# OSRS Path 2.74.6

## Dragon Slayer I: the list follows the game from the Oracle to Captain Ned

A live playthrough got stuck four times. These are fixed:

- **The Oracle (S5-04).** After talking to the Oracle, the list stayed on "talk to the Oracle". It now reads the game's own record of the Oracle's answer, as Quest Helper does, and moves on to the Dwarven Mine ladder. At the magic door it follows the silk, lobster pot, bowl and mind bomb as each one is used.
- **Wormbrain (S5-05).** The step asked you to bring three map parts, but the third one is Wormbrain's, so the arrow pointed at "get a map part" instead of Port Sarim Jail. It now asks only for the 10,000 coins.
- **The ship (S5-06).** After three planks the list still asked you to buy a plank, and the step never finished. Each repair now moves the list on. The step completes when the game marks the hull as fully fixed, even if you repaired it before the step was shown.
- **Captain Ned (S5-07).** After the map was joined into the Crandor map, the list asked for three map parts, and the step never finished. The joined map now counts. The step completes when Ned agrees to sail: the game moves the quest on to the voyage.

The in-game list no longer asks again for something an earlier line of the same stage used up (silk on the door, coins for the ship, the map parts). An item a later line still checks before use (planks, nails, the hammer) is counted by that line, not by the stage's starting total.

## Lost City: the Entrana dungeon is a magic fight, and you can get out

No weapons or armour are allowed on Entrana, so the zombies and the Tree spirit (level 101) are fought with magic. The guide did not ask for any runes, and it did not ask for the instant teleport the wiki says you need to leave the dungeon. Lost City now requires Fire Strike runes for about 65 casts (195 Fire, 130 Air, 65 Mind) and a Lumbridge teleport tablet. Both are checked before the boat and again at the ladder down. The lines now give the wiki's safespot: chop the tree from the south, then stand between the mushrooms to the south-west. They also say to run past the greater demons and to stay away from the magic door to the east, which leads to the Wilderness.

## Monkey Madness I: the Jungle demon is a ranged fight with a bow in the kit

The guide said to shoot the Jungle demon with Ranged but did not list a bow or arrows. A bow (a Maple shortbow, or the best one you can wield) and 100 arrows are now required. They are in the final loadout and are checked before the fight. The fight line follows the wiki strategy: stand still when the fight starts, turn on Protect from Magic, stay three or more squares away, let the gnomes wear it down and land the last hit yourself.

No new panels, gameplay automation or route reordering. Saves, route IDs and progress are unchanged. In-game acceptance is pending.
