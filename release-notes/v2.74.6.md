# OSRS Path 2.74.6

## Lost City: the Entrana dungeon is a magic fight, and you can get out

No weapons or armour are allowed on Entrana, so the zombies and the Tree spirit (level 101) are fought with magic. The guide did not ask for any runes, and it did not ask for the instant teleport the wiki says you need to leave the dungeon. Lost City now requires Fire Strike runes for about 65 casts (195 Fire, 130 Air, 65 Mind) and a Lumbridge teleport tablet. Both are checked before the boat and again at the ladder down. The lines now give the wiki's safespot: chop the tree from the south, then stand between the mushrooms to the south-west. They also say to run past the greater demons and to stay away from the magic door to the east, which leads to the Wilderness.

## Monkey Madness I: the Jungle demon is a ranged fight with a bow in the kit

The guide said to shoot the Jungle demon with Ranged but did not list a bow or arrows. A bow (a Maple shortbow, or the best one you can wield) and 100 arrows are now required. They are in the final loadout and are checked before the fight. The fight line follows the wiki strategy: stand still when the fight starts, turn on Protect from Magic, stay three or more squares away, let the gnomes wear it down and land the last hit yourself.

No new panels, gameplay automation or route reordering. Saves, route IDs and progress are unchanged. In-game acceptance is pending.
