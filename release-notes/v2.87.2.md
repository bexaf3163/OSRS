# OSRS Path 2.87.2

- Remaining time is known far more often. Quests you reach later (Below Ice Mountain, Black Knights' Fortress, Dragon Slayer) could not price their preparation while you were still short of their quest points or earlier quests, although the plan does those first. Quest points and prerequisite quests now only decide the order, not whether a later quest's kit can be priced. In the route simulation the remaining time is unknown on 76 of 202 turns, down from 167.
- When you need several of an item that also spawns for free (3 Beers for Prince Ali Rescue), the plan buys them: a spawn gives one per visit and its respawn time is not measured, while a purchase's time is. A single nearby free item still comes first.
- An item that can be bought or made (the Orange dye for Goblin Diplomacy) is priced once, as the purchase, not again as its ingredients.
- Dragon Slayer's later fights are priced by the fight engine once their kit is in order, instead of staying unknown.

Still open: a quest whose fight kit is still being bought keeps an unknown time until it is complete (Vampyre Slayer's garlic, stake and hammer; Dragon Slayer before its fights).

Verified before delivery: 2,636 unit tests, the route gate (23 of 23 quests, no check, remaining time unknown on 76 of 202 turns) and 769 browser checks; typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. The plugin code is unchanged since 2.87.0 (its 640 tests passed then). Paired timing against 2.87.1: mean non-XP update script time −1% (13.5 → 13.4 ms), cold-start script time lower on every measured page. Native Electron checks and the isolated Portable EXE run in the release workflow. No real-game testing is claimed.
