# OSRS Path 2.164.0

Iron mining rates now match the OSRS Wiki, because the planner knows the iron triangle. At Al Kharid Mine you stand on one tile between 3 iron rocks, so you never step between rocks and you drop each ore while mining the next. At level 41 with a rune pickaxe that gives about 49,000 XP an hour, and from 61 about 52,500, the Wiki's "three ores every 12 ticks". The old estimate was about 39,000 because it added a step and a drop to every ore. The planner still avoids Al Kharid when the level-14 scorpions would attack you.

Members mine iron in the Mining Guild from level 60. Its members' area has a triangle and rocks that respawn twice as fast, so a dragon pickaxe gives about 67,000 to 74,000 XP an hour, inside the Wiki's 70,000 to 80,000. A Members Mining plan to 99 now takes about 178 to 196 hours instead of 271 to 468.

Motherlode Mine, gem rocks and granite still have no rate, because the Wiki gives Motherlode only at level 90 with full upgrades, gem rocks need the medium Karamja Diary and granite needs tick manipulation.

Verified before delivery: the unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 132 Members-route quests), the browser checks, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
