# OSRS Path 2.55.3 — Melzar's Maze: food, a Water Strike kit, and the armour tip

- **Food is part of the kit** for S5-03: 10 Salmon (or Trout, Tuna, Lobster, Swordfish) for the rooms and Melzar the Mad (hits up to 5). The maze door
  now checks for at least 5 pieces of food, like the other dangerous entries.
- **The lesser demon** (79 HP, hits up to 8, aggressive) is fought from the spot one square east of the magenta door, where it cannot reach you. It is
  weak to water and has Magic 1: Water Strike is the cheap way, about 45 casts (Mind, Air and Water runes; a Staff of air gives the Air runes).
- **Take the iron armour off in the safespot:** helmet, platebody, plateskirt and kiteshield cut magic accuracy to about a fifth (wiki magic attack
  bonuses, -65 together); without them about 9 casts in 10 hit (an estimate). The in-game line says so too.
