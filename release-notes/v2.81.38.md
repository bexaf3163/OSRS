# OSRS Path 2.81.38

- One rule picks the worn kit for every combat style in Skills and the route: per slot the best owned piece is worn, and a better one is bought while the coins allow. Ranged ranks pieces by Ranged attack, then defence; melee by strength, then attack, then defence. Weapons are bought through the one upgrade evaluator for all styles.
- Melee training no longer goes through the Gear page's advisor, so the two cannot drift apart in the training plan. The Gear page keeps its advisor for "what makes you stronger now".
