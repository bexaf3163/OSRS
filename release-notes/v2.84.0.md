# OSRS Path 2.84.0

- The old route's 13 skill steps are gone (Woodcutting, Mining, Magic, Strength and Attack, Fishing and Cooking, Ranged, Agility, Slayer, Prayer). The planner's training objectives and the training engine replace them. Saves keep their marks as history.
- The combat route planner that only those steps used is gone: the cows-then-Brutus plans, their comparison, the style and magic gear panels and the in-game plan line. The training card on Path and the Skills page give the method, time and food from the same engine.
- Training spots stand on their own: the cows, the Minotaurs and the giant frogs no longer borrow their place and directions from a route step.
- Quest steps no longer wait for old skill or shopping steps (Lost City, Animal Magnetism, Monkey Madness I, The Grand Tree).
- The old route's preparation, shopping and money steps are gone too: the RuneLite setup, the bank tidy, the Chronicle purchase, the cowhide money step, the big Grand Exchange trips, the Adamant scimitar steps, the dragon food and materials trips, the Anti-dragon shield trip and the Bond choice. Each quest step lists the items it needs and where to get them, and the Money page plans the coins.
- The Stronghold of Security stays on the route, because it is still the only place that offers its one-time 10,000 coins and boots.
- Dragon Slayer I says where the Anti-dragon shield comes from: Duke Horacio gives it for free.
- A step that lacks coins links to the Money page instead of an old money step, and the step page no longer shows the old money goal and funding plan panels.
- The route's stage list is gone, because nothing on screen showed it any more; steps keep their numbers, and the search box still finds a step by its code.
- Members skill pages state quest facts ("The Grand Tree needs 25") instead of old route lines with step codes.
- Money: "Kill Giant frogs" and "Kill cows" no longer read "combat stats unknown" while you wield a bow. A melee method that the melee estimate cannot time is timed by the combat engine in your own style, the same model the Skills page uses. When no model fits, the label says so instead of blaming your stats.
- Training method notes no longer name a fixed food amount ("Ten food is a starter trip"); the plan's food comes from the fight model.
- Gear: the current activity kit shows its items as icon tiles with their state, like the worn list, instead of a bullet list.
- The plugin reports how many of each worn item you have, so worn arrows count as ammunition ("Mithril arrow ×100: worn") instead of asking to refresh the equipment.
- Journal: the quest details show the state and the quest points as one row of chips under the title, and the count on the selected filter tab is readable.
- Training cards say what to do with the drop they count: bury bones while a Prayer goal (a quest's need or your followed goal) is above your level, otherwise sell them for the shown price after tax; bones that sell for nothing are buried, other worthless drops left.
- Faster: with the old route planners gone, an update from the game takes about 63% less time on average, and Path starts with about 47% less script work.
- Live in-game acceptance of this release is still pending.
