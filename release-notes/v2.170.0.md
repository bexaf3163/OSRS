# OSRS Path 2.170.0

## Plain pizzas for Cooking

From 35 Cooking, plans now make plain pizzas: buy pizza bases, tomatoes and cheese, add the tomato and then the cheese, and cook the pizza on a range. Each pizza gives 143 XP, against 30 for meat, and at today's prices the three parts cost about 242 coins while the pizza sells for about 465. Pizzas go only on a range, never on a fire, and they burn by the Wiki's range chart. From 55 the plan switches to anchovy pizzas as before.

| Cooking 35 to 55 | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Method | meat at the Al Kharid range | plain pizzas at the Al Kharid range |
| XP an hour at 35 | 21,000 to 34,000 | 27,000 to 47,000 |
| Coins an hour at 45 | about 17,000 | about 39,000 |

## Drops counted at their alch value

From 55 Magic, a drop worth more alched than sold now counts at its High Alchemy value, less a nature rune. This applies to targets valued by a Wiki money guide's drop table, today the Ogress Warriors' rune armour and weapons. The alch values come from the same Wiki exchange data as the prices.

## Every free-to-play skill checked

Every free-to-play plan from 1 to 99 was checked against the Wiki's free-to-play training guides at today's prices. The check used an account with the free quests done, 40 in the other combat skills and 5,000,000 coins. The checkpoints in "Next in F2P" stop earlier, for example Cooking at 70 and Prayer at 43.

The same plans with 50,000 coins are identical, because each one starts with a batch that amount can buy. Two Wiki options are left out on purpose. Free maple trees are slower than oaks or willows, are far from a bank and earn nothing, so a balanced plan never picks them. Runecraft teleports and tiaras either need members items or lose about 34 coins per XP.
