# OSRS Path 2.148.0

Four Recipe for Disaster parts are on the route: Freeing the Goblin generals, Freeing the Mountain Dwarf, Freeing Pirate Pete and Freeing Skrach Uglogwee.

The Goblin generals part plans the slop (bread, a bucket of water, an orange, a knife, blue dye, spice, fishing bait and charcoal). The Mountain Dwarf part plans 6 asgarnian ales and 306 coins for Kaylee's asgoldian ale, the rock cake ingredients for Rohak and leather gloves to pick up the hot cake. Without ice gloves, you kill a level-13 icefiend on Ice Mountain to cool the cake, so the planner adds 2 trout as food. The Wiki names the icefiend's attack "Magical", which the fight estimate did not recognise as magic; it does now. The Pirate Pete part plans 3 raw cod, 3 bread, a pestle and mortar, a knife, an empty fishbowl, a needle and 3 bronze wire for the diving helmet, and 5 trout for the underwater mudskippers (level 30 and 31) and crabs (level 21 and 23).

The Skrach Uglogwee part plans a raw chompy, an iron spit, logs and a tinderbox for the cooked chompy, an axe for Rantz's boat, a pickaxe for the rock, 3 balls of wool and 10 ogre arrows. It uses the ogre bow and bellows you keep from Big Chompy Bird Hunting, which the planner now counts as yours after that quest. The Jubbly does not fight back.

The route accepts a Wiki subquest name such as "Recipe for Disaster/Another Cook's Quest" as the route's "Recipe for Disaster — Another Cook's Quest", so later parts can name it as a prerequisite. The plugin marks these parts complete under RuneLite's names, such as "Recipe for Disaster - Wartface & Bentnoze". Rohak's and the Lumbridge Cook's rows now point the arrow at them.

On every skill's Train tab, "Choose your own goal" sets a goal past the level quests need, and the plan then runs to that goal. Switching the header between Free and Members now switches a followed skill plan too, so Members methods appear (or drop out) at once. A plan followed in Free used to stay free-only after the switch.

Verified before delivery: 2,671 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 107 Members-route quests finish in 894 turns), the browser checks on every page, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
