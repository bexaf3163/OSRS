# OSRS Path 2.55.2 — while you fight, the game shows the kills left and the progress; Hill Giants with food that adds up

## The training view in the game

Your report from the Stronghold: once the kit is bought and the fighting starts, the guide card is in the way, and the count of monsters left and the
progress matter.

- On a training step with a pace (the Minotaurs, the Hill Giants, fishing, woodcutting...), the guide card hides itself while XP is coming in. The top
  plate shows the step, the kills left with the style ("9 minotaurs to 20 Strength on Aggressive") and a progress bar to the goal.
- Health and danger warnings stay on the plate.
- The card comes back by itself two minutes after the last XP, when you open the bank to restock, or when a shop or another target leads the arrow.
  Nothing changes on quest steps.

## S4-03: Attack to 40 on the Hill Giants

- **Food is part of the kit:** 25 Salmon a trip (Trout, Tuna, Lobster or Swordfish count too). At Defence 1 a Hill Giant hits about 4 times in 5 for up to
  4, and a fight takes about a minute: plan about 2 Salmon a giant with a Strength potion, nearly 3 without (an estimate from the wiki stats). The Big
  bones pay for it: one bone is worth about 10 Salmon.
- **Strength potions** (4) and the Amulet of strength are recommended: a dose every 4-5 minutes shortens every fight.
- **In the game:** the steps end with the fight itself (style, food, potions, when to bank), and the route goes down the trapdoor to the giants. Inside the
  dungeon the arrow leads to them instead of saying "get back up".
