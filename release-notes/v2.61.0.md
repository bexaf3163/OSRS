# OSRS Path 2.61.0

## Inventory cleanup in the game

The Shopping page's Inventory cleanup (2.59.0) now reaches RuneLite. The same advice, in a few words:

- **At the bank and at a merchant**: under the step's own rows, "Inventory cleanup (advice only):" with up to five lines such as
  "Sell: Ball of wool ×20 — keep 3, the rest has no use ahead · ~1.2k gp" or "Destroy: Research package — its quest is done, no other use".
  No new window: it is the card that is already there.
- **At the Grand Exchange**: only the lines to sell.
- **In the guide**: under the Advice tab, never among the steps.
- **Slot frames** (off by default, setting "Cleanup slot frames"): a quiet dashed frame on the bag slot, blue to sell, red to drop or destroy, grey to
  look at. A quest or step item always keeps its own frame.

The plugin shows the advice only to the character it was made for, drops a line as soon as that stack leaves the bag, and hides everything when the
app has been closed for ten minutes. "Always keep" and "Dismiss" in the app take a line out of the game at once.

Nothing is sold, dropped, destroyed or moved for you: the plugin only shows text and frames.

## Settings

- "Inventory cleanup" (on): the rows on the cards and the Advice tab.
- "Cleanup slot frames" (off): the frames on the bag slots.
