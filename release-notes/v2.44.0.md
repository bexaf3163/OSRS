**Stages 3 and 4 are streamlined: damage first, no Defence grind, one scimitar, bought food.**

- **S3-06 pays only for the Adamant scimitar.** The goal drops from 30,000 gp to 2,500 gp, and the step finishes the moment you hold any one of 2,500 coins, 25 iron ore or the scimitar itself (new "any of" completion). S3-07 buys just the scimitar: the adamant armour set, which needed Defence 30, is gone.
- **S3-08 is Attack 30 and Strength 20** on the Stronghold of Security minotaurs (floor 1), not cows and Al Kharid warriors. Defence is not trained: Dragon Slayer I gives 18,650 Defence XP, which is level 33 from level 1. A minotaur gives about 40 XP a kill (10 hit points), so budget about 330 kills for Attack 30 and 110 more for Strength 20.
- **S4-03 is Attack 40 and Strength 35.** The rune armour that needed Defence 40 is no longer recommended; the Anti-dragon shield stops Elvarg's fire at any Defence.
- **S4-04 buys 20 cooked Lobster at the Exchange** (about 105 gp each, about 2,100 gp for 20) instead of the Karamja cage-fishing loop. Fly fishing at Barbarian Village stays as an option. The voyage step now takes its lobsters from S4-04, so the shopping list counts them once.
- **The stage goals follow:** Defence is 1 through stage 4 and 33 after Dragon Slayer I, Strength is 20-25, then 35-40, then 40-44, and Fishing and Cooking stay at 30 until stage 6, so a build that skips the Defence grind is no longer shown as behind.
