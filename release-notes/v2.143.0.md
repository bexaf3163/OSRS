# OSRS Path 2.143.0

Three more Members quests are on the route: Death on the Isle, The Garden of Death and At First Light.

Death on the Isle needs nothing bought, and its fights against Adala, Naiatli and Clodius are fought without equipment and cannot be lost. The Garden of Death plans the secateurs for the vines on Molch island. At First Light plans a box trap for the jerboa, because the needle, hammer and the rest are found during the quest.
