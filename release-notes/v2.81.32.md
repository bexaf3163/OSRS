# OSRS Path 2.81.32

- The in-game helper now has one look, Ledger. The Glass and Classic styles and the "Plate style" setting are gone, along with the Sora font they used.
- Hover hints next to the plaque use the Ledger leather, gold frame and parchment text.
- The plaque's text is about 1 pixel larger.
- The navigation arrow is drawn in Ledger gold with a dark leather outline. It turns light gold on approach, green on arrival and ember for another floor. Its distance label uses the plaque's Cinzel face.
- On a detour such as buying arrows, the NEXT line on the Now page shows the real next action (for example "Seagulls at Port Sarim") instead of repeating the step's title.
