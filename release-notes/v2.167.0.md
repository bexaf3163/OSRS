# OSRS Path 2.167.0

## Free-to-play skills in four phases

Once every free-to-play quest is done, Path lists the skill checkpoints in a fixed order, one skill at a time. Before, the list put the best-value skills first and then sorted them by hours, so the order changed with your levels.

| Phase | Skills | Why this order |
|---|---|---|
| 1, earn coins first | Crafting to 70, Smithing to 50, Magic to 70 | Their plans earn or break even wherever a method allows it, so they build the coins for the later phases |
| 2, combat and gathering | Attack, Strength, Defence and Ranged to 40, Woodcutting to 36, Fishing to 58, Mining to 60 | Their plans also earn or break even, with gear bought from phase 1 |
| 3, train from phase 1 profit | Firemaking to 50, Prayer to 43, Cooking to 70 | Bought logs, bones and ashes, and pizza ingredients tie up coins, so do not rush them with starting coins |
| 4, leave for members | Runecraft | Free-to-play Runecraft is too slow to be worth it |

Hitpoints trains with combat and has no checkpoint of its own. The phase also shows on each skill's page and in the "Where F2P stops" summary.

## Melee plans buy the Gear page's weapon

Melee training plans now buy the weapon and armour the Gear page recommends. Before, a plan used only the gear you already had, so an account with no weapon was planned unarmed all the way to 99. Now, at the start and at every level where a new piece becomes wearable, the plan asks the gear advisor what it would wear or buy with your levels and coins, the same way Ranged plans its bows and arrows.

For a free-to-play account with 40 in the other combat skills and 5,000,000 coins:

| Skill to 99 | Before | Now | Kit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attack | 975 to 1,498 h, unarmed | 757 to 1,214 h | rune warhammer, adamant scimitar at 30, rune scimitar at 40 |
| Strength | 730 to 1,181 h, unarmed | 443 to 848 h | rune scimitar, hill giants from 70 |
| Defence | 1,039 to 1,582 h, unarmed | 655 to 1,195 h | rune scimitar, hill giants from 5 |

The rune warhammer needs 40 Strength and no Attack, so the plan uses it while Attack is low. Attack stays slow because training Attack only raises accuracy: the damage, and so the XP, depends on Strength. The Wiki's higher free-to-play targets (flesh crawlers and giant spiders in the Stronghold of Security) are not modelled yet.
