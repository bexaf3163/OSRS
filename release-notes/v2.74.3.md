# OSRS Path 2.74.3

## Current training and preparation

Fishing/Cooking and Mining/Woodcutting hand over to the unfinished skill with its current tool, supplies and destination. Ranged checks usable owned bows, compatible equipped arrow counts and banked food; a usable stack keeps training instead of requesting a top-up after each shot. Magic checks the selected spell's real ingredients, owned equipped staff substitution and Superheat unlocks; unverified alchemy/splash setups remain manual alternatives. Existing route IDs, goals and saves are preserved.

Owned funding ingredients no longer receive a wait for a GE purchase they do not need. Replenishment and sale liquidity remain constrained. These are bounded preparation improvements, not a complete whole-goal cost/DPS/market-window optimizer. In-game acceptance is assigned to Claude and remains separately documented; automated checks do not stand in for it.

Account Sync now marks only goals directly established by quest or level evidence. It no longer treats shopping or other convenience-route ancestors as game-confirmed. Levels alone do not prove remaining items or retained stock. Existing notes, skips, saves and the separate manual review option are preserved.

Prerequisite labels now agree with the readiness blocker: skipped or contradicted prerequisites are not green, while live quest completion or reached entry skills can satisfy the dependency without changing saved route marks.

No new panels, gameplay automation or route reordering. Release gates include the full test suites, browser UI and isolated portable EXE; these are not a live-account sync acceptance test.

## Native save-read protection

Unreadable progress/profile files no longer look like missing saves. Automatic saving or profile mirroring pauses through the existing recovery guards, preserving original sources and any readable browser fallback.

Secondary-copy failures remain visible in Settings until a successful retry. Unreadable individual profile files report an incomplete backup while healthy profiles continue copying; a secondary error does not reject a successful primary save.

## Tool preparation

Training-method tool needs now reach preparation and the in-game checklist. An absent bronze tool no longer invents a purchase while another usable tier is unreported; known carried or banked tools still count. Unrelated skill methods cannot replace a step's requirements or destination.

## Cleaner guide metadata

Removed unnecessary calendar dates from normal guide, Gear, Money, travel, item and progress displays. Internal save ordering, backups, price freshness and diagnostic timestamps remain intact.

Cached market quotes now lose their live label after a failed refresh. Known item IDs can still use actual quotes when the name mapping is unavailable.

## Preparation market estimates

Required-item and preparation views now share quantity-aware current/cached/unavailable GE estimates with the expanded in-game guide. A missing quote no longer revives a static item-card price. Historical dated market claims were removed from route instructions; fixed NPC charges and internal timestamps remain.
