# OSRS Path 2.68.0

**Gear answers one question: is any change worth making for the fight you are about to do?**

- **One recommendation.** The page names the fight it is judging for (the opponent, the style it attacks with and its max hit), shows what you wear now, and gives at most one thing to do. If nothing is worth it, it says so once, with the reason: the next real step up and what it costs, the next level to reach, or that the exchange prices have not loaded.
- **What you own comes first.** A better item in the bag or bank is "wear it", ahead of any purchase. Items you can wear but cannot afford are goals with the shortfall, never actions.
- **Coins for the next supplies are kept.** Gear is bought only from what is left after the food and supplies of your next trip (the same list Shopping shows), so a small upgrade never takes your food money.
- **Honest words.** "Faster" only when the attack interval changes. "Same max hit, more accurate" names the hit chance before and after, and the attack style when the best style changes with the item (an Amulet of strength can let Accurate reach your old max hit). The max hit is named only when it changes.
- **Defence against the style that hits you.** Armour is judged by the opponent's attack style (a Hill Giant crushes), with how often it hits you before and after. When the style is not known, the weakest melee style counts. Stab, slash and crush are never added together.
- **Two-handed weapons** say what the shield took off ("but the shield comes off (crush defence −9)").
- **Offline:** the last known gear of your character, with the time it was seen, or one short "RuneLite is not connected" card. Each character keeps its own snapshot. Unknown levels are not shown as level 1.
- **Details are folded:** Other options, Combat numbers and Future unlocks (with the 🔒 items you own but cannot wear yet).
