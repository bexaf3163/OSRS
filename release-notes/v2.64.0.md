# OSRS Path 2.64.0

**Raising coins for your next trip.** When the step you are on needs coins you do not have (food, a tool, a fee), the Preparation panel now says so in one line and offers one way to raise them, with one Start button. Everything else is folded under Details.

- **What it checks first.** A cheaper way to cover the same need (for example Trout instead of Salmon for the same health), then spare items in your bag that are safe to sell, then gathering the materials yourself, and only then earning. Items your route needs are never offered for sale, and your 50 Big bones for Hill Giants stay yours.
- **Earning methods.** Twelve free-to-play methods taken from the wiki money-making guides (each with its guide, revision and date; see `docs/FUNDING_COVERAGE.md`). A method is offered only when your account can do it now and you can afford its first batch. Income counted is dependable income only; rare drops are never a plan. Monster methods use the same combat model as the food plan, for your levels and gear.
- **Honest numbers.** Prices come from the Grand Exchange (buy and sell side separately, 2% tax with the 5,000,000 cap and the exempt list). Old or thin prices lower the confidence and the card says "not sure" instead of a firm figure. If the exchange is down, rough guide prices are marked as such. Unknown coins, an unread bank or a missing price is shown as unknown, never as zero.
- **A plan you can follow.** Start sets one arrow and a short status line in the step panel. The plan moves on only when the game shows it (you are at the place, the items are in the bag, the coins rose); what the game cannot show (a sale at the exchange) asks you to press "I did this". Pause, Cancel and Resume are one click. The plan is saved per character, survives a restart, and never completes, reorders or renames a route step.
- **Your own pace.** After enough work the app learns how fast you really go (units over time actually worked) and uses that instead of the published figure next time. Coins moved to the bank are not income; loot is an estimate until you sell it, and is shown apart from coins.
- **One line, not two.** The old "Coins for the steps ... how to make up" line is replaced by this card while you are on the step; it stays for steps further ahead.

Nothing is bought, sold, withdrawn or fought for you. The in-game panel shows nothing new in this release (the plugin line comes next).
