# OSRS Path 2.72.1

**Fixes from the live check of 2.72.0 (real app, real game on World 316).**

- **Gear advice only when it changes the fight.** A piece of armour is advised (on the Gear page, as the game's first HUD line and as a bag or bank highlight) only when the opponent would hit you at least 3 points less often. Before, an Iron chainbody in the bank was the top advice against cows because it moved their hit chance from 23% to 22%. The "needs a level" lines are unchanged.
- **Money: one figure for the coins to start.** Each row now shows the least you need to begin (the same figure as "Coins to start" in its details); a full first trip is under "Sources and calculations". A method that reaches the goal with smaller first batches no longer says the money runs out next to "Can do now"; that warning stays only where the run with your coins really stops. The money line reads "With full trips, up to about N gp of your own coins is tied up before the sales come in."
- **The plan in effect before the game connects.** Your Hitpoints from the game are now saved with the other levels, so a training step shows the plan in effect (the cows first, for example) as soon as the app starts, not the route as written. Before, Hitpoints was never saved, and the step showed the Hill Giants until the character logged in.
- **The game card no longer says "Next: Then: …"** for the second part of a training plan.
