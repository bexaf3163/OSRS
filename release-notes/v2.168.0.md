# OSRS Path 2.168.0

## Cooking at a range by the bank

Cooking plans no longer send you to the fire at Barbarian Village. That fire is a long walk from the Edgeville bank, so every inventory lost time on the walk. Plans now use the range 22 tiles north of the Al Kharid bank, the closest free range to a bank on the Wiki's list. If you carry a tinderbox, the plan still compares your own fire by the Grand Exchange bank and uses it when it is quicker for your food.

For a free-to-play account with 30 Cooking and 2,000,000 coins:

| Cooking 30 to 70 | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Spot for meat (30 to 55) | Barbarian Village fire | Al Kharid range |
| Hours | 11 to 21 h | 10 to 17 h |
| Coins per hour while cooking meat | about 13,500 to 14,900 | about 19,200 to 21,100 |

The plan still cooks meat to 55 and then makes anchovy pizzas, because at today's prices those are the methods that earn. Raw tuna costs about as much as cooked tuna sells for, and cooked trout, salmon, lobster and swordfish sell for less than raw, so buying fish to cook would lose coins. Your Fishing level does not change the Cooking plan: it never asks you to fish.

Verified before delivery: the unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 132 Members-route quests), the browser checks, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
