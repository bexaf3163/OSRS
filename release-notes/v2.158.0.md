# OSRS Path 2.158.0

Free-to-play skill training now picks methods by today's Grand Exchange prices, not only by speed, and it adds the profitable F2P methods it was missing.

Every method line on the Plan now ends with what an hour nets and what each XP costs or earns, for example "net ≈ −3k gp per hour, −0.07 gp per XP" for tuna at Cooking 40. The Plan's current stage is now the same method the Train card recommends; before, it could show bought pike while the card said tuna.

New F2P methods:
- Crafting: sapphire, emerald, ruby and diamond rings, necklaces and amulets at a furnace, after gold jewellery. They pay while they train.
- Magic: enchanting amulets (Lvl-1 to Lvl-4) and high alchemy of gem necklaces and amulets are priced methods now, no longer manual setups. A profit method waits behind ordinary ones while prices are unknown.
- Prayer: vile and fiendish ashes. Free players can scatter them, as fast as bones are buried, and vile ashes cost far less per XP than big bones.
- Smithing: iron bars with a Ring of forging (always succeeds; the ring lasts 140 bars).
- Cooking: anchovy pizza from 55, which never burns. Tuna no longer stops at 35, and cheap meat and small fish are priced, so they rank by money too.
- Fishing: harpoon tuna starts at 35.

The F2P checkpoints assume a player may stay free to play for a while: Crafting 70, Magic 70, Smithing 50, Cooking 70, Mining 60 (Mining Guild), Fishing 58, Defence 40 and Prayer 43 (Protect from Melee). The F2P Ranged table no longer lists the yew shortbow and rune arrows, which are members items, and a test now keeps every members item and method out of the F2P mode.

Strike-spell training now tells you to take off armour whose magic penalty leaves the spell no chance to hit, such as a full iron or steel set at low Magic. Before, the plan lost its Magic time estimate in that case.

Profit figures are estimates at the prices of the moment; Grand Exchange buy limits and offer times are not modelled yet.

Verified before delivery: the unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 132 Members-route quests), the browser checks on every page, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
