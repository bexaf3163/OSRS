# OSRS Path 2.66.0

**Every fight on the route was checked, and the quest fights you cannot skip are now prepared before you reach them.** The full record is in `docs/COMBAT_AUDIT.md`: 27 stages, each with its opponents, method, assumptions, problems and the decision taken.

What changes for you:

- **Food is checked before these fights:** Vampyre Slayer (Count Draynor), Black Knights' Fortress, Below Ice Mountain (the Ancient Guardian), The Corsair Curse (Ithoi), The Ides of Milk (Brutus), Priest in Peril (the Temple Guardian) and Nature Spirit (the ghasts). Before, their food was missing or only "recommended", so nothing checked it, and Ithoi and Brutus even showed "Ready" with nothing to check. Now the food is part of Preparation, the shopping list and the game's departure check.
- **Sized for your kit.** When your levels and armour need more food than the route writes, Preparation asks for more, up to one bag. It never asks for less than the route. A fight that needs more than a bag shows that as a caution: more food will not fix it.
- **"Ready" means checked.** A fight is reported as *not checked yet* when the game has not given your levels, gear or bag. Before, it could show as fine.
- **Members fights are estimated too:** the Temple Guardian and the ghasts, from their OSRS Wiki numbers.
- An opponent the app has no numbers for (the Goraks) shows a caution instead of a silent "Ready".

Still open (recorded as deferred in the audit): the Asgarnian Ice Dungeon has no in-game danger zone; Demon Slayer's food stays recommended because the 25 Bones for Traiborn and the food overfill the item list; the Ranged and safespot fights (ogresses, Lost City, Tanglefoot) are not sized to your kit; the Goraks have no preparation written yet.

Nothing is bought, eaten, withdrawn or fought for you.
