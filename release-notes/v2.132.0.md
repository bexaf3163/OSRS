# OSRS Path 2.132.0

Two quests and a miniquest join the Members route, so the Members quest-point total is now 130:

- **Alfred Grimhand's Barcrawl** (miniquest, no quest points). The guide covers the barbarian guard at the Barbarian Outpost and the ten bars from Yanille to Varrock, with 208 coins for the drinks. It unlocks Scorpion Catcher.
- **Scorpion Catcher** (Prayer 31). The guide covers Thormac in the Sorcerer's Tower, the seer, and the three scorpions at the Barbarian Outpost, Edgeville Monastery and Taverley Dungeon. It also covers the Jailer (level 47, fought with 10 lobsters as a margin), Velrak's dusty key and walking past the black demons and poison spiders.
- **Icthlarin's Little Helper** (after Gertrude's Cat). The guide covers the Sphinx and her riddle, the pyramid pit and traps, and the canopic jars. It also covers the embalming ingredients, the holy symbol and the Possessed Priest (level 91) with one Apparition guardian (up to level 81), fought with 10 lobsters. It uses the kitten from Gertrude's Cat.

Verified before delivery: 2,660 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 71 Members-route quests finish), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
