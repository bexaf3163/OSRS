# OSRS Path 2.53.0 — stock up for the road

Before a long run the guide now says which teleports clearly pay off on the coming steps and what to bring for them, the way RestedXP and Zygor tell you to stock up before you leave. It only suggests: nothing is bought, withdrawn or used for you.

- **Supplies for the road:** in a step's Travel panel and on the Shopping page. The next 8 steps are checked leg by leg; a Varrock Teleport or a Chronicle teleport counts only where it beats walking and the canoe by a clear margin. You see how many uses that is and the time they save, then what to get: Teleport cards (one card, one charge), the book itself, runes per cast. What your bag, your bank and the book already hold is subtracted; an unread bank says "open your bank", never "none". Each row says where it is sold and for how much, with a button that leads you there.
- **Stock up on the game card:** when you stand by a shop that sells what the road needs, or pass it on the way and the shortage is confirmed, the card says "Stock up for the road: buy Teleport card ×5 at Diango's Toy Store (~750 gp) · Chronicle ×6 save ~3 min". The shop or exchange window then lists those purchases as "For the road".
- **A staff counts as runes:** a wielded Staff of air means no Air runes are needed; a staff in your bag turns the line into "… · wield your Staff of air". Battlestaves, mystic and combination staves (Smoke, Steam, Lava and the rest) count on members worlds.
- **Chronicle charges:** read from the game's own messages (check charges, teleport, add cards, run out) or from what RuneLite's Item Charges plugin saved. With charges known the line no longer says "check charges". An empty book is not offered as a way; it becomes "buy a Teleport card at Diango's", or "use a Teleport card on the Chronicle" when you carry one. A new book is bought together with a card, because it starts empty.
- **More banks:** the Grand Exchange bankers and the Lumbridge Castle bank (top floor) are stops now, with the stairs counted.
- **Checked:** `tsc`, build, 963 Vitest tests (new: `tests/travelSupplies.test.ts`), `check-data` (0 errors, 0 warnings), the UI smoke test, the full Java suite (485, new: `ChronicleChargesTest`), and live on World 316: Chronicle charges read from the game, the road supplies in the app with the real bag and bank, and the game card. The live check found and fixed a wrong "stock up at the Grand Exchange" line far from the exchange. A "stock up" line by a shop and the shop window rows were tested in unit tests and a rendered image, not in a shop in the game. See `docs/travel-supplies-2.53.md`.

Restart RuneLite through OSRS Path after updating.
