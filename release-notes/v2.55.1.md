# OSRS Path 2.55.1 — S3-08 says what to do: Strength first, the level 12 Minotaurs, food and potions

Your report from the Stronghold: the game showed the step but not what to do, and nothing about food. This release fixes that, using your real stats and gear
(Attack 22, Strength 16, Defence 1, iron armour) and the wiki stats of both Minotaurs.

## S3-08 is now "Strength to 20 and Attack to 30"

- **Order:** the Aggressive style (Slash on a scimitar) until Strength 20, then Accurate (Chop) until Attack 30. With a Strength potion dose active, this
  is about 10 minutes faster than Attack first (an estimate from the combat formulas); without potions both orders take about the same.
- **Which Minotaurs:** the level 12 ones, in the middle room of floor 1. At Defence 1 the level 27 ones hit more often and harder: for the same XP they
  take about twice the food (an estimate). The level 27 Minotaur's defences are -10, not the -21 of the level 12 one.
- **Food is part of the kit:** 20 Salmon a trip (Trout, Tuna, Lobster or Swordfish count too, together). The step is no longer "ready" with an empty bag.
  At Defence 1 plan about 1 Salmon for every 3 kills; eat at 4 health. The whole step from your levels is about 80 Salmon (an estimate).
- **Strength potions** are recommended, 3 of them (12 doses, about an hour): a dose about every 5 minutes. They speed up the Attack part too.
- **Weapon:** the Mithril scimitar and the Amulet of strength. Attack 30 ends the step; the Adamant scimitar is for S4-03.

## In the game

- The pace line names the style: "35 minotaurs to 20 Strength on Aggressive". At Strength 20 it says "next Attack: switch to Accurate", and each skill
  counts to its own goal (Strength 20, Attack 30).
- The guide's last line is the fight itself: style, food and potions.
- Inside the Stronghold the distance no longer reads "~2180 tiles" to the surface entrance. The route goes to the entrance and then to the level 12 room.
  If you are already inside, the arrow leads straight to the room.
- Any separate area (the Stronghold floors, an instance) is no longer measured in tiles from the surface: the guide says to find the way in or out.
