# OSRS Path 2.59.0

## Fixed: the guide staying on a step you already finished

Reported on The Corsair Curse (2026-10-17): after talking to Colin the guide stayed on "Talk to Colin" (✓ Nearby) until you went down the stairs.
The cause: Quest Helper's own logic had moved on to "go down from Arsen's hut", a step that only moves you and has no line in our list, so the
guide kept the old line. Steps that only move the player (go up/down, climb, enter, leave, board…) now show the line of the step they lead to.
A test walks every quest, every stage and every such step in the data and fails if one maps to nothing; the only ones left are five Watchtower
travel legs whose stage has no suitable neighbouring line (there the place-and-items logic decides, as before).

Still open, not fixed: a few Quest Helper steps that are not moves have no line in our stage data (for example Prince Ali Rescue's wig, Aggie and Ned
steps, Goblin Diplomacy's searches, some Ernest the Chicken levers). The guide keeps the nearest line there. They are listed in the handoff.

## New: Inventory cleanup (app)

A card on the Shopping page tells what in your bag can be sold or looked at because nothing ahead needs it: Keep / Sell / Drop-Destroy / Review, each
with the quantity and a reason ("Keep 3 for Prince Ali Rescue; sell the remaining 20"). Advice only; nothing is sold or dropped for you. Charged,
noted and transformed items, Ironman and new-account sale limits are handled; "Always keep" and "Dismiss" are saved per character. See
docs/inventory-cleanup.md. The in-game card and highlights come later.

## Plugin: two additive fields

`/status` now carries `accountType` ("normal" or "ironman") and each bag row may carry `noted: true`. Older apps ignore them.

## Clearer setting text

"Via Shortest Path" now says what its lines are: a line in Shortest Path's "unreachable" colour (purple by default) means it found no full route (for
example to a spot upstairs) and shows the way to the nearest point. Quest Helper's own minimap lines are its settings "Display navigation paths" and
"Display arrows on the mini-map"; this plugin never changes them.
