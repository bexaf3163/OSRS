# OSRS Path 2.73.0

**Repair block 1 of the 2.72.2 review: Shopping and preparation quantities, and where things can really be bought.**

- **Potions are counted in doses, everywhere.** A Strength potion(3) and a (1) now cover a step that asks for one Strength potion(4): every size counts
  toward the doses the step needs, in the step's readiness, the trip preparation, Shopping, the coin plan and the game's card. Doses are not bag slots:
  when your doses sit in more potions than they need, the row says so and suggests using one potion on another to pour them together.
- **A dose shortfall is bought as a sensible package.** Three doses of four is "1 dose short", and Shopping prices every potion size to cover it
  (a Strength potion(1), or a full (4) that leaves spares, whichever is cheaper), instead of asking for another full potion.
- **No shop or free pickup you cannot reach.** Before a shop or a free item is recommended, its access is checked: the Champions' Guild (32 quest points),
  the Mining Guild (Mining 60), the Crafting Guild (Crafting 40), Oziach (Dragon Slayer I), members areas on a free account, and the Wilderness. What the
  app cannot confirm yet is shown only as "if …" beside a way you can use. The Al Kharid toll and the Karamja boat are named with the shop.
- **Next trip: what this step needs, and what is for later.** On S4-03 the 20 Lobsters and the dragon materials belong to the purchase steps S4-04 and S4-05:
  they are folded under "For the next steps", optional now. The budget says what the step needs, what the next steps need, the coins the steps pay out
  (2,000 gp for the ship in S4-05) and a sum that adds up to the shortfall.
- **Fixed: a Shopping line that kept appearing and disappearing** ("1 not confirmed: up to 154 gp…"). The game was asked to count only what was still to
  buy; an item found in your bank then left that list, stopped being counted, read "not confirmed" and was asked about again. Everything Shopping shows is
  now counted, whatever its state.
