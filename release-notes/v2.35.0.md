**"How to get there" now plans with fairy rings and charter ships, and can send you to fetch a teleport first when that is clearly worth it.**

- **Fairy rings (members):** the nearest ring to you and the ring nearest to the target, by their three-letter codes (for example Wizards' Tower DIS to the Rellekka slayer cave AJR). It checks for a dramen or lunar staff in the bag or worn. "Fairytale II started" and any ring-specific condition are shown as things to check, never as met. Underground rings are not used for a surface target.
- **Charter ships (members):** the cheapest pair of docks by walking distance, with the real fare from the wiki's fare table, and a check of your coins. Dock conditions (a quest, or having visited a place) are listed as things to check.
- **Fetch it first (opportunistic):** a teleport tablet (Varrock, Lumbridge, Falador) or the Chronicle that you do not carry is offered by way of the nearest bank (free, if the bank is known to hold it) or the Grand Exchange, only when the whole trip still saves at least 60 tiles, the detour is at most 120 tiles and the price is at most 3000 gp. An unknown price is shown as "price unknown", never as free. The app only suggests: it never buys anything.
- **"Lead here" button:** the new ways have a button that points the in-game arrow at the first stop (the ring, the dock, the bank or the exchange).
- **Free-to-play accounts:** members-only ways are hidden in F2P mode instead of being listed as locked.
- **Data from the wiki, not from memory:** `npm run build-transport-net` builds the rings (53), docks (21) and the full fare table from the OSRS Wiki. The Brimhaven to Civitas fare it reads (2,400) matches the wiki's own example.

Not covered yet: spell runes are not fetched this way, and Sailors' amulet, spirit trees, gliders and similar networks are not in the planner.

**Checked.** App tests (including 18 new ones), data checks, the production build, the browser interface checks and all plugin tests pass. Not run in the live game: the new options on the real position.
