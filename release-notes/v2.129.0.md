# OSRS Path 2.129.0

Four more quests join the Members route, so the Members quest-point total is now 118:

- **Big Chompy Bird Hunting** (2 quest points; Ranged 30, Cooking 30, Fletching 5). The guide covers the achey logs and the ogre arrows (shafts, 100 feathers, wolfbone tips from 4 bought wolf bones). It also covers the bellows and bloated toads, and shooting the chompy with the ogre bow. It then covers asking Bugs and Fycie which ingredients to add and cooking the chompy for Rantz. The level 64 wolves and level 53 ogres of Feldip Hills are walked past, with 5 trout as a margin.
- **The Hand in the Sand** (Thieving 17, Crafting 49). The guide covers Bert in Yanille, the beer for the Guard Captain, Sandy's rota in Brimhaven, the pink dye and rose-tinted lens with Betty in Port Sarim, the truth serum, and the earth runes and sand for Zavistic Rarve.
- **Enlightened Journey** (Firemaking 20, Farming 30, Crafting 36, 20 quest points). The guide covers Auguste on Entrana, the origami balloon, the sandbags, the dyed silk, the 12 willow branches (bought on the Grand Exchange) and the first balloon flight to Taverley.
- **The Queen of Thieves** (Thieving 20, after Client of Kourend). The guide covers Tomas Lawry, the stew for Robert O'Reilly, Devan Rutter and the Warrens, Councillor Hughes' chest in Kingstown and Lady Shauna Piscarilius.

The RuneLite plugin can now read the game's hint arrow on an NPC. This moves the guide on by itself in Pirate's Treasure (the gardener), Tree Gnome Village (the Khazard warlord) and Big Chompy Bird Hunting (the chompy).

Farming tree runs now have a time estimate (8,000 to 20,000 XP an hour of active play), so the Members route shows a remaining time again before the Farming 30 training.

Verified before delivery: 2,653 unit tests (including a new check that every item the route or a training method asks for has a test price), the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 61 Members-route quests finish), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game, including the hint-arrow check.
