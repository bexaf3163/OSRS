# OSRS Path 2.183.0

## The Gear page saves up only for pieces worth earning

After 2.182.0 the route earned only for training kit pieces up to 1 million coins. The Gear page still told a level 60 Members account with no coins to save up for a dragon full helm at 40.1 million and a dragon kiteshield at 58.5 million. Its headline "next real step up" could name the 40.1 million helm for an account that already had the cheaper upgrades.

The Gear page now follows the same rule as the route. It saves up for a piece only up to 1 million coins, and a dearer piece appears only once your coins cover it. With 3 million coins at level 60 you get the dragon chainbody, platelegs and med helm and a rune kiteshield. With 100 million coins the dragon full helm and kiteshield are still offered.

The fight planner builds its kits from the same advice. So the extra kit search added in 2.182.0, under 1 million and under 10 million coins, could no longer change anything and is gone again.

## A two-handed weapon takes the shield slot

With the 11.3 million dragon warhammer no longer a goal for a poor account, the dragon 2h sword became the best weapon for the Giant Scarab fight in Contact!. The Gear page advised it together with a kiteshield, and the fight planner rejected that kit, so the route lost its time forecast for Contact! on two turns. In the game a two-handed weapon leaves no room for a shield, so the Gear page now advises no shield beside a two-handed weapon it advises.

## Faster free-to-play decisions

The first build of this release failed the free-to-play speed budget in CI: a median of 157 ms per decision against 150 ms, after 107 ms for 2.181.0 and 143 ms for 2.182.0. A profile showed that a fifth of every free-to-play decision went into building the melee training kit again for each melee method of the same player. The kit depends on the method only through the coin budget, and only up to 1 million coins, so all melee methods of one player state now share one kit. The median free-to-play decision went from 120 ms to 96 ms on the test machine, with the same route.

Verified before delivery: 2,873 unit tests, the free-to-play route gate (route unchanged), all five parts of the Members route gate, the browser checks, typecheck and build, structure, unused-code, dead-CSS and data checks. Nothing was tested in the real game.
