# OSRS Path 2.131.0

Three more quests join the Members route, so the Members quest-point total is now 127:

- **The Lost Tribe** (Agility 13, Thieving 13, Mining 17, after Goblin Diplomacy and Rune Mysteries). The guide covers the Lumbridge Castle cellar, the brooch and the goblin symbol book, and the tunnels to Mistag with a lit candle. It also covers Sigmund's key and chest, the H.A.M. Hideout and the peace with the Dorgeshuun.
- **Mountain Daughter** (Agility 20). The guide covers the rope on the boulder, the Tall Tree, the thorny bushes on White Wolf Mountain (with gloves), the plank across the flat stone and the cairn. It also covers the Kendal (level 70, fought with 10 lobsters as a margin).
- **Getting Ahead** (Crafting 30, Construction 26). The guide covers Gordon and Mary, the flour trail to the Kebos Lowlands mine and the Headless Beast (level 82, fought with 10 lobsters). It also covers the clay, fur and bloody heads and mounting the head with 2 planks and 6 nails. Only the bear fur is bought; the farm supplies the rest of the kit.

Promoted quest drafts now keep their food values, "any of" alternatives, quest-supplied items and opponents.

Verified before delivery: 2,658 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 68 Members-route quests finish), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
