# OSRS Path 2.140.0

Three more Members quests are on the route: The Feud, The Eyes of Glouphrie and Shilo Village. 2.138.0 and 2.139.0 did not publish, so this release also carries their seven quests: Making History, Murder Mystery, Sleeping Giants, Death to the Dorgeshuun, Watchtower, Enakhra's Lament and Elemental Workshop II.

The Feud plans the 517 coins, the desert disguise, gloves that work for the jewels and food for the Tough Guy and the Bandit champion. The Eyes of Glouphrie plans the glue, logs and tools for Oaknock's machine. Shilo Village plans the light, rope, bronze wire and bones for the tomb, and sixty Fire Bolts to safespot Nazastarool's three forms behind the dolmen.

Earlier quests from 2.138.0 and 2.139.0: Watchtower plans the guards' payments and food for Gorad, Enakhra's Lament the sandstone, granite, brazier fuels and spell runes, Elemental Workshop II the coal for two earth elementals' ore, Death to the Dorgeshuun two H.A.M. sets and a closed lantern, and Sleeping Giants the foundry repairs, with the plugin knowing the cave into the Giants' Foundry.

Verified before delivery: 2,656 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 90 Members-route quests finish in 763 turns), the browser checks on every page, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
