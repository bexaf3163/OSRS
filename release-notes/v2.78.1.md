# OSRS Path 2.78.1

Hardening of the shared engines: what a recommendation costs is now ranked, not only shown, and the rules follow the facts of a step, not its code.

- **Costs count in the ranking.** The fight plans now subtract the food a plan eats (owned food is used up too) as well as adding the loot it banks. The Skills page compares coins net of what training uses up: arrows broken, runes, bait, and the food melee training eats. When a cost has no known price, coins are left out of that comparison instead of the cost being taken as free.
- **Supplies have one price everywhere.** Arrows, runes, bait, feathers and food are priced at the Grand Exchange, or at a shop your account can use when the exchange has no price (bronze arrows at Lowe's for 1 gp). Fly fishing now shows the feathers it uses.
- **Food bought for a plan stays reserved.** The reserve now keeps the whole quantity the exchange list buys for a fight plan, not just one trip, so Clean up no longer offers to sell food the plan still needs.
- **Money and Skills time gathering the same way.** The Money page's yew and lobster methods now use the same wiki success chances, your level and your tool as the Skills page, instead of a published pace scaled by a rule. Where the gathering data has no spot, the published pace stays and says so.
- **Your objective decides whether coins trade against time.** On Fastest, the fight plans and the gathering spots rank by time alone, and no loot is kept for its price. Costs are still shown.
- **Coins are bounded the same way everywhere.** Banked coins credit at most half of a plan's time, because the rate they are valued at (killing cows for hides) is a rough baseline.
- **The path forecasts with the levels it reaches.** The legs of the path ahead are re-rated every five levels, so a method that unlocks later is compared on its own model rate.
- **Safety follows the fight, not the step's code.** The Anti-dragon shield check comes from the opponent's dragonfire, so a renamed or new step with that fight gets it.
- **Under the hood:** cached rates are keyed by the exact price snapshot, and members training spots are explicitly members-only everywhere they are offered.

No gameplay automation. Saves, route IDs, adopted plans and progress are unchanged.
