# OSRS Path 2.84.1

- Drops get one decision rule on training cards and in each Money method's details:
  - Bones: bury them while a Prayer goal is above your level, otherwise sell them for the shown price after tax.
  - A raw catch: cook it while a Cooking goal is above your level and you can cook that fish, otherwise sell it.
  - Cowhides: tan them when the Money page's "Tanning cowhide" method pays more than selling them raw.
  - Feathers, runes or arrows that your followed training uses up: keep them for it.
  - A new account banks what it cannot sell yet, and a missing price is named as missing, instead of "they sell for nothing".
- Live in-game acceptance of this release is still pending.
