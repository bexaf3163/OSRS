# OSRS Path 2.188.1

## A purchase ends when the banknotes arrive

The HUD kept saying "Buy gold bar" after you had bought the bars, even with 250 noted bars in the bag or 182 in the bank. The plugin ended a purchase only when the item itself was in the bag, but the Grand Exchange hands bought items over as banknotes. So the purchase stayed open until you withdrew unnoted bars, which is why the HUD flashed back to "Train crafting to 70" at the bank. A purchase now ends as soon as the item or its banknotes are in the bag.

The app itself already counted the notes. With noted bars in the bag, it plans "unnote at a bank" and not a purchase.

Verified before delivery: all plugin tests, including a new one for a purchase that arrives as banknotes. The app is unchanged, so its unit tests and route gates were not rerun locally, and CI runs them all. Nothing was tested in the real game.
