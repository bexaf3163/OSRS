# OSRS Path 2.166.0

Free-to-play skill plans no longer lose coins when a free or earning method exists at that level. A method that loses coins now gives way to one that breaks even or earns, even when the losing one is faster. Losses stay only where the planner has no other method, and the rule weighs coins only when market prices are loaded.

- Cooking keeps cooking meat and fish until 55, then makes anchovy pizzas, which earn about 147,000 coins an hour. Before, it made jugs of wine from 35, which lost about 97,000 coins an hour. A Cooking plan to 99 now takes about 136 to 241 hours instead of 44 to 69.
- Magic casts Fire Strike on cows from 17 to 27, where the cowhides pay for the runes, instead of enchanting sapphire amulets at a loss of about 165,000 coins an hour. When the strongest spell's runes cost more than the drops pay, the plan steps down to the strongest spell that does not lose coins.
- Ranged starts on giant frogs from level 1, because their big bones pay for the broken arrows, instead of seagulls, whose bones are not worth banking from the Port Sarim docks. Bring food: frogs hit back.
- Fishing banks the catch when dropping it would throw away bait or feathers worth more than nothing. Where the exchange gives the catch no price, the planner does not call fly fishing a loss.

The planner still has no free method for Prayer (bought bones and ashes), Firemaking (bought logs), the first 4 Crafting levels (leather gloves) or the first 4 Magic levels (Wind Strike). Members plans do not change.

Verified before delivery: the unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 132 Members-route quests), the browser checks, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
