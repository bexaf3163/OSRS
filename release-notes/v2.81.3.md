# OSRS Path 2.81.3

- Processing recommendations now compare the materials/tools you own and the starting funds actually observed. A funded recipe is preferred over a faster recipe whose first batch cannot be bought, for XP, Balanced and Money priorities.
- Smaller starter batches fit available materials and funds. Banking time uses that batch size; replenishment and future sale proceeds are not assumed. Unread balances, stock and prices remain unknown.
- Preparation retains the exact ingredients of the selected recipe. Authored alternatives cannot silently substitute a recipe with different XP, timing or access mechanics. Compatible declared mixtures retain XP guidance without invented mixed-product income.
- Processing time estimates explicitly assume future supplies and exclude purchasing time.
- Cash-only updates refresh the selected recipe and its supplies through the existing rate cache.
- Claude’s Ledger design, fonts, controls, saved progress and RuneLite launch arguments are unchanged. No additional interface controls.

Live-game acceptance remains assigned to Claude. Full-goal resupply/acquisition economics and unsupported mechanics are not claimed complete.
