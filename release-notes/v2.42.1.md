**The F2P route from the first step to Dragon Slayer I is audited end to end, and the audit found four things that are now fixed.**

- **A full-route audit now runs with every check** (new `tests/fullF2pRouteAudit.test.ts` and `F2pRouteAuditTest`): all 49 free-to-play steps and 30 quest stage tables, 600+ points. It checks that every step has a place for the arrow, every tile is on the free-to-play map with a real floor, every change of floor, dungeon or island has a ladder, rope or boat named, every step has a completion signal, several-item lines count "at least N" in any mix, no step needs more than 28 bag slots, every item has a source, and that what the bank holds is withdrawn rather than bought.
- **Fixed: the battle with Elvarg (S5-08) had no place on the map**, so the app showed no target before the stage list took over. It now points to the Port Sarim docks where the ship waits.
- **Fixed: the bag-space warning ignored what a quest hands you on the way.** Three goblin mails, or the items of Witch's Potion, did not count, so the warning only came after the bag was full. A quest step now counts them too (training steps do not: ore and logs are dropped as they come).
- **Fixed: Demon Slayer's 25 bones fill the bag** and nothing said to bank the rest first. The bones line now says it.
- **Checked and left as it was:** Delrith's words are not in the data at all (the order is read from the game), Vampyre Slayer's Hammer is a critical item and the Stake and Garlic come during the step, the Oracle and ship items are on the plan at the shopping step and two steps ahead, and the planks are three separate hand-ins.
- **Known gaps, listed rather than hidden:** Dragon Slayer I's "ship repaired" and "Ned boarded" steps (S5-06, S5-07) have no automatic completion (the game signal is a varbit, and the project allows no varbit trigger until each is verified in the game), and one Corsair Cove telescope floor change in The Corsair Curse is not named in the Quest Helper text. The audit pins both lists so they cannot grow.
- **Checked:** the app and plugin tests, the data checks, the production build and the browser interface checks pass. Not confirmed in the live game: no tile or plane was walked.
