# OSRS Path 2.188.2

## Your own necklaces pay for the next diamonds

With 260 diamond necklaces in the bank and 7,346 coins, the Crafting plan told you to buy an amulet mould and 13 emeralds. In 2.185.0 the method learned to count the products it made as coins, but the choice of recipe inside the method still looked at coins alone. The 7,346 coins could not pay for 13 diamonds, so a cheaper recipe won. The recipe choice now counts those products too, at their sale price after tax. With 260 necklaces, diamond necklaces stay the pick, and the plan sells necklaces to pay for the next batch.

## Bank trips say "Take", and come back every batch

- The HUD headed a bank trip "Buy gold bar" while its arrow pointed at "Edgeville Bank: take Gold bar". The plugin treated every target that names an item as a purchase. A bank trip now reads "Take Gold bar from Edgeville Bank".
- A bank trip was offered once per step. In a crafting loop you bank every batch, so after the first trip the HUD stayed on "Train crafting to 70" and never said what to take. Bank trips now come back whenever the bag needs restocking. Other trips are still offered once.
