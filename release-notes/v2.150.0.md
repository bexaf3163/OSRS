# OSRS Path 2.150.0

Troll Romance, Underground Pass and Garden of Tranquillity are on the route.

Troll Romance plans the sled (maple logs, an iron bar and a rope for Dunstan, swamp tar and a bucket of wax mixed in a cake tin), climbing boots, and Arrg (level 113, 140 hitpoints). Arrg is fought with Fire Bolt from the safespot behind the mountain wall: the step plans a staff of fire and runes for 60 casts, since his Magic defence of 200 makes many miss (the estimate needs about 35), plus 6 lobsters for the hits taken on the way to the safespot.

Underground Pass plans two ropes, a shortbow and bronze arrows for the lit arrow at the guide rope (Ranged 25), a spade, a bucket, a tinderbox and 20 lobsters for the traps and eight fights: the three paladins (level 62), the three demons (level 91), Kalrag (level 89) and a disciple of Iban (level 13). It opens Regicide and the elf quests that follow.

Garden of Tranquillity plans the farming tools, seeds, ultracompost, plant cures, rune essence and a filled plant pot, and a fishing rod for the ring in the Edgeville well. It needs the Ring of charos from Creature of Fenkenstrain, which now has a reviewed item flow, so the planner knows you keep the ring.

Between a Rock... stays off the route: its Arzinian Being summons an avatar in a random style.

Verified before delivery: 2,678 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 113 Members-route quests finish in 981 turns), the browser checks on every page, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
