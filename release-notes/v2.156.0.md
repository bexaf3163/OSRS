# OSRS Path 2.156.0

Temple of Ikov and Zogre Flesh Eaters are on the route.

The fight estimate now models fights that only one quest arrow can win. The Fire Warrior of Lesarkus in Temple of Ikov is hurt only by ice arrows, which fire only from a yew bow or better, so the estimate shoots ice arrows from a yew shortbow at your Ranged level, whatever weapon you wear. Slash Bash in Zogre Flesh Eaters takes full damage only from brutal arrows, so the estimate shoots bronze brutals from the ogre bow you kept from Big Chompy Bird Hunting.

Temple of Ikov plans a yew shortbow, 20 limpwurt roots for Winelda and 10 lobsters. You find the boots of lightness and the ice arrows inside the temple. The route takes the Guardians of Armadyl's side: you take off Lucien's pendant, then kill Lucien (level 14) wearing the Armadyl pendant.

Zogre Flesh Eaters plans a knife, your ogre bow, 100 bronze brutals and 10 lobsters for Slash Bash (level 111, 100 hitpoints, max hit 13). The ogre bow and the yew shortbow now count as reusable, so a quest that shows the bow no longer plans it as used up.

Forgettable Tale... stays off the route for now: its Quest Helper steps have no text and it has a lock puzzle. Rum Deal and Desert Treasure I, which these quests unlock, need Slayer, which the route does not train yet.

Verified before delivery: 2,693 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 129 Members-route quests), the browser checks on every page, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
