**Dragon Slayer I is led step by step, and the dangerous moments warn you in the game.**

- **All nine Dragon Slayer I steps (S5-01 to S5-09) now have a stage list in the game**, built from the Quest Helper sources: the Guildmaster's questions (the Maze key leads the list), the Duke's shield, Melzar's Maze floor by floor with every door and key holder highlighted and the basement on its own tiles, the Oracle's four items on the magic door, Wormbrain (pay 10,000 gp or Telekinetic Grab), buying and repairing Lady Lumbridge plank by plank, merging the three map pieces into the Crandor map, Captain Ned, the Crandor dungeon with the secret door shortcut, and Elvarg. Elvarg's head in the bag finishes the kill.
- **A hard check before the ship:** the preparation plan blocks step S5-08 while the Anti-dragon shield (or any dragonfire shield) is not in the bag or worn. If it is in the bank the block says so. An unchecked bag is never treated as missing. The recommended loadout now includes a Strength potion.
- **Warnings in the game:** Elvarg's lair shows "Equip the Anti-dragon shield!" until the shield is worn; the Port Sarim docks warn until it is in the bag; Melzar's lesser demon has a safespot tip; Count Draynor's manor warns for a missing Stake, Hammer or Garlic. These zones count only while you are on that quest step and go quiet the moment the item is on.
- **Delrith's incantation:** during the fight the list shows the words in the order your account needs (read from the game, they differ per account).
