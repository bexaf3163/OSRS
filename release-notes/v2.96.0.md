# OSRS Path 2.96.0

- In Members mode the planner now plans the Members quests too. Before, its quest list held only the 23 free quests, so Members mode planned the same route as F2P and the 12 Members quests on the route were never chosen. F2P mode is unchanged: it still plans only free quests.
- The Members quest facts come from each quest's Wiki card, like the free ones. Prerequisite quests written as "Completion of ..." are read (Nature Spirit needs Priest in Peril and The Restless Ghost, Monkey Madness I needs The Grand Tree and Tree Gnome Village). Membership comes from the route step, because many Members cards do not state it. Recipe for Disaster reads its Another Cook's Quest card, not the whole quest's.
- Pelters' Veg Stall (rotten tomatoes, Varrock and four other towns) is open to Members accounts. It is still blocked on F2P.
- A second route gate plays a fresh Members account through the planner. Today it finishes 26 of 35 quests and stops at Animal Magnetism, because Slayer has no training model yet. The 9 unfinished quests are recorded as known debt, which may only shrink.

Verified before delivery: 2,623 unit tests (new: Members prerequisite parsing), the F2P route gate (23 of 23 quests, no check, 0 of 211 turns with an unknown time) and the Members route gate (26 of 35, within its debt), the browser and Electron desktop checks, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. The plugin code is unchanged apart from its version. Nothing was tested in the real game.
