# OSRS Path 2.65.0

**Another way to do a combat step.** On a training step (Hill Giants for Attack 40 and 50 Big bones, the cows, the Minotaurs) the preparation panel now compares a few plans for *your* levels, XP and worn gear and says in one sentence if one is clearly better:

> Defence 20 first, then the route: about 15 min faster and 240 fewer pieces of food (about 7,854 gp) than the route's plan.

- One button, **Use this plan**. The comparison (route as written, cows then giants, Defence 10 / 20 / 30 first) is folded under "Compare the plans": time range, food for the whole objective, what the drops would sell for, and the risks.
- It uses the same combat model as the food plan; nothing is a second opinion. Plans are ranked by the slow end of their time range, and a plan is offered only if it is at least 10% better. A plan you adopted stays while it is not clearly beaten.
- An adopted plan leads everything: the arrow and the cows/giants the game highlights, the food and shopping list (a few pieces for cows, hundreds for giants), and the pace line. **Back to the route's plan** undoes it. The plan never completes the step: the route's own trigger does.
- The Big bones the step asks for are never counted as something to sell. Cowhides count as income only when this account can sell them; unknown is not counted. Prices are the exchange's sell side after the 2% tax.
- A bank that has not been read is unknown, not empty: the plan says it may be buying more than needed.
- **Settings: keep Defence at 1.** With it on, Defence plans are not offered or recommended.
- Saved per profile and character (only the choice, never the numbers). Nothing is attacked, eaten, bought or sold for you.
