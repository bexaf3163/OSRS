# OSRS Path 2.172.0

## Arrows and spells follow plus or zero

Ranged plans pick one set of arrows for your character, and with Members time worth a lot, that set was amethyst arrows. On Brutus they cost a little more than the break-even share, so the Members plan left Brutus for Ogress Warriors at 50 and came back at 65. Now a method whose arrows would lose coins fires the strongest cheaper arrows that do not, and the shopping list names those arrows.

Magic uses the same rule, and it now applies on Members too. A spell whose runes lose coins after the drops is swapped for the strongest spell that does not.

Members Ranged from 1 to 99 now stays on Brutus from 15, with rune arrows from 50 to 65, in 237 to 367 hours instead of 240 to 393. Free-to-play plans did not change.

## Coins for Members activities

Some Members methods are minigames or courses that no price model covers, so their plans showed no coins. They now carry what the Wiki says they use and make in an hour, priced at the exchange like everything else.

| Method | Per hour, from the Wiki |
|---|---|
| Rooftop courses (Seers', Pollnivneach, Ardougne) | marks of grace, counted as 10 amylase crystals each: 124, 135 and 171 crystals |
| Blackjacking | about 43,000 coins (the Wiki's 2,500,000 coins from 45 to 91) |
| Bakery and fruit stalls | nothing, the loot is dropped |
| Falconry | the falcon's 500 coins, the loot is dropped |
| Razor-backed kebbits | 330 long kebbit spikes, 33 rings of pursuit used |
| Giants' Foundry | 195,840 to 329,280 coins from Kovac, less the bars melted |
| Mahogany Homes | 375 to 500 planks and 18 to 36 steel bars used |
| Lava runes | 4,040 essence, earth runes and lava runes, 155 talismans and 10 binding necklaces |

At today's prices Members Smithing now earns about 4,900,000 coins from 1 to 99 through the Foundry, and Thieving about 5,400,000. Guardians of the Rift, Wintertodt and Farming still show no coins. Their rewards depend on your other levels and unlocks, and the Wiki gives no single hourly value for them.
