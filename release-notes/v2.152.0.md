# OSRS Path 2.152.0

Recruitment Drive, Wanted! and Skippy and the Mogres are on the route.

Recruitment Drive has you bank every item, because the test rooms allow nothing. The route lists how to pass each of the 7 rooms, 5 of which come in a random order. Sir Leye (level 20, max hit 3) is always one of them. The fight estimate now models a fight that allows no food: it fights him unarmed and warns if the fight would need food to win.

Wanted! plans 10,000 coins for the Commorb, 20 rune essence for the Mage of Zamorak, a lit bullseye lantern for the Lumbridge Swamp Caves and 4 lobsters for the level 32 Black Knight and Solus Dellagar.

Skippy and the Mogres plans a bucket of water, nettle tea and the hangover cure's ingredients (chocolate dust, a bucket of milk and snape grass), and needs Cooking 20.

Cooking now cooks on your own fire just outside the Grand Exchange, a few steps from its bank, when you hold a tinderbox. It plans one log a trip and counts the time to light it, and no longer walks you to the Barbarian Village fire and the Edgeville bank. Without a tinderbox, the permanent Barbarian Village fire stays. Free-to-play training you do with bought materials by a bank now happens at the Grand Exchange bank too: leather and hard leather crafting, burying bones, wine and Superheat Item no longer send you to Al Kharid, Edgeville or Varrock West.

Training plans keep their time when you are short of coins. With almost no coins, Brutus and the moss giants have no Ranged rate, because their ammunition cannot be funded, so the plan now takes the best method it can price at that level instead of showing an unknown time.

In the game, the guide card always shows its 4 tabs, and an empty tab is faded and shows the Now page until it fills, so the strip no longer jumps while you train. The departure check, bulk list and shop window cards fold to their title with a click on it, and the choice is remembered. The shop card now moves with Alt+drag like the others. The guide card stays sharp under Windows display scaling.

Family Crest, Ratcatchers, Grim Tales and The Slug Menace stay off the route for now. Family Crest crosses the Wilderness past black demons, Ratcatchers needs a grown cat, Grim Tales needs a game check the plugin does not read yet, and The Slug Menace needs Slayer methods.

Verified before delivery: 2,686 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 118 Members-route quests), the browser checks on every page, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
