# OSRS Path 2.74.2

S4-03 now trains Attack to 40 without an obligatory 50-bone funding detour. Level 13 Giant frogs on the surface of Lumbridge Swamp are a real estimated and navigable alternative to cows. Do not enter the caves.

The planner balances time, supplies and repeated target selections. A short Defence-first plan can now qualify when it halves food with at most 5% extra training time, even if Dragon Slayer I awards Defence later. Keep Defence at 1 remains respected. At Defence 10 the guide explains banking and affordable steel armour before continuing; estimates use actual worn gear and exclude an unmeasured shopping detour.

Funding remains separate in Money. Later cash requirements are unchanged; bones are never treated as spendable coins before sale. Existing route IDs and saved progress are preserved; obsolete collection-plan keys cannot reinstate the quota. No new UI panels or automatic game actions.

Automated release acceptance includes the complete test suites, browser checks and isolated portable EXE. This is not a completed route playthrough or a live measurement of the new training plan.
