# OSRS Path 2.74.9

## Dragon Slayer I: the way back to Elvarg after a death

A live session died to Elvarg and respawned in Lumbridge. The list stayed on "kill Elvarg" and said nothing about how to get back: the ship is wrecked on Crandor. The step now follows Quest Helper:

- After a death, if you opened the shortcut wall in the Crandor dungeon, the list sends you to the Karamja volcano south of Brimhaven. It checks the Anti-dragon shield and 20 lobsters first.
- Inside the volcano it sends you north through the wall you opened, back into the Crandor dungeon. From there it goes to Elvarg's lair and the fight.
- The whole Crandor part follows the game's own quest state and where you are standing, so the list picks the right line by itself.

No new panels, gameplay automation or route reordering. Saves, route IDs and progress are unchanged. In-game acceptance is pending.
