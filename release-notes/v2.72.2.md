# OSRS Path 2.72.2

**Fixes from the second live check (2.72.1, World 316).**

- **The game's item list follows the food plan.** When a training plan took over at log-in, the game could keep the route's own food amount (25 Salmon, "in bank") while the app asked for 21 and you carried 23. The step is now sent to the game again once the personal food plan has settled; the figures that move with your XP still do not resend it every few kills.
- **No free items from the Wilderness.** Shopping put three planks from the Graveyard of Shadows "on the way". Other players can attack you there and take what you carry, so a free item in the Wilderness is no longer suggested; the row says where to buy it instead.
