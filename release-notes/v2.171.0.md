# OSRS Path 2.171.0

## Choose a goal for each skill

Every skill's training card now has three buttons: Fastest XP, Balanced and Most coins. Balanced is the default and works as before. Fastest XP takes the method that reaches the goal in the fewest hours, and Most coins takes the method that earns the most on the way. The choice is saved per skill and changes only which method the plan picks, so prices, rates and kit stay the same for every goal.

Every goal keeps the plus-or-zero rule. A method that loses coins is never picked while a free or earning method is open, even under Fastest XP.

## Coins on every plan, and a running balance

The training card now says how many coins the plan earns or costs on the way to the goal, as a range. The list in "Next in F2P" shows the same for each checkpoint and carries your coins down the list. When a checkpoint would spend more than you have by then, it says how many coins you are short and about how many hours of money-making cover them.

On a Members account, the Skills page lists the skill levels your open quests still need, with the skills that earn coins first, so the skills that spend coins are paid from that profit. Each row has the same coins and running balance.

## Plus or zero on Members too

Members plans now follow the same plus-or-zero rule as free-to-play plans. A loss smaller than 5% of what an hour of money-making earns counts as break-even, because a few thousand coins of arrows an hour should not send a plan to a much slower method. A plan that leaves a method because it began to lose coins is marked, so the checks accept that the next method is slower.

Because of this share, free-to-play Ranged starts on the Wiki's level 3 seagulls at Port Sarim again. They cannot hit back and cost only a few arrows an hour, and from 20 the plan moves to giant frogs.

## Fixed: Brutus was never ranked by speed

Brutus had a risk value the planner does not know, so its speed came out as "not a number" and it never won on speed. Its risk is now low, and a check makes sure every method uses only known effort, cost and risk values.

Verified before delivery: the unit tests, the free-to-play route gate (all 23 quests, decisions p50 110 ms and p95 600 ms), the browser checks, typecheck and build, structure, unused-code, dead-CSS and data gates. The Members route gate runs in CI. Nothing was tested in the real game.
