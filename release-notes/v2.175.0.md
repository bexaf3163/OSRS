# OSRS Path 2.175.0

This release also carries the changes planned for 2.174.0, which was never published because a Members route check stopped. That stop is fixed below.

## Earn while you train, on the Money page

You noticed that crafting seemed to earn more than the money guides. It does. The Money page lists the Wiki's money-making guides, and the Wiki writes few guides for processing skills, so methods like enchanting and jewellery were missing there even though the Skills page already priced them.

At 40 in every skill and today's prices, the gap looks like this:

| Method | Coins an hour |
|---|---|
| Make Amulets of defence (Magic) | about 167,000 |
| Make Ruby necklaces (Crafting) | about 128,000 |
| Make Silver bars (Smithing) | about 55,000 |
| Kill Hill Giants (best money guide for that character) | 14,000 to 19,000 |

The Money page now has an "Earn while you train" list. It shows the training methods that earn coins at your levels and today's prices, most coins an hour first, with the XP they give, and links each one to its skill page. It uses the same training engine as the Skills page, so it follows your levels, quests and mode, and leaves out anything still locked for you.

On Members the guides stay richer at mid levels (crystal keys, redwood pyre logs, unfinished potions), so there the list mostly adds options rather than beating them.

## The libation bowl for Prayer

From 30 Prayer, Members plans can use the libation bowl at the Teomat. You un-note bones at Virilis for 10 coins each, bless them, break them into blessed bone shards and offer the shards with blessed sunfire wine. Every number comes from the Wiki.

| Bone | Shards | XP per bone |
|---|---|---|
| Bones | 4 | 24 |
| Big bones | 12 | 72 |
| Babydragon bones | 24 | 144 |
| Dragon bones | 58 | 348 |
| Dagannoth bones | 100 | 600 |

Each shard gives 6 XP. One jug of wine serves 400 shards and takes 2 sunfire splinters, and the pace is 1,700 bones an hour. At today's prices the plan from 30 picks the libation bowl, because it costs about 560,000 coins an hour against 778,000 at a gilded altar, for nearly the same XP.

## The Gemstone Crab follows your own damage

The Gemstone Crab never dies, so its XP is the damage you deal, with the Wiki's 87.5% XP per hit. The combat engine now rates such monsters from your levels and gear instead of a fixed 50,000 XP an hour. For example, melee at 60 Strength with a rune scimitar gives about 25,000 to 28,000 XP an hour, and Attack hardly changes it because the crab has no defence.

A new Members Ranged method fights the crab with your bow, the Wiki's idle Ranged choice. At 20 Ranged with a willow shortbow and mithril arrows it gives about 12,000 XP an hour for about 2,000 coins of arrows. The Wiki rates darts about the same as a yew or magic shortbow with rune arrows.

A monster that never dies needs no fight preparation, so the route no longer asks to check one; that check had stopped the Members route. Monster XP modifiers with a decimal, such as the crab's -12.5%, are now read in full instead of rounded.
