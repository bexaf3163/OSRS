**Fix: Prince Ali Rescue stayed on "Lady Keli: use soft clay" after the key print was taken, and the arrow pointed at the place you were already standing. The same kind of dead end is closed in the later quests.**

- **Key print and key:** the Keli step is now done once the key print is in the bag, and the furnace step once the bronze key is, so the list and the arrow move on to the furnace and then to Leela.
- **"Done" is always offered when Quest Helper's logic cannot decide:** in a few spots the Quest Helper logic depends on a flag of its own that the plugin cannot read. There the list used to rely on place and items alone, and a step done on the spot (the print, a pickup) could hold you. Now the "Done - next" row (open the list to see it) is available on any such step, so you are never stuck.
- **Used-up items leave "Needed now":** when a stage has no item list of its own, the quest's whole list stood in for it, so wool, ashes, redberries, flour and water were still listed after the wig and the paste were made. An item that is not in the bag or the bank, that a finished line names and no line from the current one on does, now counts as already handled.
- **Same check on the later quests:** went through every quest whose Quest Helper logic can be undecided: Below Ice Mountain, Tree Gnome Village, The Grand Tree, Nature Spirit, Lost City, Fairytale I, Fairytale II and Monkey Madness I. The steps that get an item now finish by it: the mirror (Nature Spirit), the Dramen branch and staff (Lost City), the star flower, Gorak claws and claw powder (Fairytale II). Item names were checked against the wiki by item number. The other stages in that group are short or have no tile, so the always-available "Done" covers them.
