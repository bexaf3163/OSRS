# OSRS Path 2.179.0

This release starts the full audit and carries the fixes planned for 2.178.0, which was not published because the Members route check went over its speed budget.

A new sweep plans every skill from every start level to 99, on a fresh account and on a trained one, in free-to-play and Members. It checks that each plan reaches 99 through methods you can use, each with a map place, a time and no avoidable loss, and that the Train card and the plan start with the same method. The first run checked 10,584 plans and found the problems below, which are now fixed. A shorter version of the sweep now runs with every test run.

## The route no longer stops at Members skilling spots

Wintertodt, Giants' Foundry, Tithe Farm, barbarian fishing at Otto's Grotto, teak trees and the rune altars had no place on the map. A plan could pick them, but the route could not send you there and stopped with a check. All 22 methods now have their place, with coordinates from the Wiki. The data check now rejects any method a plan can pick that has no place.

## One coin trip for the whole kit

When you were short of coins for a set of gear, the route earned for one piece, sent you to buy it, then asked you to earn again for the next piece, sometimes for 54 coins at a time. It now earns once for every purchase still ahead in the same preparation. Before Enlightened Journey, for example, the route earns about 16,500 coins once and then buys all nine items. The free-to-play route check now finishes in 201 turns instead of 211, and the Members one in 1,058 instead of 1,099.

## Slayer plans wear the gear you would buy

Members Slayer took about 2,600 hours from 1 to 99 on an account without a weapon, even with 50 million coins. Melee plans already assume the weapon and armour the Gear page would buy with your coins, but the Slayer plan rated your Slayer XP by unarmed damage. Slayer XP is the melee damage you deal, so a Slayer plan now carries the same gear as a melee plan. At 60 in the combat skills, Slayer 1 to 99 now takes about 1,400 hours at about 9,400 XP an hour. Your own damage still caps the rate.

## Prayer: the Train card and the plan agree

On a Members account the Train card said Camdozaal, while the plan below it said the gilded altar. Camdozaal has no rate in the catalogue, and the Wiki puts it at about 2,400 Prayer XP an hour at best. The rule against losing coins no longer swaps a paid method for a free one that has no rate, because that would not make sense. Both now show the same method.

## Herblore says what it needs

On a Members account without Druidic Ritual, Herblore said the engine had no method. It now says "Brewing potions opens at level 3 and needs Druidic Ritual." Any skill gets the same kind of message when its first method is still locked.

## Money methods checked on six accounts

Every money-making method was checked on fresh, trained and maxed accounts in both modes. None crashes, and none that the app lets you do earns nothing. Methods that lose coins at current prices are blocked, as they should be. This check now runs with every test run too.

## Faster decisions, and what was taken back

2.178.0 also made plans forecast the Hitpoints that Melee and Ranged training gives. That is how the game works, but it changed every quest estimate, reshuffled the Members route and made its decisions about a third slower, so it is taken out again. A fresh account's Ranged plan from level 1 therefore still shows no total time: at 10 Hitpoints the plan cannot rate moss giants. The plan is worked out again from your real Hitpoints as you train.

Two repeated calculations now run once: the melee gear forecast, which the Attack, Strength, Defence and Slayer plans of one turn asked for separately, and the rate check inside the rule against losing coins. On the test machine every part of the Members route check now decides faster than in 2.177.0, and the slowest takes about 1,850 ms per decision instead of about 2,000.

Verified before delivery: 2,869 unit tests, the free-to-play route gate, all five parts of the Members route gate, the browser checks, typecheck and build, structure, unused-code, dead-CSS and data checks. Nothing was tested in the real game.
