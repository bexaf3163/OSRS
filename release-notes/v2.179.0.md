# OSRS Path 2.179.0

This release also carries the audit fixes planned for 2.178.0. That release was not published because the first part of the Members route check took 2,630 ms per decision in CI, over its 2,600 ms budget. Its notes describe those fixes.

## Faster decisions

Two repeated calculations now run once. The melee gear forecast was asked the same question by the Attack, Strength, Defence and Slayer plans of the same turn, and the rule against losing coins checked a method's rate even when nothing lost coins. The first part of the Members route check now takes about 1,920 ms per decision on the test machine, down from about 2,020.

## Slayer plans wear the gear you would buy

The audit timed every skill from 1 to 99. Members Slayer took about 2,600 hours on an account without a weapon, even with 50 million coins. Melee plans already assume the weapon and armour the Gear page would buy with your coins. The Slayer plan did not, so it rated your Slayer XP by unarmed damage.

Slayer XP is the melee damage you deal, so a Slayer plan now carries the same gear as a melee plan, all the way to the goal. At 60 in the combat skills, Slayer 1 to 99 now takes about 1,400 hours instead of 2,600, at about 9,400 XP an hour. Your own damage still caps the rate, so better combat levels and gear make it faster.
