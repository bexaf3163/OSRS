# OSRS Path 2.74.7

## Dragon Slayer I: all five of the Guildmaster's questions

S5-01 used to finish as soon as the Maze key arrived. The Guildmaster gives you the key on the third of his five questions, so the questions about Thalzar's and Lozar's map pieces could be skipped. Quest Helper counts all five as asked before it sends you to the Oracle and to Wormbrain. The step now finishes only when the game records all five questions as asked and you hold the Maze key. In the game, the list follows each question as you ask it. Before, all five lines stood on the Guildmaster's tile and could not tell themselves apart.

## Stage 7 boss fights: the food the game checks is on the list

Before the fights with the Khazard warlord (Tree Gnome Village) and the Black demon (The Grand Tree), the in-game list waits until you carry 10 lobsters. Neither step listed any food, so preparation could say "ready" while the arrow stopped and sent you for food. Fight Arena had the same check with the lobsters only recommended. Lobster ×10, or similar food, is now a required item in all three steps. A new route-wide check makes sure every item an in-game line checks in your bag is also on the step's list.

No new panels, gameplay automation or route reordering. Saves, route IDs and progress are unchanged. In-game acceptance is pending.
