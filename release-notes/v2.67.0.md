# OSRS Path 2.67.0

**Shopping answers one question: what do you still need before you go on?**

- **Next trip by default.** The list is for the step you are on and the next three open ones, the same batch your step's preparation uses. **This step** narrows it, **Stages** keeps the old range picker (your chosen range is remembered).
- **Grouped by what to do:** *Buy* (at the Grand Exchange, or a shop when it is known to be nearer and has the stock), *Take from the bank*, *Put on*, *Not confirmed*, *On the way*. What you already have and the optional extras are folded away. The six status tabs are gone.
- **Counts are fairer:** worn items count as owned. Food of another kind counts by healing (30 Trout cover 23 Salmon), and a stack another line needs is not counted twice. Armour and every tool tier count once for all steps. A bank you have not opened is "not confirmed", never "missing".
- **A long training step buys the whole goal at once** (one exchange visit) but takes one bag at a time from the bank.
- **Budget in one line:** what the purchases cost, the coins you have and what is short. Optional extras are shown apart. **Raise the coins** leads to your step's coin plan; Shopping does not plan money itself.
- **Honest sync wording:** "Open your bank in RuneLite to refresh", "Bank last checked …", "Offline — using last-known quantities". The old "Sync with my bank" button is gone: the app can only read the bank when you open it in the game.
- **Each empty state is its own:** everything owned (with *Back to the step*), nothing needed, no step in progress, offline, waiting for your character, or an error. None of them is "Nothing to buy".
- **Copy the exchange list** copies exchange purchases only. Bank withdrawals, shop and quest items are left out; unconfirmed items are listed apart. Road supplies show only when a teleport pays off; bag cleanup opens on request.

Not yet: potion doses are not converted between (1)–(4) sizes; a shop's quest or area access is not checked (Shopping only picks a shop it knows is nearer than the exchange).
