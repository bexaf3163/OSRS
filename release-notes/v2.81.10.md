# 2.81.10 — gathering capacity follows actual tool placement

- Gathering estimates free a bag slot only for a confirmed worn tool. Meeting the tool's Attack requirement no longer means it is assumed equipped.
- An unowned tool upgrade is evaluated as carried, matching the preparation that asks you to take it. Its forecast now agrees with the same tool after purchase and carrying.
- The correction is shared by gathering selection, upgrades, named-tool pacing and combined gathering/processing trips. Synthetic-content regressions cover worn versus carried tools and the purchase forecast without changing observed player state.
- These remain estimates for the prepared skilling kit, not a simulation of every unrelated item, future sale or exact session pace. No equipment is changed automatically; no interface, save schema, formula coefficients or bridge protocol were redesigned. Real-game checks belong to Claude.
