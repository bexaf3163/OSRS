# OSRS Path 2.65.1

**Dragon Slayer I gives Defence anyway: the combat plans now know it.** Later in the route, S5-09 (Dragon Slayer I) gives 18,650 Defence XP, about Defence 33, with no training. Defence trained on cows before the Hill Giants only helps the fights until then.

- Every "Defence N first" plan says so under Compare the plans.
- While that reward is still ahead, a Defence plan is no longer recommended just because it looks faster at the slow end of the estimate. It must also be at least 10% faster on the middle estimate. With the reported character it is about 15 minutes in 4 hours (6%), so the route's plan stays, and the card says why in one sentence.
- Once the reward is behind you (the step is done), Defence plans are compared like any other plan again.
- You can still pick any plan yourself: every plan in the comparison has its own **Use this plan** button. "Keep Defence at 1" in Settings still turns Defence plans off.
