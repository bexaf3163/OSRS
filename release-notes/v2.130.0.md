# OSRS Path 2.130.0

Four more quests join the Members route, so the Members quest-point total is now 123:

- **The Golem** (Crafting 20, Thieving 25). The guide covers the clay golem in Uzer, the 4 soft clay, the letter and Elissa at the Digsite, and Curator Haig's statuette in the Varrock Museum. It also covers the black mushroom ink, the phoenix feather quill and the golem's new program. The route brings a Shantay pass and 2 waterskins for the desert.
- **The Tourist Trap** (Fletching 10, Smithing 20). The guide covers Irena, the Mercenary Captain (level 47, fought with 5 trout as a margin) and the slave robes. It also covers the prototype dart from 3 bronze bars and 10 feathers, Ana in the barrel, the winch and the mine cart.
- **Children of the Sun**, a short Varlamore tale that starts in Varrock square and needs nothing.
- **Tale of the Righteous** (Strength 16, Mining 10, after Client of Kourend and X Marks the Spot). The guide covers Phileas Rimor, the Strange Device pushed and struck with ranged, magic and melee, and the Corrupt Lizardman (level 46, fought with 5 trout).

Verified before delivery: 2,655 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 65 Members-route quests finish), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
