**Item pre-flight guard: the arrow no longer leads you to a step you cannot do yet.**

- **Black Knights' Fortress (S3-02):** before you enter the fortress, and again at the hole, the game checks for the Cabbage from the Falador field (item 1965; the Draynor Manor cabbage has the same name and does not count). With none, the arrow turns to the Falador cabbage field (3058, 3290) with the instruction "Pick Cabbage south of Falador (do NOT pick Draynor cabbage)", the cabbage patch is highlighted, and the HUD shows a red "Missing Item" chip when you are near the fortress.
- **The same guard on every quest step that uses an item on an object or an NPC or hands one in** (45 step lines in 13 quest steps): Cook's Assistant, Romeo & Juliet, Rune Mysteries, The Knight's Sword (redberry pie before Thurgo, blurite ore and two iron bars before the smithing), Vampyre Slayer (garlic, stake and hammer before the manor), Pirate's Treasure, Prince Ali Rescue (dye, rope, pink skirt, paste, bronze key before the jail), Goblin Diplomacy, Demon Slayer (bucket of water before the drain, Silverlight before Delrith), Dragon Slayer I (silk, lobster pot, unfired bowl and mind bomb before the Dwarven Mine, planks, nails and hammer for the ship, the Anti-dragon shield before boarding).
- **Where the arrow goes instead:** the nearest bank if the bank snapshot holds the item, else the place named for the item (a shop, an NPC, a patch), else the Grand Exchange. It never turns an unknown into a missing item: before the bag has been read, or with a bank you have not opened, nothing is blocked.
- **HUD:** a red line "Missing Item: <item> - Turn back!" under the step name while you are within 40 tiles of the blocked step.
- **Not guarded on purpose:** the instanced Misthalin Mystery house, the Crandor map (made from three parts), the Anti-dragon shield inside Crandor (no way back to a shop; the ship step checks it) and Elvarg's head.
