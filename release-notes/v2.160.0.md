# OSRS Path 2.160.0

Members accounts get real Runecraft, Fishing and Firemaking methods, with rates from the OSRS Wiki training guides.

Runecraft: Guardians of the Rift from level 27 (after Temple of the Eye, with any pickaxe): 25,000 XP per hour up to 50, 40,000 to 75, 50,000 to 85, and 65,000 in a small team from 85. Lava runes at the Fire Altar from level 23: 42,400 XP per hour, about break-even. A Members Runecraft plan from 1 to 99 now takes about 222 hours instead of 1,818.

Fishing: Tempoross from level 35 (30,000 XP per hour, 62,000 from 70, 65,000 from 80, 68,000 from 90) and Barbarian Fishing from 48 at Otto's Grotto, dropping the fish (23,000 XP per hour, 37,000 from 58, 48,000 from 70, 52,000 from 80, 54,000 from 90; Agility and Strength 15, 30 and 45 for trout, salmon and sturgeon). A Members Fishing plan to 99 takes about 221 hours instead of 289.

Firemaking: Wintertodt from level 50 (161,000 XP per hour, rising to 290,000 from 90). It makes money and the plan takes it instead of buying logs; the time to 99 stays about the same.

Each rate is the guide's figure at the start of its level band, so the real rate inside a band is a little higher.

Verified before delivery: the unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 132 Members-route quests), the browser checks, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
