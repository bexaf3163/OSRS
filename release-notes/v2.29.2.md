**Small fixes and one new restore option, found by testing 2.29.1 on a real PC with the game.**

- **Restore: earlier steps the game cannot confirm.** After "Sync with the account", the card now also lists the steps before your furthest confirmed one that nothing in the game can prove (setting up the client, banking, buying). They are shown separately and marked only when you press "I did these". A step whose quest or levels the game says are not done is never offered.
- **Coins.** When the coins in your bag already cover what the next steps need, the plan no longer says "I do not know yet how much you have" just because the bank has not been opened.
- **Search closes with one Escape** (the search field used to eat the first one).
- **The stage list no longer jumps** when you open a step other than the current one: the "To the current step" button keeps its place and shows "You are on the current step" otherwise.
- **"Not checked" line:** the "open the bank" hint stays on the same line as the item names instead of dropping to a line of its own.
- **Wording:** the Diagnostics text in Settings had a missing word.

**Checked.** App tests, the data checks, the build and the browser interface checks pass. Not run in the live game: the new restore option (it needs a profile with unmarked steps before your furthest one).
