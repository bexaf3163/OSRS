# OSRS Path 2.53.3 — Ancient Guardian kit before the entrance, Brutus, Cooking 10, Jungle Demon

From a full audit of the 2.53.0 route export; each item below was checked against the current data and the OSRS Wiki first.

- **Below Ice Mountain:** the pickaxe and 10-15 Trout or Salmon were asked for only inside the fight room, where a stage-30 line said "items: none". They are now asked for at the entrance (stage 30), named on the walk to it (stage 20), and the entrance line is guarded: with the kit missing the arrow turns to a bank or the Grand Exchange and the red "Missing Item" chip shows. The walkthrough lists the preparation before "Enter the dungeon". Any pickaxe tier and any listed food count.
- **The Ides of Milk:** the card said you cannot die in the quest. Brutus's plain hits cannot kill you (1 HP floor), but his Snort and Growl specials can, and protection prayers do not stop them. The wording now says so.
- **Cooking 10 (S8-04):** trout needs Cooking 15 and shrimps give 30 XP, not 15. The goal now gives the 854 XP left, shrimps (29) or sardines (22) from level 1, herring from level 5, and says trout cannot be used yet.
- **Monkey Madness, Jungle Demon:** "Protect from Magic or Protect from Melee" is now one instruction: Protect from Magic, three or more squares away, Ranged.
- **App:** the item guard's app copy now counts alternative items (pickaxe tiers, food) as the plugin does.
- **Checked:** `tsc`, build, 970 Vitest tests, `check-data`, the full Java suite, UI smoke. Not tested in game: the Ancient Guardian guard (the quest is already done on the test character).

Restart RuneLite through OSRS Path after updating.
