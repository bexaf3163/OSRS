# OSRS Path 2.74.1

Cumulative stabilization repairs from the project audit, preserving the single expandable in-game guide and existing route IDs/progress.

- Save recovery preserves damaged/future originals and conflicting profile sources; native write failures are reported and failed profile edits can be retried.
- Updates verify SHA-256. Bridge snapshots preserve current-session facts and apply order; bounded event output expires stalled readers.
- Preparation distinguishes exact carried/noted/bank quantities, unread bank facts, recipe deficits and potion doses/physical bottles. Shared reserves protect items still needed by the selected route.
- Combat planning credits the XP of required collection, retains food/shopping after the XP goal, includes minimum loot-bank trips and avoids counting unearned optional loot as cash.
- The collection HUD shows the actual bone objective instead of incidental Attack 34. Projected XP reaching its level cannot advance an unconfirmed collection phase. Required preparation still leads the card.
- Shopping says Stock up: purchase reserves cover selected steps rather than one carried inventory. Existing quantities, bank withdrawals and exchange payloads remain.
- Skills quest rewards and Gear quest unlocks follow live completion evidence. A skipped quest does not prove its reward or a free toll. Explicit manual completion remains a fallback while facts are unread.
- Magic funding no longer promises cowhide income on an ineligible/unread account; owned elemental staves are reused. Lost City's entry no longer requires unrelated goals from a shared multi-skill preparation step. Spell/ranged training does not borrow a future melee purchase banner.

The release workflow runs data validation, all Vitest tests, TypeScript/build, full Java tests, browser smoke, source Electron E2E and the actual isolated portable EXE before publication. The handoff and SHA256SUMS.txt are attached with the single portable EXE; no archive is needed.

The full audit remains open. Broader trainer coverage, simultaneous food/potion/loot packing, continuous compound skill/quest phases, remaining recovery/unknown-state consumers and complete real-game/updater acceptance are tracked in CLAUDE_NEXT_SESSION.md and the source backlog. The limited real-PC game check used an earlier preview; it does not establish live acceptance of every change in 2.74.1. Keep the working EXE/data backup when upgrading.
