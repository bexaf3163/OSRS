# OSRS Path 2.74.0

**Repair block 2a of the 2.72.2 review: one in-game guide instead of two overlays.**

- **One card on the game screen.** The text plate in the top-left corner and the old "What you need" card are gone; so is the docked bar. One compact guide
  replaces them. It shows what to do now, how far along you are, and at most one warning. Nothing else is on the closed card: no step code, no long title,
  no item list, no list of what comes next.
- **The action comes from your selected plan.** On S4-03 with the cow plan it reads "Train Attack to 40 · Attack 30 → 40 · Accurate", not the route
  step's title. A quest shows its current line with "Stage 3 of 7"; an item phase shows "Collect 50 Big bones from Hill Giants".
- **Preparation that stops you comes first.** With no food, the card says "Withdraw food", "Salmon or similar: 0 / 21 · in the bank", then "Then: train
  Attack to 40". Food that is with you, or substitutes the step accepts, is never asked for again. Optional gear and "for later" items never take the
  place of a missing required item; they wait under "More advice".
- **States are said as they are**: with you, worn, in the bank, missing, "bank not checked", "last seen" (a bank copy from your last session), and
  "reading the bank" right after log-in. An item in the bank is never called ready, and a bank that was not seen is never called empty.
- **Click the header to open the details**: the route step, how to do it, what to prepare (missing first, the rest as "All 3 items are with you"), what
  comes next and "More advice" with its count. The choice is saved. Nothing opens on hover or by itself.
- **Position and size.** A fresh layout puts the card top right, clear of RuneLite's hover text, the chat, the inventory and the middle of the screen.
  Alt-drag moves it or stretches its width (240–420 px); a position you set earlier is kept. The open card never takes more than 60 % of the window
  height. Dark, nearly opaque, regular weight type, small vector icons.
- The app now sends the selected combat plan's current and next phase to the game (`guide.plan`), so the card leads with them.
