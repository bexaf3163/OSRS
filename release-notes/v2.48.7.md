# OSRS Path 2.48.7 — Modern expandable game guide

The RuneLite guide now uses one compact card with details opened by clicking **Steps** or **Advice**. Selecting the open tab again closes it; switching tabs keeps it open. Moving the pointer away no longer closes the card. Selecting another route step starts with details closed.

- A quieter charcoal Glass surface, light current action and periwinkle accents match the desktop redesign. Classic style, font/size controls, positioning and full-list mode remain available.
- The card keeps the quest stage and distance in one metadata row. The canonical current action retains requirements, including alternative approaches.
- Stage and recovery warnings remain visible with details closed and on Advice. Noncritical detour suggestions stay in the details.
- The existing manual next/back/resume, preparation, item/place navigation and advice controls are retained. No progression, readiness, route or gameplay engine was replaced.
- While the Glass guide is visible, the direction marker is smaller and avoids repeating same-level distance. Floor/underground and arrival feedback remain visible; the standalone arrow retains its configured size and distance.

The existing desktop redesign, profiles, progress and settings are preserved. Restart RuneLite through OSRS Path after updating to load the new plugin.
