# OSRS Path 2.120.0

- 103 Members money methods are now reviewed by hand, up from 86. New this release:
  - Cooking anglerfish, dark crabs and marlin at the Rogues' Den fire, a few steps from its bank, with no requirements.
  - Magic longbows, wild and summer pies, amethyst arrowtips, ascension and diamond ascension bolts, and sunlight antler bolts.
  - Fishing anglerfish (with a fishing rod) and dark crabs (with a lobster pot, in the Wilderness).
  - Runite ore in the Wilderness, where other players also hop for the rocks.
  - The Motherlode Mine, amethyst and redwoods.
- Held back with the reason:
  - Cooked moonlight antelope, which needs 50 Hunters' rumours.
  - The ironman battlestaff guide, because the route plans a trading account.
  - Double nature runes, which need the Achievement Diary cape.
  - Stranglewood fishing, which needs Desert Treasure II.
