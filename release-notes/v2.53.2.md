# OSRS Path 2.53.2 — dialogue highlight, pick-ups after kills, playing Shield of Arrav alone

- **Dialogue answers are framed from the whole stage.** The plugin used to frame only the answers of the step's own conversation, so in Shield of Arrav (Baraek) nothing was highlighted. It now frames every answer the lines of the current stage name after "Dialogue:". Baraek's line also says outright: ask about the Phoenix Gang (20 coins), do not buy the furs. All 334 answers named in the guide were checked against Quest Helper's dialogue strings; two gaps (Below Ice Mountain "Yes.", Recipe for Disaster's second accepted answer) were filled.
- **Kills that drop a needed item say "then pick it up".** Jonny the Beard (Intel report), the Weaponsmaster (two Phoenix crossbows), Goraks (claws) and the Monk of Zamorak (golden key, both lines) now read "kill, then pick up", and the line is done once the item is in the bag.
- **Shield of Arrav alone.** The last step now says what it needs: a player of the other gang to swap halves with (chat channel "OSRS SOA" or a second account), and that the quest is optional (1 quest point, 600 coins), so alone you can skip it.
- **Checked:** `tsc`, build, 969 Vitest tests, `check-data`, the full Java suite (490, new: `DialogKeysTest`).

The dialogue frame is plugin-side: restart RuneLite through OSRS Path after updating.
