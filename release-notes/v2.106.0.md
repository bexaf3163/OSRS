# OSRS Path 2.106.0

- New Members quest: Jungle Potion (1 QP, needs Druidic Ritual and Herblore 3). It joins the early Members stage of a Members account's route and never shows in free-to-play mode. Every stage is on the card: Trufitus's start, the five herbs, cleaning each one and bringing it back, the cave and the finish. The aggressive level 53 Jogres and level 46 Harpie Bug Swarms in the jungle are estimated with your kit, so the quest's "defend yourself" requirement is shown met or not, and Trout x5 (or similar food) is a required item.
- Better time estimates for Members accounts: quests with a fight now also count what their unfinished prerequisites give you (Nature Spirit and the Ghostspeak amulet from The Restless Ghost). Turns with an unknown remaining time in the Members route gate dropped from 42 to 7.

Verified before delivery: 2,636 unit tests, the F2P route gate (23 of 23 quests, 0 of 211 turns with an unknown time), the Members route gate (43 of 43 quests, no check, 7 of 340 turns with an unknown time), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
