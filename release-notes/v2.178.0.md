# OSRS Path 2.178.0

This release starts the full audit. A new sweep plans every skill from every start level to 99, on a fresh account and on a trained one, in free-to-play and Members. It checks that each plan reaches 99 through methods you can use, each with a map place, a time and no avoidable loss. It also checks that the Train card and the plan start with the same method. The first run checked 10,584 plans and found the problems below, which are now fixed. A shorter version of the sweep now runs with every test run.

## The route no longer stops at Members skilling spots

Wintertodt, Giants' Foundry, Tithe Farm, barbarian fishing at Otto's Grotto, teak trees and the rune altars had no place on the map. A plan could pick them, but the route could not send you there and stopped with a check. All 22 methods now have their place, with coordinates from the Wiki. The data check now rejects any method a plan can pick that has no place.

## Combat plans count the Hitpoints you gain

Every Melee or Ranged hit also gives a third as much Hitpoints XP. The plan's forecast raised only the skill you train, so a fresh account training Ranged still had 10 Hitpoints at Ranged 56. Moss giants counted as unsafe there, and the whole plan had no time. The forecast now raises Hitpoints with the XP, as the game does.

On Members, the route now takes The Ides of Milk, Witch's House and Mountain Daughter earlier, because their fights come out sooner once training counts its Hitpoints.

## One coin trip for the whole kit

When you were short of coins for a set of gear, the route earned for one piece, sent you to buy it, then asked you to earn again for the next piece, sometimes for 54 coins at a time. It now earns once for every purchase still ahead in the same preparation. Before Witch's House, for example, the route earns about 1,800 coins once for the whole iron set instead of five times. The free-to-play route check now finishes in 201 turns instead of 211, and the Members one in 1,058 instead of 1,099.

## Prayer: the Train card and the plan agree

On a Members account the Train card said Camdozaal, while the plan below it said the gilded altar. Camdozaal has no rate in the catalogue, and the Wiki puts it at about 2,400 Prayer XP an hour at best. The rule against losing coins no longer swaps a paid method for a free one that has no rate, because that would not make sense. Both now show the same method.

## Herblore says what it needs

On a Members account without Druidic Ritual, Herblore said the engine had no method. It now says "Brewing potions opens at level 3 and needs Druidic Ritual." Any skill gets the same kind of message when its first method is still locked.

## Money methods checked on six accounts

Every money-making method was checked on fresh, trained and maxed accounts in both modes. None crashes, and none that the app lets you do earns nothing. Methods that lose coins at current prices are blocked, as they should be. This check now runs with every test run too.

Verified before delivery: 2,868 unit tests, the free-to-play route gate, all five parts of the Members route gate, the browser checks, typecheck and build, structure, unused-code, dead-CSS and data checks. Nothing was tested in the real game.
