# OSRS Path 2.98.0

- Items you can only make are planned now. When nothing sells an item, the app plans to make it from its recipe: the ingredients first, then the making, with the recipe's time. The first one is Ecto-tokens for Animal Magnetism: 5 tokens a worship at the Ectofuntus, each worship needing one bone, one pot and one bucket (OSRS Wiki). For 20 tokens that is 4 of each, not 20. Before, the app said "Check the offer and coins" for an item no shop or exchange sells. The 60 seconds a worship are an estimate, and the recipe says so.
- An item that a shop or the exchange sells still comes from there; making it is only the fallback. The shopping list names the recipe when the step gives no instructions of its own.
- The Members route gate keeps the Ghostspeak amulet from The Restless Ghost, as a player does, and now finishes 28 of 35 quests (Animal Magnetism and Nature Spirit included). It stops at Fairytale I, whose Tanglefoot fight is not modelled yet. The known debt is lowered from 9 to 7 open quests.

Verified before delivery: 2,625 unit tests (new: Ecto-tokens are made from their recipe, 5 a worship), the F2P route gate (23 of 23 quests, no check, 0 of 211 turns with an unknown time) and the Members route gate (28 of 35, within its debt of 7), the browser and Electron desktop checks, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
