# OSRS Path 2.161.0

Members Smithing gets Giants' Foundry, after Sleeping Giants. The plan forges two-metal swords with bars from the Grand Exchange: bronze and iron from level 15 (97,920 XP per hour), iron and steel from 30 (133,920), steel and mithril from 50 (164,640), and mithril and adamantite from 70 (198,000). The first three make money, about 80,000 to 141,000 coins an hour with bought bars; mithril and adamantite costs about 37,000 an hour. A Members Smithing plan to 99 now takes about 79 hours instead of 132.

The rates are the OSRS Wiki guide's figures with the best moulds, which it assumes at level 81, so they are lower until you unlock better moulds.

Darts stay out of the plan for now: the Wiki gives XP per dart and buy limits but no XP per hour, because the rate depends on click speed.

Verified before delivery: the unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 132 Members-route quests), the browser checks, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
