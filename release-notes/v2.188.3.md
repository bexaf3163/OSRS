# OSRS Path 2.188.3

## Grand Exchange offers count as yours

While 260 diamond necklaces sat in a sell offer, the plan saw neither the necklaces nor the coins they had already sold for. So it decided you had only 7,047 coins and switched Crafting to emerald amulets. The plugin now reads your Grand Exchange offers and counts what they hold, as if it were already in the bank:

- for a sell offer, the items not yet sold and the coins already made;
- for a buy offer, the items already bought and the coins still reserved.

Collecting the offer moves all of it to the bank anyway, so the plan stays the same before and after you collect.

## The exchange list says what your coins buy

The Grand Exchange list now says what your coins buy of the step's purchases, in the step's own proportions. One example is "your 486,000 coins buy 248 Gold bar + 248 Diamond", so you don't have to work it out. It appears once your coins cover at least one batch.
