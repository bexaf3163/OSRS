# OSRS Path 2.187.0

## More Members methods for Thieving, Runecraft and Mining

Six methods from the OSRS Wiki training guides, checked on 25 November 2026, join the Members plans. Each one has its real requirements and a place on the map.

- Thieving: chests in the Aldarin villas from 36 to 45, about 40,000 to 50,000 XP an hour with a lockpick. They need Children of the Sun.
- Thieving: stealing artefacts in Port Piscarilius from 49, about 150,000 XP an hour rising to 261,000 at 99 with practice. This is now the plan's pick from 50 on, ahead of knights and blackjacking.
- Thieving: stealing valuables in Civitas illa Fortis from 50, about 65,000 XP an hour with little attention. It needs Children of the Sun.
- Runecraft: blood runes at Arceuus from 77 and soul runes from 90, about 36,000 and 44,000 XP an hour. They make about 1,474 blood runes or 1,451 soul runes an hour, so they earn coins at live prices. They need Mining 38, Crafting 38, a chisel and a pickaxe.
- Mining: the Volcanic Mine from 70, about 64,200 to 79,300 XP an hour in a team. It needs Bone Voyage. It costs numulites and food, so iron power-mining still ranks ahead and the mine stays an alternative.

Guardians of the Rift still trains Runecraft faster than the Arceuus runes, so it stays the pick and the rune altars are listed as alternatives.
