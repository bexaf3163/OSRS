# OSRS Path 2.146.0

Olaf's Quest is on the route. It plans an axe, a tinderbox and a spade, a staff of fire and runes for twenty Fire Bolts on Ulfric from his safespot, and 3 trout for the skeleton fremennik that carries the key.

Combat training can now use the Gemstone Crab in the Tlati Rainforest. The planner knew the method but had no place for it and did not count it as combat XP, so a character with low Defence got no timed plan for Fairytale I's Tanglefoot fight. Moss giants (Varrock Sewers), lesser demons (Karamja Volcano) and flesh crawlers (Stronghold of Security) also get a place on the map, so the arrow leads to their entrances.
