# OSRS Path 2.71.0

**Money: browse every earning method at any time, and start one toward a goal you choose.**

- **A Money section** in the navigation. It opens on one personal recommendation, a short list of the other methods, a search, a sort (best for you, profit an hour, coins to start, least effort) and a few filters (show locked methods, no coins needed to start, relaxed only, kind). The same evaluator and ranking as the coin card judge the methods, so the two never disagree. When your step is short of coins, the Money section ranks for that shortfall and recommends the same method as the card.
- **Honest rows.** Each row shows the net gp an hour range (or "Estimate unavailable", never a zero), the coins needed to start, the effort, and one badge. "Can do now" appears only when every requirement is known. A level, quest, tool or your coins that could not be checked shows "Not confirmed" with what is missing. Locked methods are hidden by default and list what they lack when shown.
- **Details under a row**: why the method is where it is, every requirement and how you meet it (✓ met, ✗ missing, ? unknown, 🛒 to buy), where it is and the steps, the money (same words as the coin card), risk, effort, confidence and price freshness. Sources and the calculation are folded.
- **Start… asks for the goal**: earn N more coins, cover the coins your step is short, or an open session you stop yourself. Coins are counted as you hold them: what you have now minus what you had at the start. Loot counts once it is sold. "More coins" needs your bank read once, so a bank opened later is not mistaken for coins made.
- **Starting makes the same guided plan as the coin card**: the arrow, the step's preparation panel, the in-game line, pause, resume and stop. Another method while one runs is an explicit "Switch to this method" that says what it stops. Your route step is never changed.
- **Goals bigger than one trip**: after a sale that leaves you short, the plan goes round again (to the spot, work, bank, sell) instead of sending you to buy with too few coins. This also applies to the coin card's own plans.
- The coin card has a **Browse earning methods** link.

Not verified live in the game yet: a full multi-trip run with real sales. All 12 catalogue methods are free-to-play; the membership check is in place for members methods when the catalogue gets them.
