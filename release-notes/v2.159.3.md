# OSRS Path 2.159.3

Members planning is faster after a level-up or a quest. Training forecasts now depend only on what each skill's training actually reads: its own items, its own and combat levels, the skills its recipes and shops need, and the 16 quests a training engine checks. A Woodcutting level no longer invalidates the Crafting forecast, and most quests no longer invalidate any training forecast.

On the whole Members route, the median planning decision is about 30% faster, and the slowest late-route decisions take about half as long. The route gate replay is about a third faster.

Cooking forecasts include the Firemaking level, because lighting a fire with a log needs it.

Verified before delivery: the unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 132 Members-route quests, with every reused forecast recomputed and compared), the browser checks, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
