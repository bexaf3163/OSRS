**New optional in-game layout: a docked bar that keeps the screen clear and opens the list only when you point at it.**

- **Docked bar (new setting "Docked bar", off by default):** one row at the top left instead of the HUD plate. It shows the step, what to do now (or the warning), the distance, the number of items in the bag, in the bank, missing and not yet checked (the unchecked are counted apart and never as missing), and the readiness percent.
- **The list opens on demand:** the "What you need" list appears under the bar while the mouse is over the bar or the list, at the open bank (the departure check), and for about 0.7 seconds after the mouse leaves, so crossing the gap does not close it. Otherwise only the bar is on screen.
- **Warnings come first:** a danger warning replaces the step name and takes the whole bar, in red with the red state dot. A long step name is cut with dots so what to do stays visible.
- **Never loses the list:** if the bar cannot be shown (no step, or the HUD is turned off), the list is shown as usual.
- **Works with the rest:** Alt-drag, "Large HUD text", "HUD background" and the Glass or Classic plate style apply to the bar too. With the setting off nothing changes.

**Checked.** App tests, data checks, the production build, the browser interface checks and all plugin tests pass, including new tests for the counts, the open and close rule, the text cutting and the drawing with the RuneLite font. Not run in the live game: the hover behaviour on the real screen. Turn "Docked bar" on in the plugin settings to try it.
