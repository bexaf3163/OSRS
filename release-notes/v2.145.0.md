# OSRS Path 2.145.0

The Fremennik Trials is on the route, which opens the Fremennik quests that follow it.

It plans 5,252 coins for the merchants, a tinderbox, a raw shark for the lyre and 20 lobsters for Lanzig, who is killed until a lyre drops (about 1 in 17), and for the level 69 Draugen. Koschei is fought without items, and dying to him is safe.

All seven trials run in parallel, so the in-game guide now carries up to 120 lines per quest stage instead of 60. Repeated stages share one entry, and puzzle lines in Peer the Seer's house and Swensen's maze highlight their portals, ladders, trapdoors and furniture by name.

Fletching training now has a place on the map (the Grand Exchange), so the planner can route to it.

Verified before delivery: 2,664 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 101 Members-route quests finish in 814 turns), the browser checks on every page, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
