# OSRS Path 2.85.1

- Where to buy now weighs time over small savings, for quest items and for gear:
  - When a shop and the Grand Exchange differ by less than 300 gp, the nearer one goes first, and the Grand Exchange when your position is not known.
  - Gear advice puts the Grand Exchange first whenever it has a price, unless a shop saves 300 gp or more. The other place stays listed as "or".
  - A bigger saving still goes to the cheaper place, and a shop leads when the Grand Exchange has no usable price.
- A cheaper but weaker gear choice is now suggested only when it saves at least 300 gp and costs half as much or less.
- Live in-game acceptance of this release is still pending.
