# OSRS Path 2.128.0

- Members routes now include Elemental Workshop I. The guide covers the battered book in the Seers' Village bookcase and the hidden wall, then the water, air and fire rooms of the workshop. It also covers mining an elemental rock and killing the earth elemental (level 35, hits up to 4), smelting the elemental bar and making the shield. The quest needs Mining, Smithing and Crafting 20 and gives 1 quest point, so the Members total is now 113. The item list adds a bronze pickaxe, 4 coal, leather, thread and 3 trout as a safety margin.
- A followed skill goal no longer stays on the screen after you reach it. In free-to-play mode, once every F2P quest is done, the guide moves on by itself to the next unfinished skill checkpoint from the "Next in F2P" list. While quests remain, and in Members mode, it goes back to the quest route, which already builds in the training each quest needs. The RuneLite overlay follows without a click.

Verified before delivery: 2,651 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 57 Members quests finish), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game, including the automatic hand-off after a reached goal.
