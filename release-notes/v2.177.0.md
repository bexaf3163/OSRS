# OSRS Path 2.177.0

## The route earns coins by training too

When the route needs coins, it used to show only the best money-making method from the Money ranking, such as collecting cowhides. Now it also weighs the training methods that pay, the same ones the Money page lists under "Earn while you train", and picks whichever brings in more coins an hour. So when crafting ruby necklaces pays more than cowhides, the route sends you to craft, and you get the Crafting XP on top.

## Only what your coins can start

Processing methods need coins for their first batch of materials. A training method now counts as an earner, on the Money page and in the route, only if your known coins buy that whole first batch. Items the RuneLite plugin does not report count as none. When your coins are not known, nothing is left out.

At 40 in every skill on F2P, with the route check's saved prices, the best training earner changes with your coins:

| Your coins | Best training earner | Coins an hour |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | The monks of Edgeville | about 6,300 (cowhides stay ahead) |
| 20,000 | Make Ruby necklaces | about 170,000 |
| 5,000,000 | Make Amulets of defence | about 275,000 |
