# OSRS Path 2.159.0

Members skill training gains methods from the OSRS Wiki recipe pages, priced by today's Grand Exchange prices like the F2P methods in 2.158.0.

New Members methods:
- Crafting: molten glass into unpowered orbs, lantern lenses and light orbs from 46 (needs a glassblowing pipe), battlestaffs from 54, and green to black d'hide bodies from 63.
- Smithing: steel cannonballs from 35 after Dwarf Cannon, which pay while they train.
- Cooking: karambwan from 30 after Tai Bwo Wannai Trio, and monkfish, shark and anglerfish from 62.

Darts are not included yet, because the Wiki gives them no fixed time per action.

Profit figures are estimates at the prices of the moment; Grand Exchange buy limits and offer times are not modelled yet.

Verified before delivery: the unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 132 Members-route quests), the browser checks on every page, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
