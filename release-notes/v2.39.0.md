**"Fetch it first" now reaches the game, explains itself, and covers more than teleport tablets.**

- **Why it never showed live (diagnosis):** the suggestion lived only in the desktop app (the "How to get there" card and the step tip). The game list never received it. And its limits starved real routes: a saving of at least 60 tiles and a fetch of at most 120 tiles. The exchange is about 240 tiles from Draynor, so it could never pass from the places the route actually goes through; only a bank next to you could.
- **Relaxed limits:** the saving needed is 40 tiles (about 12 s of running) and the fetch has a 300-tile sanity cap. The saving already counts the detour (direct minus fetch plus the walk after the teleport), so a second tight cap only hid worthwhile shortcuts.
- **Every verdict is logged with its reason:** `exceeds_detour_limit`, `insufficient_savings`, `price_unknown`, `missing_coins`, `not_whitelisted` (and `price_too_high`), with the numbers behind it. Set `localStorage["osrs-put:debug-fetch"] = "1"` in the app's developer console to print them, or pass `onDecision` to the planner. An offered suggestion can carry a caveat (price unknown, coins short).
- **Beyond tablets:** the plan's missing items that have a known place (a shop, a seller, Aggie making a dye, the exchange) are judged as stops on the way. A stop is offered when it adds little to the walk now (or you are already next to it) and a separate trip later would cost clearly more. Several items at one place are one stop. On the Varrock shopping steps (S2-01, S2-05) the Grand Exchange comes first when it is within about 90 tiles, with all the items bought there.
- **In the game:** the stop is sent in the plan and shown as one clickable line, "⚡ Detour: Get Red dye at Aggie (+10 tiles, saves ~3 min)". It stays visible in the strict closed card, in the full list and in the Tip tab. A click points the arrow at the stop. The app only suggests: it buys nothing. The position is read every 15 seconds and the numbers are rounded, so the plan is not resent on every step you take.
- **Test with real coordinates:** a test takes the real Goblin Diplomacy plan with a missing Red dye, a player standing in Draynor (3093, 3244) and the Goblin Village target, and checks that the Aggie stop is produced.

**Checked.** App tests (new tests for the verdicts, the limits, the Draynor to Goblin Village case, the exchange priority and the payload), data checks, the production build, the browser interface checks and all plugin tests (new tests for the row, the click target and the refusal of a broken detour) pass. Not yet confirmed in the live game.
