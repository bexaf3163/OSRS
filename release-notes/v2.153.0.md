# OSRS Path 2.153.0

Temple of the Eye, the Lumbridge Guide part of Recipe for Disaster and The Ascent of Arceuus are on the route.

Temple of the Eye plans a bucket of water, a chisel and a bronze pickaxe, and the route walks you through the temple and the Guardians of the Rift tutorial.

Freeing the Lumbridge Guide plans a bucket of milk, an egg, a pot of flour and a cake tin. Traiborn enchants the milk, egg and flour, which become the cake of guidance for the Lumbridge Guide.

The Ascent of Arceuus plans 3 lobsters for 5 Tormented Souls (level 16, max hit 2) and the Trapped Soul (level 30, max hit 4) in Arceuus.

Verified before delivery: 2,687 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 121 Members-route quests), the browser checks on every page, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
