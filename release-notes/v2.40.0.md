**Fetch-it-first learns the difference between a shop and the Grand Exchange, the bank is never bypassed, and the game gets the way to go.**

- **Different limits by kind of place:** a vendor or a crafting NPC is worth a walk of up to 300 tiles, the Grand Exchange up to 500. A blocker of the current step (an item the step cannot be done without, such as Orange dye in Goblin Diplomacy) is judged by time: buying it must beat making it by hand by at least 30 s, whatever the tiles. Al Kharid to the Grand Exchange now offers the purchase for an active blocker.
- **Teleport-aware cost:** the way to a stop is counted from where a teleport in the bag would drop you (Varrock or Falador teleport tablet, the Chronicle, runes with the level). The text says so: "(+40 tiles, by Varrock teleport tablet, saves ~3 min)". Home Teleport is never counted, it has a long cooldown.
- **The bank is never bypassed:** an item the bank holds is not bought and not crafted. The stop is the bank that costs the least extra walk on the way: "Bank: Withdraw Orange dye at Draynor Bank (+12 tiles, saves ~1 min)". The plan carries the list, `bankWithdrawals` (id, name, quantity), and the open bank frames those items.
- **The bridge contract (plan payload):** `activeDetour` (text, label, targetTile, costTiles, actionType), `bankWithdrawals` (itemId, itemName, quantity) and `recommendedTransport` (type, destination, interactionId, interactionName, item, tile, text). The plugin refuses a malformed one whole. The old `detour` field is replaced by `activeDetour`.
- **In the game:** a new clickable row for the recommended transport (fairy ring, charter ship, canoe, boat, teleport). A click points the arrow at its first stop and frames the item it uses in the bag for 20 s. It activates nothing; you click. The bank withdrawals are a tip line.
- **Audit notes:** the plugin already keeps the bank per character (`BankSnapshot`, `OWNED` event with `bankSeen` and `bankSavedAt`), so the app needs no second copy: a stale second cache would claim items the bank no longer holds. The transport data has no game object or NPC ids, so interactionId is 0 and the names are used. There is no agility shortcut router yet; shortcut objects are highlighted only where a quest step names them.

Not yet confirmed in the live game.
