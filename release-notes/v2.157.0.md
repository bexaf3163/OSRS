# OSRS Path 2.157.0

Family Crest, One Small Favour and Another Slice of H.A.M. are on the route.

Update the RuneLite plugin with this release, because the app now speaks protocol 8. One Small Favour has 66 in-game stages, and the plugin now takes up to 80 stages of a quest instead of 40. An older plugin still gets the first 40 stages, as before, and the app asks you to update it.

Family Crest plans the shrimps, salmon, tuna, bass and swordfish for Caleb, a bronze pickaxe, two rubies with the ring and necklace moulds, and a staff of fire with Death, Air, Water and Earth runes. Chronozon (level 170, max hit 18) dies only after each of the four elemental Blast spells hits him, so the estimate casts Fire Blast from a safespot and takes no damage. The steel gauntlets you get at the end count as yours.

One Small Favour plans the steel, bronze and iron bars, the herbs, the hot water, a pot, soft clay, a chisel, a hammer, an adamant pickaxe and 10 lobsters. The pigeon cages and the uncut gems come from the quest itself, so the route never buys them. The Slagilith (level 92, max hit 13) takes full damage only from a pickaxe, so the estimate fights it with the adamant pickaxe.

Another Slice of H.A.M. plans a bullseye lantern and 10 lobsters. The H.A.M. Archer and Mage on the tower cannot be reached in melee, so the estimate shoots them with the crossbow and bronze bolts the quest leaves upstairs. Sigmund is estimated as his level 50 version, because the Wiki page keeps the level 64 one in a second box that the data does not read yet. The ancient mace you get counts as yours.

Verified before delivery: the unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 132 Members-route quests), the browser checks on every page, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
