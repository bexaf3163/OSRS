# OSRS Path 2.80.4

Simpler skill recommendations and more realistic training estimates.

- Compact skill icons open a recommendation directly, including separate Attack, Strength and Defence. Balanced is the default; choose XP or Money when needed. Details and alternatives start collapsed.
- Ranged and Magic estimates include food, eating, healing and bank time. Equipping a banked bow removes the old melee weapon and shield from predictions. Loot collection slows both XP and supply spending per hour.
- Shared processing recipes now cover Fletching and Herblore, plus corrected wine, Prayer, Superheat and rune crafting calculations. Recommendations name the actual product and supplies.
- Known missing next-trip supplies are checked against known available coins. Unknown bank contents or prices remain unchecked. Training forecasts follow changing methods and optional upgrades account for supply reserves and acquisition time.
- Money priorities use known proceeds after modeled supplies, pickups and bank time. Route combat income is explicitly after food; potion and armour acquisition costs remain an open limitation.

Single portable Windows EXE with the bundled RuneLite bridge. Saves, route IDs and explicit followed plans are preserved. No gameplay automation.

Publication requires the existing automated data, test, build, browser, source Electron and isolated portable checks. Real RuneLite acceptance remains assigned to Claude; this release does not claim live-game validation. Remaining guide-only Members skills and other limitations are recorded in docs/CODEX_ENGINE_HARDENING_HANDOFF.md and the attached continuation file.
