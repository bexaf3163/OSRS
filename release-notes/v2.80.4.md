# OSRS Path 2.80.4

Training recommendations now use a harder, more consistent shared model.

- **Full-goal forecasts.** Training time now follows the route across future level brackets instead of extrapolating only the first method, with the model refreshed as levels change. Measured pace stays attached to the method that was actually measured.
- **More realistic combat training.** Melee, Ranged and strike Magic now share recovery, food, banking and loot-time rules. Bow loadouts replace incompatible melee weapon/shield combinations before the estimate, and unknown or unsupported combat facts stay unknown instead of becoming optimistic guesses.
- **More skills on the processing engine.** Wiki-backed methods were added for wine, explicit banked Prayer, Superheat Item iron, the F2P rune altars, Fletching and Herblore. Shared batch, requirements, station, supply and output rules are reused instead of step-specific formulas.
- **XP / Balanced / Money recommendations.** The compact Skills view can rank by speed, balance or known profit. Unknown costs are never treated as free, missing next-trip kit can block an unaffordable choice, and an existing followed plan remains visible instead of being silently replaced.
- **Gear, food and funding are stricter.** Upgrade advice reuses shared equipment eligibility and acquisition costs, next-trip kit is reserved before declaring spare coins, and fallback food advice respects membership and compares price against effective healing.

No gameplay automation was added. Existing saves, route IDs, adopted plans and progress remain compatible.
