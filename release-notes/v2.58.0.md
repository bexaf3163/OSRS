# OSRS Path 2.58.0

## Fixed: the game freezing after a level-up or step change

The game could freeze right after a step changed in the middle of a fight (three times in the logs: 5, 7 and 15 October, always the same
error, "scripts are not reentrant"). The plugin asked the game for a quest's state from inside a chat message (the "Congratulations, you've
just advanced your Attack level" line), and the game cannot start a script while it is already running one. The plugin now only asks during
the game tick, keeps the last answer for everything else, and swallows the game's refusal if it ever happens anyway.

## Fixed: no arrow back into the cave

After leaving the Stronghold of Security (or any dungeon on a step with a route) to bank or buy food, the arrow pointed at a place inside
the cave that cannot be drawn from outside, so there was nothing to follow. The route now leads back to the way in (the entrance) as soon as
you are outside, and on to the room again once you are down. Standing at the entrance does not flip it back and forth.

## Fewer windows at the Grand Exchange

The exchange used to draw four plates at once: the HUD, the "What you need" list, the "Exchange · what to buy" card and the "Bulk list" card.
Now there is one card: the bulk list while it still has something to buy (anything the step needs that is not in it follows as "Also for
S3-08"), otherwise the step's card. The "What you need" list stays away and the HUD is one line while the exchange window is open. Danger and
low health still show.
