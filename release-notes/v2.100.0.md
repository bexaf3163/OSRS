# OSRS Path 2.100.0

- Every quest on the route can now be planned to the end in Members mode: a fresh Members account in the route gate finishes all 35 (the 23 free ones and the 12 Members ones), with no check on the way.
- Monkey Madness I, the last one: the Jungle Demon is fought the way the quest's wiki strategy says, three squares away with Protect from Magic on, where it only casts magic. The app now knows that a prayer can block everything a foe does at range: with Prayer 37 or more there is no damage to plan food for, below it the fight is not ready. The prayer drains 1 point every 3 seconds; the kit's prayer potions cover that.
- The Members route gate now fails if any quest is left unfinished, like the F2P one. Its known debt is the turns whose remaining time is still unknown (253 of 312), which may only shrink.

Verified before delivery: 2,626 unit tests (new: the Jungle Demon does no damage with Protect from Magic and is not ready below Prayer 37), the F2P route gate (23 of 23 quests, no check, 0 of 211 turns with an unknown time) and the Members route gate (35 of 35, no check), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
