# OSRS Path 2.86.1

- Found by simulating a fresh account through the F2P quests turn by turn, then fixed in the shared engines:
  - Melee training no longer asks you to swap weapons back and forth: a worn weapon counts as owned.
  - Training never sends a weak character to Moss giants: a method whose opponent can hit too hard for your Hitpoints is locked, with the reason, and Chickens and cows win at level 1.
  - The planner keeps its current quest unless another is proven clearly faster, so equipping an item or reading the bank no longer swaps the goal.
  - Vampyre Slayer's regeneration requirement is met by the modelled Count Draynor fight; Pirate's Treasure estimates its level-4 gardener; Prince Ali Rescue says to walk past the level 26 jail guards. A fresh account now reaches 21 of 23 F2P quests through planned actions (earn, buy, equip, train, quest).
  - Delrith's fight counts the two level-7 and one level-20 dark wizards of the stone circle.
- Skills: the Equipment line is the engine's own decision: buy the advised tier and what it saves ("Buy an Adamant axe (≈ 517 gp): it saves about 23 min before the next tier"), why your current tool stays, or the items the method uses. The fixed per-skill notes are gone.
- After all 23 F2P quests, Path shows "Next in F2P": the unfinished F2P checkpoints, good value first, then the quickest for your levels and gear, each with the engine's method and a Follow button. Members content is not planned.
- In game: the training line says the engine's choice in a few words ("Train woodcutting to 36: Oak trees, bank at Draynor Bank."), and the HUD plaque no longer shows the internal step code.

Still open: Below Ice Mountain (pillar mining) and Dragon Slayer (Melzar's Maze safespot magic) end in a check; remaining-time estimates are mostly unknown.

Verified before delivery: 2,630 unit tests and 769 browser checks; typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Paired timing against 2.85.2: mean non-XP update script time +1% (12.4 → 12.6 ms), cold-start script time at most +6%, within the 10% budget. The Java plugin tests, native Electron checks and the isolated Portable EXE run in the release workflow; they could not run in the development container. No real-game testing is claimed.
