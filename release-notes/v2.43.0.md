**Fast travel along the route: the game now shows the quick way as an action, points at the dock or the canoe station, and drops the old arrow after you teleport.**

- **A short action chip on the game HUD** whenever a teleport, canoe or ferry saves a real walk: "⚡ Use Chronicle → Champions' Guild", "⚡ Break Varrock teleport tablet → Varrock Square", "⚡ Cast Varrock Teleport → Varrock Square", "⚡ Canoe: Lumbridge → Barbarian Village", "⚡ Ferry: talk to Captain Tock → Corsair Cove". The app only suggests: nothing is clicked for you.
- **Canoes and ferries point at the right thing.** Clicking the row sends the arrow to the dock or the canoe station and highlights the captain, or the Canoe Station object, there. Canoes had no first stop at all before, so the click did nothing.
- **No stale arrow after the ride.** A teleport, boat or fairy ring ends the arrow to the dock and sends it back to the step from where you landed. The app now judges the way again the moment you land instead of at the next 15-second poll.
- **Canoe fix.** When the best canoe needs more Woodcutting than you have, the best one you can chop now is offered too (a log canoe at level 12, a dugout at 27). A canoe is ready only with an axe at hand: before, the app called it ready without one.
- **New ferry:** Rimmington to Corsair Cove and back, free, after The Corsair Curse is started (Captain Tock; Cabin Boy Colin after the quest). It is shown as possible, never ready, because the quest state cannot be read.
- **The route buys the book properly.** The Chronicle step (S1-10) now includes the 1,800 gp the book and ten cards cost, so a player who has not yet earned it sees that. The Magic 25 step now recommends Law runes next to the Air and Fire runes it already lists, so the Varrock Teleport branches have what they ask for.
- **Checked:** new tests for the Chronicle, tablet and spell choice from Draynor to Varrock, the Magic unlock levels (25, 31, 37), canoes with and without an axe or the level, both ferries in both directions, the chip and stop in the plan payload, and the plugin side (chip, highlights, stale-arrow rule). Not confirmed in the live game: the Canoe Station and fairy-ring highlight use the object names from the wiki, and the Corsair Cove dock tile is the ship's tile from the Quest Helper data.
