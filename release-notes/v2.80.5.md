# OSRS Path 2.80.5

More reliable combat preparation and supply budgets.

- Combat comparisons include consumed Strength potions and the armour upgrades actually needed. Small losses stay visible; unchecked costs stay unpriced.
- Funding distinguishes unchecked supplies from a genuinely affordable next trip. Gear explains when the current opponent is unsupported.
- Quest preparation checks an unread bank before directing you to buy missing supplies. Earlier-session bank data cannot establish absence, and unresolved corrective targets clear outdated navigation.
- Whole-goal Shopping, funding and cleanup protection retain food and armour for all remaining combat phases. The immediate preparation checklist still asks only for the current phase. Switching training methods clears obsolete combat purchases.

Single portable Windows EXE with the bundled RuneLite bridge. Existing saves and route IDs are preserved. No new menus or gameplay automation.

Publication requires data, unit, full Java, build, browser, source Electron and isolated portable checks. Live-game acceptance of these changes remains open; automated fixtures are not measured combat results. Food substitution, acquisition travel/fees, goal-wide potion budgeting and remaining Members models remain in the attached Claude handoff.
