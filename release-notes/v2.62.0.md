# OSRS Path 2.62.0

## "Am I ready for this?" in one line

Above the preparation list of every step there is now a verdict: **Ready**, **Ready with cautions**, **Preparation needed**, **Requirements
not met** or **Cannot assess yet**, with the next thing to do in one sentence, named with your real items and counts:

- "Bank: withdraw 13 Salmon — you already own it. Then buy about 13 Salmon."
- "Preparation needed: bring food before going — Trout ×8."
- "Bank contents have not been checked; visit a bank to assess owned supplies."

Under "Why" the reasons are grouped: access and quest requirements, what the activity needs, safety supplies, optional upgrades, and what could not
be checked. Unknown is never shown as missing or as ready. The bank says how fresh it is ("Bank as last seen 3 days ago").

## Minotaurs and other training fights, for your kit

For a combat training step the verdict checks your worn weapon and armour, the food for the next trip **by healing** (20 Lobsters cover 26 Salmon;
20 Cooked meat do not), what the bank holds, and both versions of the opponent side by side: "Minotaur (level 12) suit your current kit best:
238 kills, about 79 Salmon in all … Minotaur (level 27): 108 kills, about 202 Salmon in all …". Edible items a later quest needs are kept for it
and not counted as food.

## Fixed

- **A pickaxe or axe you cannot use yet no longer counts.** A Rune pickaxe at Mining 10 was taken as "you have a pickaxe" (in the app, in the
  game's lists and at the Below Ice Mountain entrance). Now the row says "Rune pickaxe (needs Mining 41): too high for your level yet".
- **Food at a dangerous entrance counts by healing.** "10 Trout" before the Ancient Guardian is 70 health: 8 Salmon are enough, 10 Cooked meat
  are not. The message says it in health.
- **A two-handed weapon with the Anti-dragon shield**: boarding for Elvarg with a 2h sword now says the shield cannot be worn with it.

## In the game

The verdict is at the top of the bank card ("Preparation needed — Bank: withdraw 13 Salmon …") and first under the guide's Advice tab. It is not
on the Steps tab, so it does not flicker while you eat during a fight.

Advice only: nothing is equipped, withdrawn, bought or eaten for you. Food numbers are estimates; no kit guarantees survival.
