# OSRS Path 2.76.0

- **Skills: combat training for your character, not the wiki's average.** Melee, Ranged and Magic methods are now timed by the same model as the fight plans, with your levels and the gear you wear: "≈ 5k–10k XP per hour for you". The time to the goal says "(for your levels and gear)". Without the game connected, the wiki's guide stays and is labelled as such.
- **Drops and costs count on the Skills page too.** The drop worth selling (Big bones at the frogs, cowhides at the cows) is counted at Grand Exchange prices after tax, but only where it pays for the pickups and bank trips, the same rule as the fight plans. Arrows broken and runes used are shown per hour. An account that cannot sell yet does not count what it cannot sell.
- **One answer in both places:** for the same character, the Skills page and the fight plan now pick the same spot (for example Giant frogs with their Big bones), and the path under the card no longer switches away from it a level later.
- New melee methods from the spot registry: Al Kharid warriors, barbarians, Minotaurs and Brutus (after The Ides of Milk). Adding a spot without its Skills method, or the reverse, now fails the tests.
- Magic: every Strike hits as hard as its tier allows (2, 4, 6, 8 at Magic 1, 5, 9, 13), and a spell landing for 0 does no damage.

No gameplay automation. Saves, route IDs and progress are unchanged.
