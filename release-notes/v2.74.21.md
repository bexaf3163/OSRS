# OSRS Path 2.74.21

The Training card on the Skills page no longer recommends a method you have outgrown.

## The fix

- **Melee:** on the relaxed priority the card kept "Chickens and cows" from level 20 to 60. On the balanced and fastest priorities it kept Giant frogs past 40, because the app could not see the food that Hill Giants need. Now Giant frogs run 20–40, then Hill Giants, Moss giants and Lesser demons. What to bring is shown on the card.
- The same mistake is gone for **Ranged** (seagulls at 60) and **Mining** (copper and tin at 90), and for every free skill. A new check guards all of them.
- A method you have outgrown still wins when its supplies are already in your bank and the new one needs buying first, so you can use up what you have.

## New methods

- **Ranged:** moss giants from a safespot from 57, the wiki's best free XP after Brutus.
- **Fishing:** fly fishing now runs to 99, as the wiki trains it. Lobsters and swordfish stay as the food and money options.
- **Members:**
  - Agility: the rooftop courses from Canifis to Ardougne.
  - Thieving: fruit stalls in Hosidius and Ardougne knights.
  - Fletching: maple, yew and magic bows.
  - Herblore: high potions from 60.
  - Farming: tree and fruit tree runs.
  - Mining: Motherlode Mine to 99.

No gameplay automation or route reordering. Saves, route IDs and progress are unchanged.
