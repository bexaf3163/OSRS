# OSRS Path 2.134.0

Two quests and a miniquest join the Members route, so the Members quest-point total is now 136:

- **Enter the Abyss** (miniquest, after Rune Mysteries). The guide covers the Mage of Zamorak in the Wilderness (bring nothing you would mind losing), then in Varrock. It also covers the scrying orb charged by Aubury, Sedridor and Wizard Cromperty, and it opens the Abyss for runecrafting.
- **Eadgar's Ruse** (Herblore 31, after Druidic Ritual and Troll Stronghold). The guide covers Parroty Pete's drunk parrot from vodka and pineapple chunks, hiding it in the Troll Stronghold prison, Tegid's dirty robe and Eadgar's fake man. It also covers the goutweed and Sanfew.
- **Tears of Guthix** (43 quest points, Firemaking 49, Crafting 20, Mining 20). The guide covers Juna in the Lumbridge Swamp Caves and the sapphire lantern, made from a bullseye lantern and a cut sapphire. It also covers the light creature, the magic stone and the stone bowl.

Verified before delivery: 2,663 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 77 Members-route quests finish), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
