# OSRS Path 2.87.0

- 2.86.1 was not published: its plugin tests failed because the in-game plaque, without its step code, passed an empty label to the font renderer. An empty label now takes no room and the plaque carries the NOW stamp alone. This release includes everything listed for 2.86.1.
- Dragon Slayer, Melzar's Maze: the Lesser demon is estimated the way the route fights it, with Water Strike from the safespot where it cannot reach you. The estimate counts your Magic level, the magic bonus of what you wear (metal armour lowers it) and the Wiki's 40% water weakness; you take no damage, and it checks that the step's 50 casts of runes are enough. Magic 5 is listed as needed during the quest.
- Below Ice Mountain: the Ancient Guardian gets the ordinary melee estimate (the Wiki says standard combat works; how long the pillars take to mine is not on the Wiki). Mining the four pillars stays in the route as the other way. A fresh account now plans through Below Ice Mountain: 22 of the 23 F2P quests in the turn-by-turn simulation.
- Magic training uses the same spell rule, so an opponent's elemental weakness counts there too. The fixed list of fights "done another way" is gone: both are data now.

Still open: Dragon Slayer stops at "Verify combat readiness". Its later fights must be ready before the quest starts, but the quest itself hands over the Maze key and the Anti-dragon shield. Remaining-time estimates are mostly unknown.

Verified before delivery: 2,633 unit tests, the 640 plugin tests (compiled here against the cached RuneLite jars, since the Maven mirror refused downloads) and 769 browser checks; typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Paired timing against 2.86.0: mean non-XP update script time −3% (12.8 → 12.4 ms), cold-start script time lower on every measured page. Native Electron checks and the isolated Portable EXE run in the release workflow. No real-game testing is claimed.
