**The in-game list is now driven by the same logic as Quest Helper: the step is determined by messages, dialogues, the quest journal, variables, items and place — not only by place and bag.**

The cause was a live session (S2-09 Pirate's Treasure): the player put the rum in, filled the crate, talked to Luthas and got 30 coins — and the list stayed on "Tell Luthas the crate is full". Place and bag cannot show that, while Quest Helper sees it from the chat line "Luthas hands you 30 coins.".

**What was done**

- From the Quest Helper sources (Zoinkwiz/quest-helper, master of 16.09.2026) it was extracted in which order and under which conditions it chooses a step — for all 32 quests of the route (`src/data/questMachines.json`, a plugin resource). Each of the 644 stage lines has a key `k` — the name of a Quest Helper step.
- The plugin reads the same things as Quest Helper: lines of the chat and of message windows ("You stash the rum in the crate."), dialogue lines, the quest journal, game variables, items (bag, worn, bank), the place, NPCs nearby — and chooses the same step. The "latch" conditions (done once — done for good) work as in Quest Helper.
- The machine's choice is evidence: when it exists, it sets the cursor, and the step no longer depends on where you ran. With no evidence (the plugin has just started, no messages yet, the "default" step) the earlier logic decides: place and items. A condition the plugin cannot check (the Quest Helper arrow on an NPC) counts as "unknown", not "no": the machine then does not decide.
- Open the quest journal — Quest Helper re-checks the step the same way after a restart; on S2-09 the list itself suggests this while the step is unclear.
- The "done" button stayed where the game cannot see the step by itself: if a message did not arrive, you will not get stuck. A pressed "done" is stronger than the machine: it will not move the cursor back.
- The "Steps like Quest Helper" setting (In-game helper) turns the machine off completely — then everything is as in 2.26.2.

**Checked**

- The plugin answers the same as the reference (Python, written from the Quest Helper sources) on 2700 checks across all quests; the S2-09 scenario from the boat to Redbeard Frank — on the real lines.
- The arrow tiles of all 526 lines for which Quest Helper gives a tile match its tiles (within 12 tiles); the NPC and object highlight matches in 499 of 502 lines. The three missing ones were added: Romeo (S2-05), General Khazard (S7-02), Hetty's cauldron (S2-03).

**The debug journal became more detailed** (the folder `~/.runelite/osrs-path-telemetry`, only on your computer; the settings "Log: chat and dialogues" and "Screenshot on every step" in the Developer section)

- Chat and dialogue messages, dialogue answer options, menu clicks, other game variables, the quest journal text and every machine choice are recorded (`qh`: the variable value, the Quest Helper step, the line, the path).
- A screenshot is taken on every stage and line change (up to 40 per session, no more than once per 6 s) — the engine state is recorded next to it.
- The developer plate (Ctrl+Shift+D) shows a "QH: …" line — what the machine chose and why.

**Not checked in the live game.** This version was not run in the game: it was checked by tests and by comparison with the sources. If a step did not move, send a screenshot or the journal — now it shows which messages arrived and what the machine chose.

**Known gaps**

- 30 Quest Helper steps (detours by stairs, repeated approaches to an NPC) are not in the stage lists; the machine does not decide on them, and the earlier logic chooses the line.
- 45 lines (of 1016 possible "stage — value" pairs) cannot be chosen by the machine: these are transition steps and "collect an item" steps without a Quest Helper step of their own.
- The highlight of items on the ground (for example the White apron at Gerrant) is not read yet.
