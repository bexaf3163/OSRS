# OSRS Path 2.137.0

Three more Members quests are on the route: Eagles' Peak, Ghosts Ahoy and The Forsaken Tower. Ghosts Ahoy plans the 31 ecto-tokens, the nettle tea and the bucket of slime (neither is sold on the Grand Exchange, so the app shows how to make them), three of each primary dye for the random flag, and food for the level 32 giant lobster.

Eagles' Peak brings Hunter into the route. Hunter 1 to 9 is the Natural History Quiz, then the Hunter methods take over, and a wielded noose wand or insect net no longer stops the next fight from being estimated.

Quest steps highlight more objects in the game, because the Quest Helper object list now includes ids from 32,000 up (105 lines in 11 quests gained a highlight).

Verified before delivery: 2,650 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 80 Members-route quests finish), the browser checks on every page, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
