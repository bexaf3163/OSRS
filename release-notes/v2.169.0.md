# OSRS Path 2.169.0

## Ogress Warriors for Ranged

Ogress Warriors in the Corsair Cove Dungeon are now a Ranged training target. You fight them from a safespot, so they cannot hit you. The method opens only once you have finished The Corsair Curse, because that quest opens the Corsair Cove bank. A kill is valued by the Wiki money guide's drop table (rune armour and weapons, law and nature runes, uncut gems) at current exchange prices, not only by its Big bones.

For a free-to-play account with 70 Ranged and the free quests done, the plan compares:

| Target | Ranged XP an hour | Coins an hour after arrows |
|---|---|---|
| Ogress Warriors | 7,800 to 28,600 | about 62,000 |
| Giant frogs | 11,600 to 25,400 | about 46,000 |

The planner still picks giant frogs, because their XP is steadier at the slow end and training is always balanced between XP and coins. Ogress Warriors are listed beside them, so you can choose the coins.

## Flesh Crawlers for melee

The level 28 Flesh Crawlers on the second floor of the Stronghold of Security are now a melee target for accounts with 40 Defence, as the Wiki's free-to-play melee guide suggests. They attack first, so you do not walk between kills. They drop nothing worth selling, so giant frogs, which pay with Big bones, stay the usual pick.

## Banks that need a quest

The Corsair Cove bank is never offered as "the nearest bank" for other steps, because it opens only after The Corsair Curse. Only the Ogress spot, which itself needs the quest, uses it.

When every free-to-play quest and checkpoint is done, "Next in F2P" now says that combat past 40 is optional and points to the Skills page.
