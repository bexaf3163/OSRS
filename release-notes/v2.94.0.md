# OSRS Path 2.94.0

- Earning guides explain the method now. Every free-to-play guide on the Money page is rebuilt from its OSRS Wiki guide's own method, not a generic outline. Its steps are the Wiki's numbered steps, or the instructions in its prose when it writes them as text. For example, Collecting ruby rings gives the emote order that opens the vault, then grab the ring, hop worlds, emotes again to leave, bank upstairs. Collecting garlic says to go upstairs in Morgan's house in Draynor Village, search the cupboard repeatedly and bank to the south. Under the summary, "how it works" gives the rest of the guide's text: where exactly, what to pick up and what to leave. Its tips follow the steps. 15 of 56 Wiki guides now have their own steps and 53 have their own description; the rest keep the outline, because their guide gives no method to read.
- The 24 hand-written guides keep their steps and gain their Wiki guide's description and tips (23 of them have one).
- Fixed what made guides confusing: a guide no longer asks you to bring what it makes ("Bring: Ruby Ring" on Collecting ruby rings), the summary no longer repeats the place ("…Varrock West Bank. Varrock West Bank"), and "Repeat steps 1–4" points at the right steps. Figures the Wiki computes on its page, which came out as holes ("about to per hour"), are left out.
- A guide stays in the list while its Wiki page exists, even when the Wiki's own list leaves it out because of today's prices. The app ranks methods by live prices itself. Collecting garlic, Catching shrimp and anchovies and Casting Bones to Bananas are kept for this reason.

Still open: the remaining colourful emoji are replaced by Ledger icons next.

Verified before delivery: 2,651 unit tests (new: the guide parser on a numbered method, on prose, on computed-figure leftovers and on an empty field), the route gate (23 of 23 quests, no check, 1 of 211 turns with an unknown time) and 787 browser checks (new: an opened guide lists its own steps and never asks to bring its product); typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates; the Electron desktop checks ran locally under a virtual display before 2.93.0's release. Guide data was rebuilt from the OSRS Wiki on 2026-11-12. One paired timing against 2.93.0: mean non-XP update script time −1% (14.0 → 14.0 ms), cold-start script time lower on every measured page (−1% to −9%). The plugin code is unchanged since 2.87.0 (its 640 tests passed then). Nothing was tested in the real game.
