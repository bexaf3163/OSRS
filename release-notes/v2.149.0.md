# OSRS Path 2.149.0

A Porcine of Interest, Tai Bwo Wannai Trio and Shadow of the Storm are on the route.

A Porcine of Interest plans a rope, a knife and 4 trout for Sourhog (level 37). Spria's reinforced goggles keep its hits at 6; without them it hits 20 to 30, so the route tells you to equip them first.

Tai Bwo Wannai Trio plans the brothers' gifts: banana rum from Zembo's Karamjan rum, burnt and marinated jogre bones (logs, a tinderbox and karambwanji paste), a karambwan-poisoned iron spear (a raw karambwan, a pestle and mortar), an agility potion, seaweed, and air and mind runes for Wind Strike on the monkey, which melee cannot damage. Its in-bag steps now highlight the item to use.

Shadow of the Storm plans a silver bar for the demonic sigil, a Shantay pass, 10 lobsters for Agrith Naar (level 100, hits up to 10) and a black outfit that does not touch your combat gear: a black cape, plus a desert shirt and robe dyed with the quest's black mushrooms (2 vials and a pestle and mortar). The planner fights the demon with Silverlight, which deals the final blow, and now knows you keep Silverlight from Demon Slayer.

Demon Slayer has a reviewed item flow: 25 bones (handed in a few at a time), a bucket of water and a coin go in, and Silverlight comes out.

The item list now carries every dose size of a potion a step needs, such as Agility potion(1) to (4). Doses are counted across all four sizes, so before this a required potion's stock stayed unknown and the planner stopped to ask you to check your bank. A missing potion is now priced, placed and bought as whole potions of the named size through the same sources as other items, so its trip is timed; before, a step that needed a potion it could not make stopped on "Check the offer". Shop places written "ground floor[UK]" in lower case now read as "ground floor".

Cold War stays off the route for now, because it needs a crafting table in a player-owned house.

Verified before delivery: 2,676 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 110 Members-route quests finish in 881 turns), the browser checks on every page, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
