**The in-game list is now two calm rows, and opens in full only when you point at it.**

- **Strict two-row card (new setting "Compact list", on by default):** row one is the step code and the stage ("S2-10 · Stage 3 of 7") with the progress bar and the readiness percent; row two is the step you are on, for example "▶ [2/6] Dye the wig yellow (Aggie)". The recipe in brackets ("(2 onions + 5 gp)"), the text after a semicolon ("; buy rope too") and everything else is left out of the closed card. Only who or what to use stays in brackets.
- **Opens under the mouse:** while the mouse is over the card it opens downward in a quick reveal, and shows the recipe of the step, the tab strip, the next steps, "Done - next" and "Back", "In your bag: N of M items", the items you still need and where to go. It stays open for about a third of a second after the mouse leaves, so the edge of the card never makes it blink.
- **Nothing is lost:** the open card contains everything the full list had; it only waits for the mouse. The heading and the step row do not move when it opens. Buttons work exactly as before.
- **Warnings stay visible:** a warning about the step, or the recovery mode after a death, is shown in the closed card as an extra amber row.
- **Long step names:** at the real card width a step can need a second text line, so the step row may wrap to two lines rather than cut off the name of who to talk to.
- **Old behaviour:** turn "Compact list" off to see the whole list all the time. The docked bar (2.34) keeps its own rule: it opens the full list itself.

**Checked.** App tests, data checks, the production build, the browser interface checks and all plugin tests pass, including new tests for the closed and open card and a check that no text sticks out of the frame in any game font, width or size.
