# OSRS Path 2.52.0 — travel shortcuts that show up in the game

The fastest way to the current objective is now on the game card itself, and a teleport you cannot cast yet says exactly what to get and where.

- **On the main card:** one travel line above the tabs. Green: a way to use now ("➜ Use Chronicle → Champions' Guild · saves ~36 s · check charges"). Amber: what to get first ("Varrock Teleport saves ~32 s: withdraw Law rune, Air rune ×3, Fire rune at Draynor Bank"). A click frames the item in the bag or points the arrow at the bank or shop. The app never uses, buys or withdraws anything.
- **Preparation:** a spell, canoe or boat that lacks runes, an axe or coins is offered with the stop that fixes it: withdraw at the nearest bank when the bank is known to hold enough, otherwise buy where the item data says (the shop for your game mode, else the Grand Exchange). Only the remaining quantity is asked for, and the stop time is counted before the way is called faster.
- **Bank counts of runes, the Chronicle and axes** are now reported by the plugin, so "withdraw" is said only for what the bank really holds. An unread bank stays unknown, never "empty".
- **The objective** is the quest line the game confirms, not a temporary arrow; a shop or bank stop never replaces it.
- **Home Teleport:** its 11–15 s cast counts in the trip, and an unknown cooldown is "check the cooldown", not "ready". **Spellbook:** the standard teleports are not offered on another spellbook.
- **Free-to-play:** teleport tablets (members items) are no longer suggested. **Chronicle:** bought at Diango's Toy Store, not the Grand Exchange (it is not tradeable).
- **Dungeons:** a position or objective underground is no longer routed over as if on the surface.
- **The arrow follows the step:** after switching steps the old step's arrow and distance no longer stay on the card.
- **Card drawing:** a card that just grew no longer draws its last rows outside its background for a frame.
- **For developers:** `localStorage['osrs-put:debug-transport'] = '1'` logs every candidate with its verdict, journey, saving and what it lacks (bag and bank counts).
- **Checked:** `tsc`, build, 946 Vitest tests (new: `tests/transportLive.test.ts`), `check-data` (0 errors, 0 warnings), the UI smoke test, the full Java suite (new tests in `TransportChipTest`, `DetourRowTest`, `ModernGuideTest`), and live on World 316 — see `docs/transport-audit-2.52.md` for what was live and what was simulated.

Restart RuneLite through OSRS Path after updating.
