# OSRS Path 2.57.0

## Food on the training plate

While you fight on a combat training step, the top plate now shows the food in your bag and about how many kills it lasts
("Food: 8 Salmon · ~21 kills"), worked out from your own food plan. With 2 pieces or fewer it turns into a red alert ("bank soon"), and with
none left it says "bank now". The departure check ("what to bring") still waits until you stop fighting.

## Fixed (found in the 2.56.0 review)

- The step's preparation card and "What you need" showed the food for the whole goal (for example **Salmon ×110 Missing**) instead of the
  next trip. They now ask for one bag (**Salmon ×26**) and say "13 of 26" rather than "missing" when part of it is in the bank. Only the Grand
  Exchange shopping list buys for the whole goal.
- The food row says both numbers at once: "have 13, 13 short; buy about 97 for the whole goal", so one trip to the exchange is enough.
- On the Minotaur and Hill Giant steps the Preparation view no longer suggests training at another place (it named the Al Kharid warriors): the step
  itself is the method.

## Simpler

- In Preparation, the item cards with where to get each item are now behind **Where to get each item**: "What you need" above already lists every
  item and its state once.
- The S3-08 and S4-03 walkthroughs say "fill the bag with Salmon" instead of a fixed 20 or 25, matching the food plan.
