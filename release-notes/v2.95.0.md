# OSRS Path 2.95.0

- Druidic Ritual is on the Members route (S7-06). You start at Kaqemeex in the stone circle north of Taverley, bring raw bear meat, rat meat, beef and chicken, and get 4 quest points and 250 Herblore XP. The plugin follows its 4 stages like the other quests. It is the first quest built by the new quest pipeline: drafted from Quest Helper and the Wiki, with every tile, id, item and quest point checked against those sources before it shipped.
- All 23 free-to-play quests were checked again against the newest Quest Helper and the Wiki. Only Cook's Assistant changed: the plugin now also recognises the second Bucket of milk item id. The Wiki item lists of the other quests hold no errors.
- The game renamed metal swords to shortswords, so "Rune sword" is now "Rune shortsword" in the Dragon Slayer steps, the gear table and the plugin's highlights. Before, the item data could not be rebuilt and the Rune shortsword was not counted as a weapon you keep.
- Shops on upper floors read cleanly again. The Wiki started wrapping floor names in HTML, so some shops showed "1st floor[UK]2nd floor[US] of Champions' Guild".
- The item data is rebuilt from today's Wiki. The Lumbridge General Store now sells a Bronze pickaxe and hammers spawn in Lumbridge, so a fresh account picks those up nearby. Teleport card, Chronicle, Staff and Red dye stay in the data for the travel and recipe planners.
- When a quest fight's kit is still being bought and your levels cannot win it yet, the plan now counts the training to a winning setup. Before, the quest showed "time unknown" until the training was planned. On a fresh account no turn has an unknown time now.

Verified before delivery: 2,622 unit tests (new: the Wiki's HTML floors, one short name per instruction in a quest draft, a weak character's fight preparation is priced with training), the route gate (23 of 23 quests, no check, 0 of 211 turns with an unknown time), the browser and Electron desktop checks, 640 plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures and structure gates. Two paired timings against 2.94.5, in both orders: cold-start script time -4% to +2% on Path, Shopping and the Ranged skill page; page switches -11% to +9%. Nothing was tested in the real game.
