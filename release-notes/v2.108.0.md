# OSRS Path 2.108.0

- New Members quest: Merlin's Crystal (6 QP, 1,000 Firemaking XP). It joins the early Members stage of a Members account's route and never shows in free-to-play mode. Every stage is on the card, from King Arthur and Keep Le Faye to the black candle, Excalibur and the crystal. Sir Mordred (level 39) is estimated with your kit, and Trout x5 (or similar food) is a required item. Bat bones are bought, so no giant bat has to be killed.
- New Members quest: The Dig Site (2 QP, 15,300 Mining XP, 2,000 Herblore XP; needs Agility 10, Herblore 10 and Thieving 25). Every stage is on the card, from the three exams to the explosive and the stone tablet. The brush, jar, panning tray and trowel come from the quest itself.
- Thieving 1 to 5 by pickpocketing men now has a rate, derived from the Wiki (8 XP a pickpocket, about 70% success and the stun time), so Thieving goals are timed from level 1.
- Herblore potion training now has a place (the Grand Exchange), so a Herblore goal above the quest XP is planned and timed instead of waiting on a check.

Verified before delivery: 2,638 unit tests, the F2P route gate (23 of 23 quests, 0 of 211 turns with an unknown time), the Members route gate (47 of 47 quests, no check, 6 of 367 turns with an unknown time), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
