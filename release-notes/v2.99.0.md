# OSRS Path 2.99.0

- Six Members quests can now be planned to the end. A fresh Members account in the route gate finishes 34 of 35 quests, up from 28:
  - Fight Arena, Tree Gnome Village and The Grand Tree: the bosses (Khazard Ogre, Khazard Scorpion, Bouncer, Khazard warlord, the Black demon) are fought from the safespots the quest guides name, with Fire Bolt. The app now trains Magic to 35 for them, with any Magic method, because nothing reaches you on a safespot. The Staff of fire, Chaos and Air runes are required items of those steps now, since the fight cannot go on without them, and a staff in the step's kit counts as endless fire runes.
  - Lost City: the Tree spirit is fought with Fire Strike from between the mushrooms, as Entrana allows no weapons or armour. The Dramen staff you make in Lost City is kept for Fairytale I.
  - Fairytale I: the Tanglefoot can only be hurt by the Magic secateurs, which the quest hands you; they are in the gear data now as quest kit, never advised as a purchase.
  - Waterfall Quest: the route line now says to go left and avoid the fire giants, in the words of the Wiki quick guide, so the quest's "evade" requirement counts as met.
- Monkey Madness I is the one Members quest left: its Jungle Demon is fought with Protect from Magic, and prayers are not modelled yet.
- The Members known debt is lowered from 7 open quests to 1.

Verified before delivery: 2,625 unit tests (new: a safespot spell fight trains Magic to the spell), the F2P route gate (23 of 23 quests, no check, 0 of 211 turns with an unknown time) and the Members route gate (34 of 35, within its debt of 1), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
