# OSRS Path 2.141.0

Two more Members quests are on the route: Biohazard and Haunted Mine.

Biohazard plans the 30 coins for Captain Barnaby's ship to Rimmington and 2 trout for the level 13 mourner who holds the key. Haunted Mine plans the chisel for the crystal and 15 lobsters for Treus Dayth, who has no safespot.

Fights against monsters that attack with ranged are now estimated, because the planner reads their Ranged level and ranged bonus instead of treating them as unknown.

The plugin now sends up to 60 lines per quest stage, the same limit it reads, so the last lines of long quests such as Murder Mystery are no longer cut off.

Verified before delivery: 2,658 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 92 Members-route quests finish in 771 turns), the browser checks on every page, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
