# OSRS Path 2.181.0

## Most coins still trains the skill

With the Most coins goal, a skill's plan picked whatever earned the most coins, even when it hardly trained the skill. At Ranged 15 it sent you to ogress warriors for their drops at 663 to 3,400 XP an hour, while ordinary methods give 5,000 to 12,000.

Most coins now chooses only among methods that are at least a third as fast as the fastest method open to you that does not lose coins. Among those it still takes the one that earns the most. The Train card and the plan use the same rule. Fastest XP and Balanced are unchanged.

Verified before delivery: 2,870 unit tests, the free-to-play route gate, all five parts of the Members route gate, the browser checks, typecheck and build, structure, unused-code, dead-CSS and data checks. Nothing was tested in the real game.
