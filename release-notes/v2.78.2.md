# OSRS Path 2.78.2

A better tool is weighed against its price.

- **The Skills card says when a better axe or pickaxe pays for itself.** For the time left to your goal it works out how much faster each better tool you could buy would be, at the same spot. On Balanced it is suggested only when the hours saved are worth more than its price. On Fastest it is the biggest time saver, with its price shown. On Relaxed nothing is suggested. Example: "a Rune pickaxe (≈ 19k gp) saves ≈ 7 h".
- Only tools your account can use and you don't already own are offered, and only when their price is known (Grand Exchange, or a shop you can use).
- **Mines under the ground.** The Dwarven Mine iron and coal rocks are now in the gathering data (from the wiki), with the ladder in. The Money page times its Dwarven Mine methods with the same model as the Skills page, the bank trip goes through the ladder, and the in-game path leads down it. The Barbarian Village coal rocks were added too.

No gameplay automation. Saves, route IDs, adopted plans and progress are unchanged.
