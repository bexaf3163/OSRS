**Two multi-hour grinds are gone from the free-to-play route, and Wormbrain is simply paid.**

- **S3-09 (Magic 33 for Telekinetic Grab) is removed.** S5-05 now pays Wormbrain the 10,000 gp in the dialogue: no spell, no fight, no runes. The step asks for 10,000 coins, so the readiness check and the shopping list count them. Magic stays at 25 (Varrock Teleport) until stage 6, and the stage 5 advice no longer recommends Magic 33. The bribe is meant to come from the stage 4 loot (moss giant bones and nature runes, S4-03); there is no separate money step for it.
- **S2-13 (Fishing and Cooking to 30) is removed.** No free-to-play quest needs those levels. The fly fishing rod and 500 feathers are off the big shopping list (S2-01, about 1,600 gp less), and the Salmon for S4-01, S4-02 and S4-03 is bought on the Grand Exchange. The Fishing and Cooking goals are 1 for stages 2 to 5.
- **S3-06 follows Shield of Arrav (S3-05).** It already ended on any of 2,500 coins, 25 iron ore or the Adamant scimitar; the four stage 3 quests pay 5,280 coins, so the mining is bypassed when the money is there. The route now requires S3-05 explicitly.
- **S4-03 is Attack 40 alone** (it unlocks the Rune scimitar); the Strength 35 gate is gone. Strength goals: 20-25 through stage 4, 35-36 in stage 5 with the Dragon Slayer I reward.
