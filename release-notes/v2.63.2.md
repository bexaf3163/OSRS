# OSRS Path 2.63.2

## Fixes from the live check

- **Inventory cleanup keeps what an open quest still needs.** The Ogre artefact you carry for Chief Tess was listed as "untradeable, no use
  ahead", both on the Shopping page and on the guide's Advice tab in the game. An item that an open quest's own steps check for is now kept:
  "A quest item: The Corsair Curse checks for it, and that quest is still open."
- **The Preparation view's "Bag cleanup" and the weight advice keep them too.** They suggested banking the Ogre artefact, the Tinderbox, the
  Spade and the Strength potion while The Corsair Curse still needs them; potions are now never listed, like food.
- **"Dropped by Scurrius#non-MVP"** now reads "Dropped by Scurrius"; the item drawer shows the drop table as "Scurrius (non-MVP)".
- **Quest points come from the game while it is connected.** The app counted 41 because the optional Shield of Arrav is marked done here; the
  game counts 40, since that quest cannot be finished alone. The journal now says "not finished in the game" on such a quest.
- **"Where" in the middle of a quest starts at the game's current target**, such as "Now: Go down to Tess (Hole)", instead of where the quest
  began. "Lead here in the game" no longer sends the arrow back to Port Sarim.
- **Quest overview:** a stage that repeats the line before it word for word is now one line ("Talk to Captain Tock" was listed twice in a row).

## Checked live

The app was updated to 2.63.1 with the release file (size and checksum checked), and RuneLite was restarted through the app. The 2.63.1
plugin answered, the character logged in on World 316, and these all worked:

- the guide panel, its way-down line ("Cross Gangplank") and the arrow target;
- the Advice tab: the readiness line and the cleanup rows;
- every page of the app with the live data.

RuneLite's logs show no errors from the plugin.
