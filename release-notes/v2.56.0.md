# OSRS Path 2.56.0

## Food and time, worked out for you

On a combat training step (Minotaurs on S3-08, Hill Giants on S4-03) the food block now plans from your own levels, XP and worn gear:

- **Next trip**: how many pieces to take now (one bag).
- **Whole goal**: how many the rest of the step needs, as a range.
- **What you have**: your bag and bank, other fish counted by healing (3 Lobsters are 4 Salmon), and **how many to buy**. Without the bank seen,
  no number is guessed.
- The time, trips and kills left, and whether this is a formula estimate or a rough one.

Behind **Details**: Strength and Attack phases, how long a bag lasts, the level 27 Minotaurs side by side with the level 12 ones, and whether
your Strength potions actually help with your gear (with the Mithril scimitar alone at these levels they do not raise your max hit; with the
Amulet of strength they do).

## It learns from your fights

The RuneLite plugin now records your recent kills on a combat training step — per monster version, so level 12 and level 27 Minotaurs are kept
apart — with the time between kills and the damage you took. After 10 clean kills the estimate is pulled towards what really happens, a little
more with every kill (bank trips and breaks are left out, a new weapon starts over). The plan then follows the Minotaurs you actually fight.

## Readiness and shopping use the plan

- The step's food check asks for one bag (the next trip), not the old fixed 20 or 25.
- The Grand Exchange shopping list buys the food for the whole goal, so one trip to the Exchange is enough.
- A later step (Hill Giants) is planned from the levels the earlier steps will reach and the weapon it asks for.
- Without the game linked, everything stays as written in the route.

The numbers come from the OSRS Wiki combat formulas and each monster version's own stats, and are tested against a separate dice-by-dice
simulation of the fights.
