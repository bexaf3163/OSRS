**A cockpit step view on the desktop: what to do now first, everything else folded.**

- **Hero card:** when RuneLite is linked, the step view opens with only the sub-step you are on: "Step 4 of 6", its title and action, the exact dialogue options to pick, and a "Lead to Target in Game" button that points the arrow at it. The sub-step is found from the target the game's list already follows; with no link, another step, or a detour (a purchase, a bank trip), the card is simply not shown and the old layout stays.
- **Items strip:** only the items that sub-step asks for, as small cards with In Bag, In Bank, Missing or Not checked. An item is Missing only when both the bag and the bank are known; a bank that was not opened says Not checked, never Missing.
- **Folded away:** "Future Preparation" (the shopping and look-ahead for the next steps), "Full Walkthrough Reference" (the whole text), and in Inspector mode the raw item tables, fields and images ("Items, details and fields"). Each opens on a click and costs nothing while closed. Warnings, "Done when", the Done button, level targets and the readiness status stay in view.
- **Dossier column:** with nothing chosen it shows the NPC of the current sub-step (or the step's own NPC) under "Your current target", with its place, floor and wiki link. If there is no NPC to show, the column folds away and the step takes its width. Choosing something yourself still works as before.

**Checked.** App tests (new tests for the sub-step matching, dialogue parsing and item status), data checks, the production build, the browser interface checks (new checks for the hero, the folds and the dossier, at phone and wide widths) and all plugin tests pass. The one change to an existing check: the "one trip" test now opens "Future Preparation" first, as a player would.
