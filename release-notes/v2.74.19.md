# OSRS Path 2.74.19

More ways to train and to earn, from the OSRS Wiki's free-to-play guides.

## Money

- **Kill Giant frogs for Big bones.** The level 13 frogs in Lumbridge Swamp drop Big bones on every kill and hit for at most 2. The pace comes from the app's combat model for your gear, and the price from the live exchange.
- **Shop runs:** jug packs from Fortunato's Wine Shop in Draynor, and kebabs from Karim in Al Kharid (the toll counts until Prince Ali Rescue is done).
- **Processing at a bank or furnace:** filling jugs at the Falador waterpump, pizza bases, uncooked berry, meat and apple pies, silver bars, chocolate dust (with a knife), chocolate cakes and anchovy pizzas.

Every new method reads both sides of the trade from the live market and drops out when it does not pay. Methods whose product changes with the market (the Rising Sun drinks, pastry dough, dyes, jewellery) are still left out, each with its reason in the coverage list.

## Skills

- **Melee from 20 to 99:** Giant frogs (20–40), Hill Giants (40–60), Moss giants (60–80) and Lesser demons (80+), plus the Edgeville monks (1–20, free healing). Before, the free list stopped at "Chickens and cows" at 20.
- **Woodcutting past 60:** willows continue to 99, as the wiki guide trains them, and yews from 60 are listed as the profit choice.
- **Smithing:** mithril platebodies (68–88), adamant platebodies (88+), bronze items for 1–18, and The Knight's Sword as the quest that covers 1–29.
- **Crafting past 43:** cutting diamonds.

## The compact view

- The Now tab no longer repeats the starting line under it, and its list is titled "Next".
- The Bag tab shows the readiness verdict once, and no level label repeats the verdict's own first words.

No gameplay automation or route reordering. Saves, route IDs and progress are unchanged.
