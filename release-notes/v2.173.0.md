# OSRS Path 2.173.0

## Members fights from the Wiki's training guides

Members combat plans used only one Members-only fight, the Gemstone Crab from 30 to 55. They now follow the Wiki's Members guides when you have the quests.

| Style | Method | Levels | XP an hour, from the Wiki | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Melee | Sulphur Naguas in Neypotzli | 55 to 75 | 75,000 to 130,000 | Perilous Moons, 48 Slayer, 43 Prayer, Sulphur blades |
| Melee | Maniacal monkeys with Hallowfell | 75 to 99 | 200,000 to 300,000 | Monkey Madness II, 43 Prayer, Hallowfell |
| Melee | Gemstone Crab, now to 99 | 30 to 99 | 50,000 | Children of the Sun |
| Ranged | Chinning maniacal monkeys, 1,866 chinchompas an hour | 45 to 99 | 290,000 to 470,000 | Monkey Madness II, 43 Prayer |
| Magic | Ice Burst on maniacal monkeys, 1,085 casts an hour | 70 to 99 | 250,000 to 310,000 | Desert Treasure I, Monkey Madness II, 43 Prayer |

With those quests, a Members melee plan from 1 to 99 now runs cows, Brutus, the Gemstone Crab, Sulphur Naguas and Maniacal monkeys, in 51 to 77 hours.

Chinning and Ice Burst are the fastest Ranged and Magic XP, but at today's prices they lose about 825,000 coins an hour or more. The plus-or-zero rule therefore lists them under other methods and keeps the plan on methods that earn or break even.

## The Chaos altar for Prayer

The Chaos altar in level 38 Wilderness gives the same 350% XP as a gilded altar, and half the offerings are not used up. The Wiki's pace is 2,000 offerings an hour from 1,000 bones, plus 50 coins per bone to un-note. Each bone therefore gives twice the gilded altar's XP. The altar is listed beside the gilded altar, and the gilded altar stays first because the Chaos Temple is risky with player killers around.
