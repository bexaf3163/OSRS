# OSRS Path 2.139.0

Seven more Members quests are on the route. 2.138.0 did not publish, so this release carries its four quests too: Making History, Murder Mystery, Sleeping Giants and Death to the Dorgeshuun, plus Watchtower, Enakhra's Lament and Elemental Workshop II.

Watchtower plans the coins, gold bar and death rune for the ogre guards, the dragon bones for Toban, two ropes, the parts of the magic ogre potion and food for Gorad (level 68). The blue dragons of the enclave are walked past. Enakhra's Lament plans 52kg of sandstone in 10kg pieces, the granite, the brazier fuels and the runes for Fire Bolt, Wind Bolt and Crumble Undead. Elemental Workshop II plans 8 coal for the ore of two earth elementals.

Death to the Dorgeshuun plans two full H.A.M. sets, a closed lantern for the swamp gas and food for the guards and Sigmund. Sleeping Giants plans the foundry repairs, and the plugin knows the cave into the Giants' Foundry. Murder Mystery follows every suspect, upstairs included.

Verified before delivery: 2,653 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 87 Members-route quests finish in 724 turns), the browser checks on every page, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
