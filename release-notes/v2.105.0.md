# OSRS Path 2.105.0

- Three new Members quests: Monk's Friend (1 QP), Gertrude's Cat (1 QP, 1,525 Cooking XP) and Fishing Contest (1 QP, Fishing 10). They join the early Members stage of a Members account's route and never show in free-to-play mode. Their stages, tiles and texts come from Quest Helper and the Wiki through the quest pipeline. Where Quest Helper gave no text, the lines were written from the Wiki quick guides.
- Monk's Friend: the route says to run past the thieves under the stone circle, which proves the quest's "run past" requirement, the same way as Waterfall's fire giants.
- Gertrude's Cat: the seasoned sardine is made from a raw sardine and doogle leaves (picked behind Gertrude's house), not bought for 500 coins. Fishing Contest: the red vine worms are dug up during the quest.
- Much better time estimates for Members accounts. A later quest no longer loses its time because an earlier one is not done yet: what an unfinished prerequisite gives or leaves held (Lost City's Dramen staff, The Restless Ghost's Ghostspeak amulet) is planned as carried. Wearable gear and quest-given items also survive the app's unknown-consumption steps (fights, training). Turns with an unknown remaining time in the Members route gate dropped from 82 to 42.

Verified before delivery: 2,635 unit tests (new: what unfinished prerequisites leave held, items a quest ends holding, quest-given items kept through unmodelled consumption), the F2P route gate (23 of 23 quests, 0 of 211 turns with an unknown time), the Members route gate (42 of 42 quests, no check, 42 of 332 turns with an unknown time), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
