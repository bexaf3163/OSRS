# OSRS Path 2.93.0

- The app scales with its window. Text, spacing and layout grow together as the window gets bigger, like zooming a browser page, so nothing gets cut off. Up to 1300 × 800 it stays at the design size; above that it follows the tighter of width and height, up to 1.75×. A typical 1920 × 1080 window shows everything about 1.3× larger.
- Shield of Arrav no longer keeps the game on "Verify the current stage of Shield of Arrav". Seen live: every other F2P quest done, Shield of Arrav started in the game, and its route step marked done in the app. The app never sends a closed step to the game, so the plugin could not read the stage, and the check stayed forever. An optional quest whose route step you closed (done or skipped), and that no unfinished quest needs, is now set aside: it is no longer an objective, the Path's left page says every F2P quest is done, and the right page shows "Next in F2P". Remove the mark on its step, or choose it as your goal, and it is planned again.
- Settings keeps only RuneLite and Progress and copies. The switches the engines make redundant are always on now and gone from Settings: places on the map, bank highlighting, training pace, levels from the game, upgrade advice and auto preparation (clearing the arrow in the game still pauses a preparation trip). "Keep Defence at 1" moved under the Skills grid. The method priority (XP, Balanced, Money) stays on each skill's card, which already set the same preference. The update check is always automatic, and nothing installs without your button.
- The app version and its update button sit at the foot of the left rail: "Check", or "Restart to update" in gold when a new version is downloaded. The App section of Settings is gone.
- Ledger look throughout: the update notice and the bank reminder are Ledger notices with the Ledger's buttons (before: plain white or blue buttons, and the bank reminder's button spanned the page). "Next in F2P" is a list of rows (skill icon, levels, method and time, "Worth it" or "Optional", a Follow button) instead of a plain list of links. The Journal's right page shows the chosen quest alone: its state in plain words ("Completed", "Marked done in your route, but the game does not count it as finished…"), what it needs, and its route. Session statistics stay in Settings → Progress and copies. Inner scroll areas such as the Quest overview use the Ledger's thin gold scrollbar instead of the system's grey one. The "Where" map is a framed plate, and its buttons use line icons instead of emoji. Buttons that are links no longer show a red link underline.

Still open: the remaining colourful emoji elsewhere in the app are replaced by Ledger icons next, and every earning guide on the Money page is rewritten into clear steps.

Verified before delivery: 2,649 unit tests (including the window zoom factor and a closed Shield of Arrav being set aside), the route gate (23 of 23 quests, no check, 1 of 211 turns with an unknown time) and 786 browser checks (new: the update and bank notices, Shield of Arrav open, skipped and marked done, Settings with two sections, the version control on the rail); typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. The paired timing comparison against 2.92.1 was not completed for this release (stopped after one of eight runs to deliver it now). The plugin code is unchanged since 2.87.0 (its 640 tests passed then). The window scaling runs only in the desktop app: its factor is unit-tested, and the Electron checks run in the release workflow. Nothing was tested in the real game.
