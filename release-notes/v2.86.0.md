# OSRS Path 2.86.0

- Fight preparation ranks options by what is known. An option whose training or travel time is unknown no longer wins by counting those parts as zero: something you can do now comes before a check, then the least unmeasured training, then the fewest unknown parts. The Corsair Curse no longer answers "Train Strength to 78".
- A weak character is offered even melee training (lowest skill first) and the kit those levels unlock, beside the single-skill options; Defence-at-1 is kept.
- Count Draynor, Delrith, Brutus and Elvarg are estimated from the OSRS Wiki's fight mechanics (new data file `fightMechanics.json`): Draynor's regeneration, garlic and stake-and-hammer finish; Delrith with Silverlight's doubled damage after the three dark wizards; Brutus's specials; Elvarg's dragonfire behind the Anti-dragon shield (10) and without it (70). The planner now gives levels, kit and food for these fights instead of "Review the combat strategy" or "Verify combat readiness". The shield's slot stays reserved in every option. Dragon Slayer as a whole still asks for a check while the Melzar's Maze safespot (magic) is not modelled.
- Demon Slayer's food is a required item of the fight loadout, taken after Traiborn's bones.
- Better tools at their unlock: an axe or pickaxe tier is weighed over all the XP it will serve until the next tier, and the trip to buy it is the walk from your training spot to the Grand Exchange. At Woodcutting 31 with a Steel axe the next action is now "Get Adamant axe"; the Rune axe at 41 and better pickaxes follow the same rule. A Mithril-to-Adamant step (about 2 minutes saved after the walk) is not advised.
- Gear data adds Silverlight and the Anti-dragon shield from the Wiki as quest kit (never advised as a purchase); the opponent table adds the dark wizards.

Verified before delivery: 2,625 unit tests and 765 browser checks; typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code, dead-CSS and Wiki-link gates. Paired timing against 2.85.2: mean non-XP update script time +9% (10.7 → 11.7 ms), cold-start script time at most +9%, within the 10% budget. The Java plugin tests, native Electron checks and the isolated Portable EXE run in the release workflow; they could not run in the development container. The browser-check job runs on Ubuntu 24.04: Playwright 1.56 cannot install Chromium on the new Ubuntu 26.04 `ubuntu-latest` image. No real-game testing is claimed.
