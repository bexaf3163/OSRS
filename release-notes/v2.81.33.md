# OSRS Path 2.81.33

- Skills → Plan now lists the training plan as stages: the levels, the place, the rate and what is new when each stage starts (the next pickaxe or axe, a stronger spell, new supplies). The rate of a later stage is worked out with the tool or spell that stage recommends.
- On a free account the Plan ends with the F2P stop and what membership offers from that level.
- Magic plans climb from strikes through the F2P bolts (17–35) and blasts (41–59) instead of Wind Strike to 55. Spell levels, XP and runes come from the OSRS Wiki.
- Mining, checked against the OSRS Wiki: iron rates rise with the pickaxe (about 50,000 XP/h at 41 with a rune pickaxe); the Motherlode rate is no longer guessed; granite is listed for members; the F2P stop at 45 names the members methods with their rates.
- Woodcutting keeps oaks until 60 when logs are dropped, as the Wiki advises, instead of switching to willows.
