# OSRS Path 2.184.0

Fixes from a live session: crafting diamond necklaces at Crafting 57, then switching the followed skill and mining at 18.

## Training goals count from your exact XP

While you crafted toward Crafting 70, the Shopping list asked for more gold bars after every necklace instead of fewer. The followed skill's goal was counted from the first XP of your level, 5,916 necklaces from the start of level 57, instead of your real 209,265 XP, which needs 5,871. Each necklace then used a gold bar you held while the goal stayed the same until the next level, so the number to buy grew.

The followed skill, and the F2P and Members skill lists, now count from the exact XP the game reports, so the goal shrinks with every action. This applies to every skill in both modes.

## A plan buys the tool its stage plans

With no pickaxe, a mining plan at Mining 18 asked for a bronze pickaxe, while the same plan on the Skills page planned a steel one. A plan with no tool owned now rates and buys the tier its stage plans: steel at Mining 18, rune at 41, adamant axe at Woodcutting 36. Axes and pickaxes, F2P and Members alike.

## No leftover detour after switching skills

After following Prayer and switching back to Crafting, the game card kept saying "Buy Vile ashes". The buying detour is found by a walk check that restarts on every move, so while you kept walking the old one stayed. A detour whose items are not in the current plan is no longer sent to the game.

## Runite mining needs Mining 85

The Money list treated "Mining runite ore (free-to-play)" as doable at Mining 18. The Wiki lists "Mining 85, Attack 40 (optional), Defence 40 (optional), Magic 25 (optional)" on one line, and the reader dropped the whole line because some of it was optional. It now drops only the skills marked optional.
