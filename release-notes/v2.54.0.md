# OSRS Path 2.54.0 — new-account sale restrictions, a funding plan, step tasks

From the audit backlog; every item was checked against the OSRS Wiki and the current data first.

- **New-account sale restrictions.** A new free-to-play account cannot sell cowhides, leather, iron ore and 37 other items on the Grand Exchange until it has 20 hours of play, 10 quest points and total level 100. The route sells cowhides (S1-13, S2-01) and iron ore (S3-06). The plugin now reports your quest points and total level; with them the steps say whether the sale can fail:
  - **restricted** (a number is below the limit): a banner on the step, the money goal drops to a no-sale figure (8,000 gp on S1-13), loot that cannot be sold is not counted, and hide-based money methods are listed apart;
  - **unknown** (both numbers met): one line, because the hours of play cannot be read; "I can already sell these" ends it;
  - S2-01 shows what to buy with your coins in priority order: quest materials, then the Imp Catcher beads and the first scimitars, then the mithril scimitar, the amulet of strength and the energy potions.
- **Step tasks.** What the game's completion check does not measure is now tracked beside the step and never blocks it: wield the Mithril scimitar (S2-08), take the Cowbell amulet from Gillie (S4-02), the Museum quiz and A Porcine of Interest (+1 QP, S8-04), wear Ava's device (S8-05), Attack 60 and wielding the Dragon scimitar (S9-04). The stage 8 and 9 quest point range now includes A Porcine of Interest (59–61, 68–70).
- **Route export** prints the real completion condition (quests that also wait for the item they must keep) and the tasks.
- **Plugin:** `/status` carries `questPoints` and `totalLevel` (additive, no protocol change; an older plugin simply lacks them, and the app then says "unknown").
- **Checked:** `tsc`, build, 1000 Vitest tests, `check-data`, the full Java suite (491), UI smoke. Not tested in game yet.
- **Document only:** `docs/route-reorder-proposal.md` (nothing was reordered).

Restart RuneLite through OSRS Path after updating.
