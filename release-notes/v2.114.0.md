# OSRS Path 2.114.0

- A Members account values its time by Members earning. Loot is kept, and coins weigh against XP, at the rate of the simplest Members method (grinding goat horns) when that pays better than cowhides. Free-to-play accounts still use cowhides.
- 24 Members money methods are now reviewed by hand like the free-to-play catalogue: tanning all four dragonhides, steel cannonballs, enchanted rings and amulets, gold and gem bracelets, redwood pyre logs, bullseye lanterns, jangerberries, bananas, wine of Zamorak, gloves of silence, goat horns, flax spinning at Lumbridge, Cooks' Guild pies, steel nails, the Agility Pyramid, limestone bricks, and guam cleaning and potions. Each has its real place, bank, trip and pace, and the levels its figures assume.
- Members methods bank at real Members banks (Ardougne, Catherby, Seers' Village, Castle Wars, Ferox Enclave and more), from the Shortest Path bank list the app already pins. Before, a method far from the free-to-play banks could bank across the map.
- Guides that need an unlock the route does not plan are held back with the reason: the Robes of Ruin dig for ruby rings, the Enter the Abyss miniquest, house lecterns and workshop tables, Nightmare Zone points, and diaries the figures assume. Zaff's battlestaves are a daily purchase, not an hourly method.

Verified before delivery: 2,643 unit tests, the F2P and Members route gates, the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
