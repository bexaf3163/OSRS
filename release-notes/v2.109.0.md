# OSRS Path 2.109.0

- New Members quest: Holy Grail (2 QP, 11,000 Prayer XP, 15,300 Defence XP; needs Merlin's Crystal and Attack 20). It follows Merlin's Crystal on a Members account's route and never shows in free-to-play mode. Every stage is on the card, from Merlin's tower and Entrana to the Fisher Realm, the grail and King Arthur. The level 120 Black Knight Titan is estimated with your kit, and Lobster x10 (or similar food) is a required item.
- Merlin's Crystal now records that you keep Excalibur, so Holy Grail's preparation counts it as carried.
- The Quest Helper reader now tells a step from a local variable with the same name, which fixed Holy Grail's last stage (bringing the grail to King Arthur).

Verified before delivery: 2,639 unit tests, the F2P route gate (23 of 23 quests, 0 of 211 turns with an unknown time), the Members route gate (48 of 48 quests, no check, 6 of 362 turns with an unknown time), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
