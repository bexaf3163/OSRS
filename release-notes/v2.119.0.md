# OSRS Path 2.119.0

- 86 Members money methods are now reviewed by hand, up from 82. New this release:
  - Grubby chests in the Forthos Dungeon, which need a knife for the webs.
  - Sinister chests in the Yanille Agility Dungeon. These need only the 49 Agility to enter, because the Magic level was for an optional teleport.
  - Charging and alching bracelets of ethereum.
  - Wine of Zamorak in the Deep Wilderness Dungeon.
- The earth and air orbs carry the Wilderness warning again.
- Guides now match quest names in any letter case. Mining basalt and three other guides were built although their quest (for example Making Friends with My Arm) is not on the route yet. They are held back until it is.

Verified before delivery: 2,643 unit tests, the F2P route gate (23 of 23 quests, 0 unknown turns), the Members route gate (51 of 51 quests, 4 of 376 turns unknown), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
