# OSRS Path 2.60.0

## The arrow now leads to the way in, not to "find the way down"

Reported on The Corsair Curse (2026-10-17): "Talk to Chief Tess (Corsair Cove Dungeon)" only said "Target is underground - find the way down",
and nothing showed where that was. Three changes:

- **Quest Helper's own step leads.** When Quest Helper's current step is a move (climb the ladder, enter the hole, go onto the ship) or a step our list
  has no line for, the arrow, the highlight and the distance line follow that step's own tile and object: "Go down to Tess (Hole) · ~40 tiles". The list
  still says what the step is for (Chief Tess).
- **A way in or out for any target in another layer.** A table of 706 dungeon entrances, cave holes, trapdoors and ladders (built from the Shortest Path
  plugin's transport data) gives "Way down: Enter Hole", "Way up: Climb Vine ladder", "Way in: …" for every step, quest or not. The object is highlighted.
- **Stairs for "a floor up".** The same table's 1,659 staircases and ladders: a target on another floor now points at the stairs of its building
  ("Way up: Climb-up Staircase"); going down, at the stairs of the building you are in. Where the table has no ladder (the Corsair Cove huts), Quest
  Helper's own step does.

## Every quest checked from start to end

A new automated walk goes through all 32 route quests, every stage and every step Quest Helper can pick (869 picks) and checks that each
one has a line or a place to show, that every dungeon or separate-area place has a known way in, and that a way back up is known wherever a stage
sends the player underground and back. It found and this release fixes:

- Misthalin Mystery: the piano step had no line (it now says the tune: D, E, A, D).
- Ernest the Chicken: "pull up lever C / D" after a wrong lever had no line.
- Monkey Madness I: stringing the amulet with the ball of wool had no line.
- Lost City: "lost the Dramen branch, go back for another" had no line.
- Prince Ali Rescue: "dye the wig" when the wig is not yellow yet, in the later stages.

Known and left as is: three Grand Tree travel steps after the Black demon have no tile in Quest Helper either; the arrow leads to King Narnode through
the trapdoor at the foot of the Grand Tree instead.
