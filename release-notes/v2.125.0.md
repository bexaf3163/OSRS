# OSRS Path 2.125.0

- Members routes now include Death Plateau. The guide follows the quest through Burthorpe: Eohric and Harold, the dice gamble, the IOU and its combination, and the five stone balls. Each ball has its own highlighted spot on the castle mechanism. It then covers Saba's cave, Tenzing's climbing boots, Dunstan's spiked boots, the food for Tenzing and the secret path north. The quest gives 1 quest point, so the Members total is now 110.
- The item list asks for 1,000 coins, because winning Harold's gamble depends on luck. 12 of them buy Tenzing's climbing boots.
- The RuneLite plugin can now confirm a step when an item lies on a given tile, so each stone-ball step ticks by itself once the ball is placed.

Verified before delivery: 2,648 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 54 Members quests finish), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game, including the new item-on-tile check.
