# OSRS Path 2.165.0

Members Ranged now uses members bows. The planner reads them from the OSRS Wiki's Bow table. A yew shortbow from level 40 fires up to rune arrows, and a magic shortbow from level 50 fires up to amethyst arrows. Before, a Members plan kept a maple shortbow with adamant arrows all the way to 99.

A Members Ranged plan to 99 now takes about 191 to 521 hours instead of 259 to 641. Free-to-play accounts never get the members bows, and their plans are unchanged.

The RuneLite plugin's item watch list still holds 40 names, so it watches the members bow and arrows only once the plan picks them. The Gemstone Crab and chinning stay out for now: the Wiki gives no Ranged rate for the crab, and the planner has no darts or chinchompas yet.

Verified before delivery: the unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 132 Members-route quests), the browser checks, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
