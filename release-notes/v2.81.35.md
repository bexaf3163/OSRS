# OSRS Path 2.81.35

- Every free skill's F2P stop now says what membership gives from that level, with OSRS Wiki rates: for example Wintertodt from Firemaking 50 (about 161,000 XP/h), Tempoross from Fishing 35, teaks from Woodcutting 35, the Blast Furnace for Smithing and chinchompas from Ranged 45.
- Optional chapter: each step shows its stages and the members comparison, and "Choose membership, or keep playing F2P" lists every skill's F2P stop beside what membership pays more.
- Ranged: Brutus (after The Ides of Milk) is listed as the best F2P Ranged XP, from a safespot.
- Crafting: leather armour runs from gloves to chaps until 28, then hardleather bodies (about 91,000 XP/h), as the Wiki advises.
- Woodcutting: oak trees stay the choice until 60 when you drop the logs.
