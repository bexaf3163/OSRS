# OSRS Path 2.82.8

- Skill goals come from the training engine instead of the old training table. When the planner needs no level in a skill, the goal is the level where the engine switches to a different method, for example Woodcutting 1 trains to 15 because Oak trees take over there, and Smithing 1 trains to 15 for iron bars. Before, the goal was the end of a fixed table row such as "1-15".
- The skill page says why: "Goal 15: the training engine switches to Oak trees there." When one method stays best all the way, the goal is 99 and the page says that no other method takes over before it.
- The calculator's default goal follows the same rule for every skill: the planner's level first, else the engine's next method change, else the next level.
- A level the planner needs still comes first. Skills without a training method (Hunter, Construction, Slayer) still aim for the next level.
- The skill page runs the training engine once for the goal and the "What to train with" card, so both always show the same method and level.
- Screens that read exchange prices within the same minute share one price view, so the training engine reuses its method ratings instead of computing them again.
- The training table stays in the Reference tab as reference only.
- Live in-game acceptance of this release is still pending.
