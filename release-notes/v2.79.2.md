# OSRS Path 2.79.2

Fixes from a live report.

- **No quest shopping while you follow a skill plan.** With a leveling plan followed (e.g. Defence on the frogs), the game no longer says "Buy Anti-dragon shield" for the next quest step. While a skill plan leads, the quest route waits, and so does its preparation. An arrow the preparation had already set is taken down; an arrow you set yourself stays.
- **The card shows the plan you follow.** When the game follows your leveling plan, the top card shows that method (e.g. Giant frogs) with your own XP per hour and what its drops bring per hour. The recommendation is one line under it when it differs.
- **Other methods show their earnings too.** Each method under "Other methods" has the same summary as the recommendation: XP per hour for you, coins per hour from drops, and the cost of supplies.

No gameplay automation. Saves, route IDs, adopted plans and progress are unchanged.
