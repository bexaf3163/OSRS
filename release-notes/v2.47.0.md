**Route audit fixes: a weapon you can wield, Dragon Slayer I completes on the quest, map pieces by identity, fewer grinds.**

- **S4-03 trains Attack 40 at the Edgeville Hill Giants** (140 XP a kill) with the Adamant scimitar; the Rune scimitar is no longer required or bought before Attack 40 (the gear advice offers it from 40). The step also asks for 50 Big bones banked (about 14,000-16,000 gp at the exchange: check the price when you sell). Bones in the bank are not coins until they are sold.
- **S5-09 completes on the quest alone.** The Rune platebody (84,500 gp, Defence 40) is an optional recommendation; it was part of the completion before.
- **S5-03, S5-04, S5-05:** each step recognises only its own map piece by item ID (1535, 1537, 1536), or the joined Crandor map. One piece can no longer complete all three.
- **S1-09:** the step text now says what the trigger already did: Fighting boots or Fancy boots are the proof of all four levels; the coins may be spent.
- **S7-05:** the route no longer sends you to Hazelmere by fairy ring CLS. The travel planner leaves fairy rings out until Fairytale II (S9-03) is done, and offers them normally after it.
- **S1-07:** the manor coordinates are treated as an instance: no Shortest Path route, no world-map marker, no transport suggestion. The arrow and the highlights inside the manor still work.
- **Retired:** S1-11 (Fishing 20 / Cooking 15). Its number stays free, old saves load without it, and the validator allows the gap.
- **S1-12 is Mining 10.** Doric's copper and iron ore are on the S2-01 shopping list instead of being mined.
- **S1-13 is 12,000 gp of coins** (was 20,000); unsold cowhides are not coins.
- **S3-06 is 2,500 coins only.** Iron ore or the scimitar in the bag no longer completes a money step.
- **S4-04** no longer mentions feathers, rods or fly fishing.
- **S5-08:** the Anti-dragon shield and the lobsters are required; the Rune sword and the potions are advice.
- **S8-03 completes at Agility 40.** Graceful pieces are a bonus.
- **Checked:** `tests/auditFixes.test.ts`, new cases in `AutoCompletionManagerTest` and `NavigationTest` on the real route data, regenerated fixtures and the full gate. Not confirmed in the live game, and the UI smoke test could not run on this machine (no browser installed).
- **Money, honestly:** from S4-03 on the route needs about 17,000 gp (20 lobsters about 2,100, the dragon materials and the ship about 5,000, Wormbrain 10,000). 50 Big bones are an estimate of 14,000-16,000 gp, and about 3,900 gp is left from the stage 3 rewards after the scimitar, so it is tight but close; the preparation list shows any shortfall as missing money.
