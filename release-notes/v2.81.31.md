# OSRS Path 2.81.31

- The in-game helper has a new look, Ledger, that matches the app: a leather plate with a progress ring, the step code and a NOW stamp.
- Opened, its details sit on a parchment page behind tabs for Now, Prepare, Next and Advice, so one section shows at a time, and the next step stays at the bottom of the Now page.
- Ledger is the new default. The earlier Glass and Classic looks stay under Plate style in the plugin's settings.
- The new plate is drawn once when something changes and then copied, so it costs less per frame than the earlier one.
