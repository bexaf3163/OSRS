# OSRS Path 2.112.0

- New Members quest: In Search of the Myreque (2 QP, 600 XP each in Attack, Strength, Defence, Hitpoints and Crafting; needs Nature Spirit and Agility 25). It follows Nature Spirit on a Members account's route and never shows in free-to-play mode. Every stage is on the card, from Vanstrom Klause and the swamp boat to the bridge repair, the Skeleton Hellhound and the Stranger. The level 97 Skeleton Hellhound is estimated with your kit, and Lobster x10 (or similar food) is a required item.
- Nature Spirit now records what it leaves you: the blessed silver sickle and the druid pouch, so later Morytania quests count them as carried instead of asking you to find them.

Verified before delivery: 2,642 unit tests, the F2P route gate (23 of 23 quests, 0 of 211 turns with an unknown time), the Members route gate (51 of 51 quests, no check, 6 of 390 turns with an unknown time), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
