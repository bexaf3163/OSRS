# OSRS Path 2.78.0

- **Woodcutting, Fishing and Mining for your character.** With your levels and tools known, the Skills page times each method from the OSRS Wiki's own numbers: the success chance for your level and your best usable axe or pickaxe, the time per attempt, how long trees last and rocks take to come back, and how full the bag gets. "≈ 23k–28k XP per hour for you" replaces the wiki's guide for everyone. While the bank has not been checked, the app does not guess your axe: the wiki's guide stays.
- **The right spot, and bank or drop.** Every free spot of the method is compared (Draynor or Port Sarim willows, the Varrock or Lumbridge Swamp mines, …). A spot next to its bank wins when the shorter walk makes it better. Logs, ore and fish are banked only when their Grand Exchange price after tax pays for the walk, and never on the Fastest objective. The "To the place" button and the in-game arrow go to the chosen spot.
- **Monsters that attack you are avoided.** Spots with aggressive monsters (the scorpions of Al Kharid mine, the dark wizards by the Draynor bank) are skipped for a player they would attack. This follows the wiki's rule: combat level at most twice theirs. An unknown combat level never counts as safe.
- **Coins never outweigh speed by more than half.** The rate the app uses to value coins (killing cows for hides) is a rough baseline, so banked coins can at most halve a method's effective time.
- Data: a new wiki-checked registry of trees, rocks, fishing spots, tools and spots (`npm run build-skilling`). Adding a members area or a new resource is an entry and a rebuild.

No gameplay automation. Saves, route IDs and progress are unchanged.
