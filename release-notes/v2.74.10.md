# OSRS Path 2.74.10

## A calmer step page, Shopping and Skills

The app was showing the same thing several times. This pass removes repeats only: every function stays where it was or one click away.

- **Step page.** Preparation was three boxes saying "preparation needed": the status card with its ring, the verdict box, and a "Review preparation" button. It is now one panel. The status line (ring, percentage, Fix) sits at its top, followed by the verdict's own advice and the item list. The "Where" card loses its "Travel and directions" button. Preparation, Travel and Details still open from the row under the current action, or with the P, T and D keys.
- **Shopping.** The budget box was seven lines of numbers. It now shows what to buy now, the next steps, a shortfall and "Raise the coins" when there is one, and any items without a price. The arithmetic, unconfirmed rows, optional extras and price notes are under "How this is counted".
- **Skills.** The twelve "Leveling roadmap" buttons are quiet text buttons. They open the same roadmap; the cards now read as levels and goals first.

No new panels, gameplay automation or route reordering. Saves, route IDs and progress are unchanged.
