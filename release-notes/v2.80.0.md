# OSRS Path 2.80.0

Fish cooked on the spot.

- **Fishing and Cooking at once.** For the "Fishing to 50 and Cooking to 50" step, the app works out whether cooking your trout and salmon on the fire beside the Barbarian Village fishing spot gets you to both goals sooner than fishing first and cooking later. When it does, the step says so in one line, and the game highlights the fire.
- Only what your Cooking level can cook is cooked (salmon from 25, trout from 15). Each burn chance comes from the wiki.
- When cooking separately later is faster (e.g. wine from Cooking 35), the step just fishes.

No gameplay automation. Saves, route IDs, adopted plans and progress are unchanged.
