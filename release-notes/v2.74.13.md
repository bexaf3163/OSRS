# OSRS Path 2.74.13

## Skills: a training guide, not a list of levels

Each skill card now leads with what to train right now ("Now: Willows"), your level and the stage goal, and a **Training guide** button. Level fields appear only while the game is not reporting your levels; with RuneLite connected the level is simply shown.

A skill opens on its **Training guide**: the recommended method with its place and a button to lead you there, then the whole 1–99 leveling roadmap with **Follow this plan** right below it, no longer on a separate tab or in a drawer. Calculator and Reference are still one tab away; level editing and live XP are at the bottom of the guide.

## The in-game card stays short

Every route line on the game card now fits in 225 characters. Thirteen lines were paragraphs that pushed the rest of the card off the screen for a whole fight (Elvarg, the Ancient Guardian, Melzar's demon, the Jungle Demon and others). They were rewritten to keep every action, number and dialogue answer, and a test holds the limit across the route.

## Saving stays paused when it should

If both saved copies of your progress are damaged, the app pauses saving to protect them. Marking steps from the game ("The game knows you have already completed…" → Mark as done) could lift that pause and write a nearly empty save over the damaged file. It no longer can; only an explicit import in Settings resumes saving. The packaged app is now tested against a damaged file, both copies damaged, and a save from a newer version.

## Smaller things

- The quest list on a step fades at the bottom while more lines are hidden below.
- One Settings entry on wide windows (the sidebar's); the top-bar gear stays for narrow windows and the compact view.

No gameplay automation or route reordering. Saves, route IDs and progress are unchanged.
