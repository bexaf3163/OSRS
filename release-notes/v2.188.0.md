# OSRS Path 2.188.0

## Stealing artefacts counts its coins

In 2.187.0, stealing artefacts lost to blackjacking once The Feud was done. The plan counted the coins blackjacking makes, but treated artefacts as earning nothing, although every heist pays 500 to 1,000 coins. The method now uses the Wiki's own numbers. You can do about 48 heists an hour without a teleport, and each one gives 750 XP plus 40 times your Thieving level. That makes about 130,000 XP and 36,000 coins an hour at 49, and 226,000 XP at 99. With these figures, artefacts are the pick from 50 with or without The Feud.

The earlier rate of 150,000 to 261,000 XP an hour assumed the Book of the Dead teleport, which needs The Queen of Thieves. The note on the method says how much that teleport adds.

## Herbiboar for Members Hunter

Herbiboar on Fossil Island joins the Hunter plan from level 80, at about 137,000 XP an hour rising to 171,000 at 99 (OSRS Wiki, checked 25 November 2026). It needs Herblore 31 and Bone Voyage. Once those are done, it is the pick ahead of carnivorous chinchompas, because it trains faster with little attention. The herbs from harvesting are not counted. The Mushroom Forest is now on the map.
