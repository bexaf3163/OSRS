# OSRS Path 2.69.0

**The guide picks a sensible training plan by itself. Nothing to switch on first.**

- **Combat training (S4-03 Hill Giants, S3-08 Minotaurs).** The recommended plan is in effect as soon as the step opens: the step's instructions, the place, the walkthrough, the food and shopping amounts, the arrow and the in-game panel all follow it. For the reported character (Attack 30, Defence 1, iron armour, 400 gp) that is *Cows to Attack 40, then the Hill Giants only for the 50 Big bones*: about 310 fewer pieces of food (about 10,000 gp). With better levels and kit the route as written stays.
- **One line, one reason.** The step status says *Plan: …* with one short reason. *Why this plan?* holds the comparison, the estimates and every other plan, each with **Use this instead**. The route's own instructions are not shown beside the plan's: they are folded under *The route as written*.
- **Your choice stays.** A plan you choose (the route as written included) is kept. If another becomes clearly better it is offered (*Looks better now: … Use it*), never forced. *Follow the recommendation* hands the choice back to the guide.
- **No switch in the middle of a trip.** If a better plan appears while you are fighting with food in the bag, the arrow stays. The status line says *From your next bank trip: …* and the change applies when you bank or run out of food. A plan that becomes unsafe or impossible (the bones already banked) gives way at once.
- **Older saves.** A step where you never chose a plan had the route as written by default: it now gets the recommendation. Choices made with the old **Use this plan** button are kept as yours.
- **Offline:** the plan is worked out from your last known levels and gear, so the guide does not fall back to another plan while RuneLite is closed.
- **Training methods** (Fishing in S6-02 and the Skills pages) default to **Balanced**: the quickest practical way to the goal, counting getting missing items, how much attention it takes and material cost. A new unlock does not make you switch for a sliver; a short goal does not send you shopping for a tool. Settings → *Training choices*: **Balanced**, **Fastest** (an earlier *Efficient* choice stays as Fastest), **Relaxed** (the old calm order). The old *Calm / Efficient* switch is gone.
- Audit of every affected stage, with old and new defaults: `docs/DEFAULT_PLANS.md`.
