# OSRS Path 2.189.0

## Grand Exchange offers count again

RuneLite 1.13.8 changed how an offer reports the coins it holds, so the plugin dropped every offer and the app never saw items or coins waiting in the exchange. The plugin is now built against RuneLite 1.13.8, and a check of every RuneLite call the plugin makes passes against it.

## Fixes from a full code audit

An outside review listed 69 possible problems. Each one was checked against the code, and these were real:

- Banknotes of a quest item no longer count as the item in your bag, so the guide shows "Unnote" instead of a tick. Noted teleport tablets and noted staves no longer count as usable either.
- Logging out clears the last character's Grand Exchange offers. A cancelled buy that already bought some still counts until you collect it, and auto-tick counts items waiting in the exchange.
- Turning off "Upgrade hints" no longer hides your bag, coins and worn gear from the app.
- Bag space is right for Rune essence, rune weapons, Rune bars and Bolt of cloth, which take a slot each. Thread, cannonballs and teleport tablets stack.
- In Members mode, the ranged gear plan includes Members armour.
- Magic defence follows the Wiki DPS calculator. The app underrated how well a low-level player defends against magic.
- A tool upgrade that saves coins now says how many coins it saves, not "saves about 1 min".
- On a fresh account, the first kill at level 1 counts toward the training pace.
- Accounts above total level 2,277 keep their levels, because the limit is 2,376 since Sailing.
- The plugin knows gilded, infernal, 3rd age and crystal pickaxes and axes, and that they are members tools.
- Texts and card titles name steps instead of codes like S5-06.
- A click on a small card with a spell or item selected, or with the world map open, goes to the game.
- The Grand Exchange card also lists supplies for the road, and the bank card says "Wear" when the items are already in your bag.
- The plugin's Port setting is gone, because the app always uses port 38282.
- The map's floor buttons read G, 1, 2, 3, as their tooltips do, and place names show as plain text.
- When RuneLite runs a different character than the open profile, that character no longer gets the profile's travel plan.
- The portable app brings every profile along on its first start, saves reach the disk at least every 2 seconds, and the backup copy cannot be cut short by a crash.
- The plugin refuses requests from web pages.

Some claims were wrong. The app already identifies itself to the Wiki, the RuneLite launcher already enables assertions, and the Quest Helper region size matches Quest Helper itself.

Verified before delivery: all app and plugin tests, the F2P and Members route gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
