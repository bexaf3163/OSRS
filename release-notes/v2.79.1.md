# OSRS Path 2.79.1

Money and Skills time smelting the same way.

- **Smelting on the Money page uses the Skills engine.** Steel and silver bars at the Edgeville furnace are timed by the wiki's recipe for your Smithing level. The scaled published pace is no longer used for them. Iron bars count their 50% chance.
- Methods with no skill recipe (pie shells, pizza bases, jugs, shop runs) keep their published pace, and say so.

No gameplay automation. Saves, route IDs, adopted plans and progress are unchanged.
