# OSRS Path 2.74.8

From the full route review.

## One outline, not every ladder

Some lines cover several places at once, such as "climb to Glough's house, then search the cupboard". These lines have no single tile, so they used to outline every object with their ID: every ladder of the Gnome Stronghold lit up at once. They now outline only the nearest copy, on your own floor first. Lines that do have a tile still outline the copy nearest that tile, as for Melzar's doors.

## Lines that were missing

- **Monkey Madness I:** a new line before Marim. Equip the Karamjan monkey greegree and wear the M'speak amulet, then enter the gate. The gate is outlined and the greegree is highlighted.
- **Fairytale I:** the Tanglefoot line now also says to pick up the queen's secateurs it drops. It finishes only when they are in your bag, because the quest cannot be finished without them.

## Food the game checks is on the list

Two more steps had an in-game line that waits for food you were only "recommended" to bring: The Knight's Sword (10 trout before the Ice Dungeon) and Fairytale I (10 lobsters before the Tanglefoot). The food is now required in both, so preparation and the game agree. A route-wide test now checks that every item an in-game line waits for is a required item of its step. The one exception is Demon Slayer: its food would not fit in the bag beside the 25 bones at the start, and the game checks it later.

No new panels, gameplay automation or route reordering. Saves, route IDs and progress are unchanged. In-game acceptance is pending.
