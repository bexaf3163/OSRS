# OSRS Path 2.54.1 — food before dangerous entries, stored canoe axes, Count Check, Fishing 1-40

From the audit backlog; each item was checked against the OSRS Wiki and Jagex's 1 April 2026 post first.

- **Food before a dangerous entry (the Ancient Guardian lesson, applied to the whole route).** The stage tables listed food, if at all, only in the stage of the fight. The entry line of each dangerous encounter now asks for food in pieces (5 to 20, counting any listed fish) and its stage list names it, so the game screen shows it before you go in and the arrow turns to a bank or the Exchange when it is short: the Ice Dungeon (S2-07), Draynor Manor (S2-08), the Black Knights' Fortress (S3-02), Delrith (S3-03), Ithoi (S4-01), the bull and Brutus (S4-02), Elvarg's ship (S5-08, 20 pieces with the shield), the Fight Arena (S7-02), the Khazard warlord (S7-03), the Black demon (S7-05), the Temple Guardian (S8-01), Mort Myre (S8-02), the Tanglefoot (S9-02) and the Jungle Demon (S9-04). An unread bag blocks nothing.
- **Selected iron** is named as the game names it (S8-05 walkthrough), so the item highlight and the text agree.
- **Canoe axes.** Since 1 April 2026 an axe stored at any canoe station is shared by all of them and cannot be read from the game. A canoe with no axe in the bag was "locked"; it is now "possible", and the card says to bring an axe or use the one stored at a station. The "get an axe first" way is still offered.
- **Count Check.** The one free teleport to the Stronghold of Security entrance is a way in the planner (S1-09 and anything near the entrance): "Talk to Count Check", shown as "check: you never entered the Stronghold" because the game does not tell. It disappears once S1-09 is done or skipped. The plugin highlights Count Check.
- **Fishing 1-40 (S6-02, the optional Bond branch).** The step started at lobsters. It now has the climb: net shrimps and anchovies at Draynor (1-20), fly fishing at Barbarian Village (20-40), harpoon tuna from 35, with the net, the fly fishing rod and the feathers from Gerrant's Fishy Business in Port Sarim.
- **Not modelled, on purpose (no verified coordinates or unreadable state):** the River Dougne canoe network (five stations added 1 April 2026; the wiki gives no tiles and no confirmed order, and one wiki page marks it Members-only), spirit trees and gnome gliders (no coordinates), the Cowbell amulet as a planner way (its charges cannot be read; the landing tile is the cow field).
- **Checked:** `tsc`, build, 1008 Vitest tests, `check-data`, the full Java suite, UI smoke. Not tested in game yet.

Restart RuneLite through OSRS Path after updating.
