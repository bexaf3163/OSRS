# OSRS Path 2.159.1

Skill plans no longer send you to buy the wrong supplies, and they keep getting faster as your level rises.

Crafting no longer sends you to buy sapphires while you hold rubies. Gems and other materials you already hold now cost what selling them would bring, not what buying them again would cost.

Methods and recipes are now compared at market prices. The planning margin (buying a little above and selling a little below the market) made every expensive recipe lose to the cheapest one, so Crafting stayed on sapphire necklaces from level 22 to 99. Now it moves on through emerald and ruby jewellery to diamond necklaces from 56 and diamond amulets from 70. Smithing no longer falls back to bronze axes above level 52, and gathering skills rank their spots the same way. The costs and coins shown on the plan still include the margin, so you are not left short of coins.

Magic no longer plans Superheat Item, which loses money on every cast. From 25 it plans Amulet of magic, then Amulet of defence from 27 and High Level Alchemy from 55, all of which pay. Enchanting and alchemy now plan one elemental staff (Staff of water, air or fire) instead of buying thousands of runes, unless you already hold the runes.

Mining and woodcutting plans no longer slow down halfway: every stage is now rated with the pickaxe or axe it plans, and the black pickaxe and axe, which the rate model does not know, are no longer planned. Iron is mined by dropping the ore, with no bank trips, at the three iron rocks of Al Kharid mine (South-east Varrock below combat level 29, where the scorpions attack): about 24k to 39k XP per hour from level 41 instead of 13k to 26k.

Members woodcutting no longer switches from teaks back to willows at level 75: the Crystal, Infernal, 3rd age and gilded axes and pickaxes, which have no rates of their own, are now rated like the best axe or pickaxe their level allows.

A new test builds every free-to-play and Members skill plan from 1 to 99 and fails if any stage is much slower than the one before it.

The checks before a release are faster: the plugin layout tests take about 5 minutes instead of 33, and the route gate rates the training methods only where they can change.

Profit figures are estimates at the prices of the moment; Grand Exchange buy limits and offer times are not modelled yet.

Verified before delivery: the unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 132 Members-route quests), the browser checks on every page, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. The new picks were checked against today's Grand Exchange prices. Nothing was tested in the real game.
