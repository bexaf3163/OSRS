# OSRS Path 2.159.1

Crafting no longer sends you to buy sapphires while you hold rubies, and higher gems are chosen again as your level rises.

Gems and other materials you already hold now cost what selling them would bring, not what buying them again would cost. A player with rubies and gold bars in the bank makes ruby jewellery with them.

Methods and recipes are now compared at market prices. The planning margin (buying a little above and selling a little below the market) made every expensive recipe lose to the cheapest one, so Crafting stayed on sapphire necklaces from level 22 to 99. Now it moves on to emerald, ruby and diamond jewellery: for example diamond necklaces from 56 and diamond amulets from 70. Smithing no longer falls back to bronze axes above level 52. Gathering skills (fishing, mining, woodcutting) rank their spots and catches the same way. The costs and coins shown on the plan still include the margin, so you are not left short of coins.

Profit figures are estimates at the prices of the moment; Grand Exchange buy limits and offer times are not modelled yet.

Verified before delivery: the unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 132 Members-route quests), the browser checks on every page, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. The new picks were checked against today's Grand Exchange prices for every free-to-play skill. Nothing was tested in the real game.
