# OSRS Path 2.74.4

## Melzar's Maze is clear to follow

The guide in Melzar's Maze (S5-03) now follows Quest Helper's own room-and-key logic: in the rat room without a key it asks for the marked zombie rat again, with the key it shows the right door, and behind the door the ladder. The same holds for the ghost, skeleton and basement rooms. Before, a key spent on a wrong door left the list on the door, because the nearest tile cannot tell the maze's rooms apart through a wall.

Only the right door is outlined. When several objects share the line's object ID (the maze's same-coloured doors), the one nearest the line's tile is marked, as Quest Helper marks the object on its step's tile; this applies to every quest line.

Each coloured door line names its door by the guide's floor tile and by the wall it is on, says that the other doors use up the key, and explains "The door is locked": every coloured key crumbles on the door it opens, a wrong door too.

## Other Dragon Slayer I lines

The Thalzar magic door line says where the door is: the square room in the north-east corner of the Dwarven Mine. Repairing Lady Lumbridge asks for 30 Steel nails per plank (3 planks, 90 nails), not one.

No new panels, gameplay automation or route reordering. Saves, route IDs and progress are unchanged. In-game acceptance of this change is pending.
