# OSRS Path 2.111.0

- New Members quest: Shades of Mort'ton (3 QP, 2,000 Herblore XP, 2,000 Crafting XP; needs Priest in Peril, Crafting 20, Herblore 15 and Firemaking 5). It joins the Morytania stage of a Members account's route and never shows in free-to-play mode. Every stage is on the card, from the diary and serum 207 to the five Loar Shades, the temple repairs and the funeral pyre. The level 40 Loar Shades are estimated with your kit, and Lobster x10 (or similar food) is a required item.

Verified before delivery: 2,641 unit tests, the F2P route gate (23 of 23 quests, 0 of 211 turns with an unknown time), the Members route gate (50 of 50 quests, no check, 6 of 369 turns with an unknown time), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
