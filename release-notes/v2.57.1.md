# OSRS Path 2.57.1

## Combat kits for the quest bosses

- **Vampyre Slayer, The Corsair Curse, The Ides of Milk**: one Strength potion(4) is advised, with the boss's real numbers (Count Draynor 35 hit points, Ithoi 55, Brutus 58). Corsair Curse and Ides of Milk say to wield the Adamant scimitar.
- **Demon Slayer**: Trout for the dark wizards at the stone circle (they hit up to 6 with magic); Delrith has only 7 hit points.
- **Fight Arena, Tree Gnome Village, The Grand Tree (members)**: a Staff of fire and Fire Bolt runes for the safespots the wiki names, with casts needed and a note to take metal armour off first.
- **Ranged to 30** (members): darts on Rapid as the faster way.
- **Buy the Adamant scimitar**: stock up the Minotaur food and potions in the same trip.
