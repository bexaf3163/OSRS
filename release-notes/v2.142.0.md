# OSRS Path 2.142.0

Three more Members quests are on the route: Daddy's Home, Tower of Life and The Ribbiting Tale of a Lily Pad Labour Dispute.

Daddy's Home plans 10 planks, 5 bolts of cloth and 100 steel nails for Old Man Yarlo's furniture, and its Construction XP counts toward Tower of Life. Tower of Life plans the hammer, saw and the beer for 'The Guns', and lists the homunculus answers for Logic. The Ribbiting Tale needs nothing bought, because the axe lies next to Marcellus.

Quest Helper drafts now read quests filed as miniquests as Members content, and skip helper classes such as puzzle solvers that hold no steps.

Verified before delivery: 2,660 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 95 Members-route quests finish in 762 turns), the browser checks on every page, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
