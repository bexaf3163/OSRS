# OSRS Path 2.74.18

## Skills

- **Ranged training counts the arrows for the whole goal.** The training card now gives the shots to your goal level with your current bow and arrows, leg by leg against each method's own target (Seagulls, the Edgeville monks, Giant frogs, Flesh Crawlers). It also says how many arrows break if you pick the rest up (one in five) and that every shot costs one if you leave them. The count comes from the OSRS Wiki's ranged formulas and the wiki's numbers for bows, arrows, armour and targets. Metal armour lowers ranged accuracy and is counted; prayers are not, so the count errs high.
