# OSRS Path 2.64.1

**The in-game panel follows your coin-raising plan.** While you are raising coins for your next trip (2.64.0), the HUD plate under the arrow adds one line:

> Raising coins: Kill cows for cowhide · 12 / 28 Cowhide

- It is the same plan the app shows, taken from the same data: what to do now in a few words and how far it has gone. Loot you have not sold yet is marked "(estimated)". The game does not work anything out for itself.
- The arrow the plan sets reads as a plain instruction ("Go to the cow field north of Lumbridge.") instead of "Go to: Funding: ...".
- The line shows only for the character the plan belongs to, only while the app is running (it goes away if the app has been closed for ten minutes), and not at all while the plan is paused or cancelled.
- An older plugin ignores the new field; an older app sends nothing new. Nothing is bought, sold, withdrawn or fought for you.
