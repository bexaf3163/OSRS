# OSRS Path 2.74.12

## The in-game card keeps the instruction, the game shows the answers

The plugin's own session log showed the card's instruction line cut off with "…" before its end: 107 characters for the Oracle, 201 for Wormbrain, 328 for Elvarg. Much of that length was the dialogue answers, which the game's chat box already highlights for you. The card now leaves the answers out and keeps the instruction. Text after the answers stays too, such as "It costs 2,000 coins." for Klarense's boat. The full line, answers included, is still in the hover hint and in the OSRS Path panel.

No new panels, gameplay automation or route reordering. Saves, route IDs and progress are unchanged.
