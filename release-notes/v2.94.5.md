# OSRS Path 2.94.5

- Food is named by what you own. When a step asks for Salmon and your bank holds Lobster, the step's readiness and the preparation trip now say "Take 4 Lobster at the bank: they heal as much", like the shopping list already did. Before, the bank reminder read "Take from the bank: Salmon ×12 (or Trout, Tuna…)" and never named the Lobster you actually had. Every "take from the bank" line uses the same wording.
- The "The guide updated to V2!" banner and the "New in version V2" notice on steps are gone. Only two early steps (S1-03 and S1-04) ever carried them, and the app already marked them as checked when you ticked them, so they appeared only for very old saves. Old saves still load, and quest points kept from an old reset still count.
- Checked, nothing to change: a sweep of every free-to-play skill's recommended method, level by level, found no wrong pick. Woodcutting keeps oaks a little past 30 with a bronze axe: the wiki's own guide lists oaks for "15–30/60/99", and the app rates oaks and willows within 3% there. Smithing switches to Bronze platebodies at 18 because they give the most XP until Iron warhammers at 24.

Verified before delivery: 2,617 unit tests (new: Lobster in the bank for a Salmon step is named in the readiness and the bank trip, and the step's own food keeps its name without the healing note; the V2 review's own tests are retired with it), the route gate (23 of 23 quests, no check, 1 of 211 turns with an unknown time), 797 browser checks and the Electron desktop checks, run locally under a virtual display; typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Two paired timings against 2.94.4, in both orders: mean non-XP update script time +3% (14.3 → 14.8 ms); cold start +7% on Path, +3% on Shopping, −4% on the Ranged skill page; page switches −7% to +28%. A CPU profile of the Path cold start on unminified builds (3 loads, twice per build) shows less work in 2.94.5 (107 and 126 ms against 110 and 130 ms), so the timing difference is not traced to a code path. The plugin code is unchanged since 2.87.0 (its 640 tests passed then). Nothing was tested in the real game.
