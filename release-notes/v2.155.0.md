# OSRS Path 2.155.0

Heroes' Quest, Throne of Miscellania and Lunar Diplomacy are on the route.

Heroes' Quest plans a harralander potion (unf), a fishing rod and bait for the lava eel, a pickaxe for the rockslide on White Wolf Mountain, the dusty key, and 10 lobsters for the level 47 Jailer and the level 111 Ice Queen. Entrana allows no weapons or armour, so the route banks them first and fights the firebird bare-handed. The gang part needs a partner from the other Shield of Arrav gang. Black Arm members wear black armour in Scarface Pete's mansion, and Phoenix members kill Grip with Ranged or Magic. Like Shield of Arrav, the quest is optional, and so is Throne of Miscellania, which needs it.

Throne of Miscellania plans 1,905 coins for the Flower Girl's flowers and the 75% support, a gold ring, a shortbow for Princess Astrid or a cake for Prince Brand, an iron bar for the giant nib and logs for the giant pen.

Lunar Diplomacy plans the tools, the four elemental talismans, a lit bullseye lantern, your dramen staff (the altars turn it into the lunar staff), guam and marrentill, a needle and thread, and 15 lobsters for the level 111 Suqahs and Me, your double in the Dream World.

On the free-to-play route, a Members quest no longer keeps a closed Shield of Arrav open. CI gives the plugin tests 30 minutes and the route gates 90, because the plugin job ran out of its 15 minutes once.

Royal Trouble, My Arm's Big Adventure, the King Awowogei part of Recipe for Disaster and A Ruff Situation stay off the route for now. Royal Trouble needs Slayer 40, My Arm's Big Adventure needs 60% Tai Bwo Wannai favour, King Awowogei needs three more greegrees, and the Quest Helper entry for A Ruff Situation uses a newer format the draft tool does not read yet.

Verified before delivery: 2,690 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 127 Members-route quests), the browser checks on every page, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
