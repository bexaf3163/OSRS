# OSRS Path 2.182.0

## Dragon gear for Members

The audit found that the Gear page stopped at rune on Members too, even at level 99 with 100 million coins, because the gear list held only free-to-play weapons and armour. Every Members melee and Slayer plan therefore assumed rune gear.

The gear list now has the dragon tier from the Wiki: the dragon dagger, scimitar, longsword, mace, warhammer, battleaxe, 2h sword, axe and pickaxe, and the dragon full helm, med helm, platebody, chainbody, platelegs, plateskirt, kiteshield and sq shield. Each carries its real wearing requirements, for example 60 Attack and Monkey Madness I for the dragon scimitar, 60 Attack and Heroes' Quest for the dragon mace and battleaxe, 60 Strength for the dragon warhammer, and 60 Defence and Legends' Quest for the dragon sq shield. Dragon gear is advised only on Members, and only when you meet its requirements and the price is known.

The Wiki reader for wearing requirements had two faults that the new items showed. It read every sentence of an article that looked like a requirement, so a sentence about boosting Strength turned into "99 Attack, 97 Ranged" for the dragon battleaxe. It now reads the first sentence about wearing, where the Wiki states it. It also missed the wording "an Attack level of at least 60". All 163 earlier items came out unchanged.

## No more earning 100 million coins for a shield

With dragon gear in the list, the Members route earned 100,959,148 coins with Cannonballs before Roving Elves, then bought a dragon full helm for 40 million and a dragon kiteshield for 58.5 million to train Attack on Gemstone Crabs. Two rules caused it.

The training kit bought the strongest piece in every slot whatever it cost, and sent you to earn the rest. With rune as the top tier that meant earning about 38,000 coins per piece. Now the plan earns only for pieces up to 1 million coins. A dearer piece is bought only from coins you already hold.

When no kit wins a quest fight at your current levels, the fight planner searches for the lowest even melee levels at which the best kit wins. It offered only the most expensive kit or no kit at all. It now also searches with the best kit under 1 million and under 10 million coins, so the time comparison weighs earning a dear kit against a little more training. Free-to-play has no piece over 1 million coins, so its route is unchanged.

The Members route now earns 400,914 coins before Roving Elves for a dragon chainbody, med helm and platelegs. Later, for the level 191 Giant scarab in Contact!, it earns 2.17 million for a dragon platebody, because the planner found that faster than more training.

The abyssal whip is not in yet: it has its own attack styles, which the combat model needs first.
