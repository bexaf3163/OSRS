# OSRS Path 2.97.0

- Slayer has a training method on Members accounts: Turael's Slayer tasks (Turael in Burthorpe, cows in the Lumbridge cow field are one of his tasks). Slayer XP is the HP of each monster you kill on task, so the app rates it as a quarter of the melee XP of the same kills, in your best style. Other tasks and the walk to Burthorpe for each new task make it slower, as the method's note says. Before, the Skills page had no Slayer method, and the planner stopped at Animal Magnetism (Slayer 18).
- The Members route gate now gets through Animal Magnetism's training and stops on its Ecto-tokens, which come from worshipping at the Ectofuntus. That process is not modelled yet.

Verified before delivery: 2,624 unit tests (new: Slayer earns a quarter of the best melee XP on the same kills), the F2P route gate (23 of 23 quests, no check, 0 of 211 turns with an unknown time) and the Members route gate (26 of 35, within its debt of 9), the browser and Electron desktop checks, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
