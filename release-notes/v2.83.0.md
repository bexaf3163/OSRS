# OSRS Path 2.83.0

- The old skill leveling guides are gone. The Skills page no longer shows the Wiki leveling roadmap ("1-15 trees, 15-30 oaks") or the "Training plan" bracket tables. The training engine alone picks the method, the goal and the plan from your levels, items, coins and play style, and its card shows that plan from level 1 to the goal.
- "Follow training plan" on the engine card is the only way to follow a skill. Following a skill always runs the engine's training session on Path, in Shopping and in the game.
- Skills without a training method in the engine yet (Hunter, Construction, Slayer) say so instead of showing a guide row.
- The plugin no longer receives a guide skill path. In the game a followed skill is led by the engine's training step.
- The Reference tab keeps the skill facts, your levels and the XP table. The "Skill training: how it works" text and the members-skills introduction, which described the old step-by-step route, are removed.
- Combat training food comes from the fight model for melee, ranged and magic. The training methods no longer carry a fixed food amount, such as 10 Salmon. When no single fight can bring you to the eating point, and the monster is neither aggressive nor in a group, food is a recommendation, not a requirement. At 44 Defence and 44 Hitpoints in leather, giant frogs ask for 2 Salmon as optional. At 1 Defence and 10 Hitpoints they still require it.
- The planner's order names training objectives in words ("Train Ranged to 40") instead of their code ("training:ranged:40").
- Layout: the quest point chip on Path sits centred in its row with space before the planner's list. Rows that open no step stay readable. The row no longer repeats "time unknown" next to the same reason. Bottom bar labels wrap to a second line instead of being cut, and below 980 pixels the bar keeps one row down to about 650 pixels.
- Live in-game acceptance of this release is still pending.
