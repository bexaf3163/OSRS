# OSRS Path 2.74.5

## Melzar's lesser demon is a magic fight, and the guide says so before you go in

The lesser demon at the end of Melzar's Maze (level 82, Defence 71) is fought with magic from the spot one square east of the magenta door, where it cannot reach you and melee cannot reach it. The Water Strike runes (50 Water, 50 Air, 50 Mind) were only recommended, so preparation showed ready and a player reached the demon without them. They are now required for S5-03: the maze entrance line asks for them, and at the demon a player with no runes is sent to buy them. The demon line, the danger warning and the step's notes say "magic only"; Water Bolt from Magic 23 is named as the faster option. The Staff of air suggestion is dropped, because a worn staff cannot yet replace a required rune.

## A fight that takes a whole bag of food is not ready

When the fight estimate for your levels and worn gear needs about a full bag of food on average (Elvarg at Attack 40 and Defence 10, for example), the readiness verdict now says preparation is needed and names the fight, instead of a caution. The Elvarg line gives her defence and the wiki's safer method for lower levels: flinching her in the south-east corner of her cave.
