# OSRS Path 2.147.0

In Aid of the Myreque is on the route, which opens the rest of the Myreque story.

It plans the rebuilding of Burgh de Rott (11 planks, 44 steel nails, swamp paste, steel bars and coal), Aurel's crate (10 raw mackerel, 10 bronze axes, 4 tinderboxes), the silvthrill rod (silver bar, mithril bar, sapphire, soft clay and runes for Lvl-1 Enchant), the tools, and 15 salmon for Gadderanks and the vampyre juvinates. Juvinates take damage only from silver, so the planner wields the blessed silver sickle from Nature Spirit against them.

Its 45 Quest Helper stages share repeated entries, so the in-game guide carries 29.

Verified before delivery: 2,667 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 103 Members-route quests finish in 858 turns), the browser checks on every page, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
