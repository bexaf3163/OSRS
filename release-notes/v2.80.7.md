# OSRS Path 2.80.7

Food preparation now uses healing consistently across the app and RuneLite.

- Twenty Trout no longer count as twenty Salmon. Mixed foods are added by healing before rounding, and real Hitpoints limit the healing credited to each bite.
- Departure checks, readiness, Shopping, funding and preparation share the food allocation rules. One physical stack cannot cover two food requirements, and quest ingredients stay reserved.
- An unread alternative stays unchecked. Confirmed sufficient stock still proves you own enough, even when other alternatives are unread.
- Merged requirements accept only alternatives valid for every source. Exact quest ingredients are not silently replaced with combat food.
- The RuneLite checklist uses the same healing and allocation rules. Enabling the plugin mid-session and gaining Hitpoints refresh the healing limit.

No additional menus or panels. Existing saves, route IDs and manual ownership marks are preserved. Unrecognised alternatives no longer receive assumed healing credit.

The isolated real update from 2.80.5 to 2.80.6 passed: automatic restart and cleanup preserved the saved step byte-for-byte. This is update evidence, not in-game combat acceptance. The attached Claude handoff records remaining packing, acquisition, potion and content work.
