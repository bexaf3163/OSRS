# OSRS Path 2.51.0 — route audit

the route now says what is optional, what a step keeps for a later step, what each stage really gives, and what you must carry where.

- **Below Ice Mountain (S3-04):** the Ancient Guardian fight is now one clear method: use Mine on the four Structural pillars in the room corners (Mining 10 and any pickaxe, they are not ore). The pre-flight asks for a pickaxe of any tier and food; the health warning knows the Guardian hits up to 7 and warns below 14 HP while that line is current; the pillar is highlighted; the east-side safespot is the fallback. The quest closes only when the game says it is complete.
- **Optional steps never block:** `softRequires` suggests a step without locking the next one (Shield of Arrav S3-05 → S3-06). A skipped optional quest lowers the quest point goal instead of stalling the route.
- **Carryover:** a step that is complete in the game can still be "not ready for the route" (Sheep Shearer is green, but the three balls of wool for Prince Ali Rescue are not in the bank). Five early steps now keep the wool, the flour, the ashes, a copper and a tin ore, and a Bronze med helm; the auto-tick waits for them and the card says why.
- **Waterfall Quest:** three loadouts. Glarial's Tomb takes no runes, weapons, armour, arrows or logs; the runes, the rope, the amulet, the urn and food belong to the dungeon visit and are planned for the second bank trip.
- **Bond branch:** stage 6 is only the optional way to a Bond for an account without membership. With membership, switch to Members mode: stage 6 disappears and Waterfall Quest is next. S6-01 is now "Ranged to 50" (the goal and the method agree).
- **Members route:** Prayer 43 is a real step (S8-06) before Monkey Madness I, which now has a complete preparation (gold bar, ball of wool, Lockpick, food, prayer restoration, free slots, four loadouts) and a Daero choice computed from your live Attack XP. Agility 40 and Graceful are optional.
- **A gap closed:** the Members route (stage 6 skipped) never trained Woodcutting 36 for Lost City or Cooking 10 for Recipe for Disaster. S8-04 now trains them with Ranged 30, Slayer 18 and Crafting 31.
- **Goals recomputed from the route:** nine stages; a single number is guaranteed by the route, a range reaches the likely extras (Strength 25, iron ore, the Daero option), optional cells are marked. Old claims removed: Mining 30, Attack 10 in stage 1, Attack 50-60 and Strength 50-60 in stage 6.
- **Safety text:** food and eating advice is tied to the monster's biggest hit ("two hits"), not a fixed HP number; Brutus's specials are avoided by moving two tiles.
- **Data:** Hill Giants (not moss giants) drop the Big bones and nature runes; "Trout x10 or Salmon x8" is one row with an alternative; High Level Alchemy profit counts the Nature rune; the Bond price is one live figure.
- **Tools:** `check-data` now verifies that every goal has a source step, quest points match the route, no step needs a level the route does not guarantee, and quantities are well formed.
- **Plugin:** milestone varbits (`atLeast`), tool alternatives in the pre-flight, checklist and panel, and a stage line's own max hit. Protocol unchanged (6); the new fields are optional.
- **Checked:** `tsc` and build, 914 Vitest tests (new: `tests/routeModel.test.ts`), `check-data` (0 errors, 0 warnings), the full Java suite (new: `RouteModelTest`), the UI smoke test (254 checks). **Not run:** the Quest Helper generator (no Python on this machine). **Not confirmed in the live game:** the Structural pillar fight, the Waterfall loadouts and Monkey Madness I preparation.

- **Merged with the redesign:** the new Path page shows the optional / Bond branch label, "Loadouts" and "What to carry" notes in Preparation, and the reward choice next to the walkthrough. Below Ice Mountain lists the Beer in stage 15 so the desktop stops asking for it afterwards.
