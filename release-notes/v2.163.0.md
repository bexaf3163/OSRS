# OSRS Path 2.163.0

Members Prayer now offers bones at a gilded altar in a house party. You buy noted bones, unnote them at Phials in Rimmington for 5 coins each and use them by hand on a host's altar on world 330. The altar gives 350% of the burying XP, and the OSRS Wiki guide's pace is 2,550 bones an hour with banking.

The planner picks the bones by time and price. At current prices it offers Big bones from 1 to 99 at about 122,000 to 134,000 XP an hour for about 778,000 coins an hour. A Members Prayer plan to 99 now takes about 97 to 107 hours instead of 189 to 277 hours of scattering vile ashes, for a similar total cost. Dragon bones are 5 times faster but cost about 15.6 coins per XP instead of 5.8, so the plan uses them only when your time is worth more.

The altar knows Bones, Big bones, Babydragon, Dragon, Wyvern and Dagannoth bones. The Chaos altar and the libation bowl stay out for now: the Chaos altar is in level 38 Wilderness, and the libation bowl needs a bone-shard model.

Verified before delivery: the unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 132 Members-route quests), the browser checks, the Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
