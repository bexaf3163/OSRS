# OSRS Path 2.81.34

- One shopping trip: when a step still needs several items that are neither with you nor in the bank, the in-game helper shows one card, "Buy 3 items", listing all of them with where each is sold, instead of one item at a time. The Prepare tab no longer repeats them.
- Better Ranged armour: armour with the same Ranged attack is now ranked by its defence, so Ranged 20 and Defence 20 suggest the Studded body over a Hardleather body (both +8 Ranged; 98 against 66 total defence). Defence values come from the OSRS Wiki.
