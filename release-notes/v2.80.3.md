# OSRS Path 2.80.3

Firemaking gets a real rate.

- **XP per hour for your Firemaking level.** Normal, oak, willow, maple and yew logs, burnt in lines by a bank. Uses the wiki's 4 ticks a log, each log's level and XP, and the wiki's chance to light a log (lower at low levels, certain from 43).
- A failed light costs time, not the log, and the logs you burn are shown as the cost per hour.
- The figures are checked against the wiki guide's 1,485 logs an hour.

No gameplay automation. Saves, route IDs, adopted plans and progress are unchanged.
