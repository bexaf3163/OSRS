# OSRS Path 2.63.0

## Quest fights, estimated for your kit

The "Food for combat" panel of a quest fight now says what the fight is likely to cost you, from your real levels and worn gear:

- "Count Draynor: about 76 s on Accurate; you take about 37 damage (up to 65); bring 9 Trout or more."
- Ithoi the Navigator, Brutus, Melzar the Mad, Elvarg and the Black Knights get the same.

The style is the one that takes the least damage. "How this is worked out" shows your hit chance and its hit chance, and what was assumed. Ithoi
and Melzar attack with magic: their accuracy is rolled against your magic defence, so iron armour does not make them look harmless. Attacks the
model does not cover (Elvarg's dragonfire, Brutus's special) are named and the food is called a lower bound.

The fights the route does another way say so instead of a melee estimate: the Lesser demon in Melzar's Maze (from the safespot with Water
Strike) and the Ancient Guardian (by mining the four pillars).

The readiness line uses it too: when you carry less food than the estimate's high end, it says "Ready with cautions: Count Draynor may take up
to 9 Trout with your kit (an estimate); you carry 5."

## Not changed on purpose

The combat brief's fixed shopping amounts for S2-01 and S3-07 (Strength potion ×5, Salmon ×50) are not added: the Shopping page already buys the
whole-goal food from your personal plan, and the plan shows when Strength potions do not speed things up with your gear. Fixed amounts would
double the food and sometimes buy potions that do nothing.

Estimates only: the range holds about 9 outcomes in 10, and no kit guarantees survival.
