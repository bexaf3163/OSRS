# OSRS Path 2.74.15

Fixes found while playing with 2.74.14.

## The guide

- **A lost Anti-dragon shield comes free from Duke Horacio.** The card used to say "Buy: Grand Exchange, 120 gp". It now names Duke Horacio in Lumbridge Castle first, wherever you are, and the exchange stays as an alternative. Home Teleport takes you to Lumbridge.
- **Safety warnings on the in-game card are shown whole**, on both the closed and the open card. Before, a long warning was cut after two or three lines and lost its reason.
- **The open card says what comes next once.** It used to show the same line under "Then:" and again under "Next". The closed card keeps "Then:".

## Updates

- **The update banner shows the first real line of the release notes**, not the title "# OSRS Path …".

## Under the hood

- The session report no longer flags whole warnings as too long; only a warning longer than the card can hold is reported.

No gameplay automation or route reordering. Saves, route IDs and progress are unchanged.
