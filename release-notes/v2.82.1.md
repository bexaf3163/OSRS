# OSRS Path 2.82.1

- Food is required only when your own quest-fight estimate needs it, at that amount. The guide's amount is never a floor: a strong kit that takes no real damage is asked for no food (2.82.0 still required the guide's 5 Trout at Brutus and 10 Salmon at Ithoi).
- Training food, food for fights with no estimate yet, and the guide's extra margin are shown as labelled suggestions ("Optional: ..."), never as purchases, bank orders, readiness blocks or in-game checklist items. This removes the "Salmon or similar 0/10" HUD line on Ranged training.
- The quest planner no longer falls back to the text of unknown terms or quest names. When times are incomplete it ranks by fewer unknown parts, then the known minimum, then the quest's own cost, and says so with LOW confidence.
- Walking routes into buildings, upstairs and out of the Grand Exchange centre get a labelled allowance from the nearest reached tile instead of an unknown time, so most quests now have a full time.
- Path shows the planner's live order with each quest's minutes or unknown parts and the reason, above the guide walkthrough (written order, for reference). Previous/Next and the Journal follow the planner's order.
- A leftover training step in the game no longer outranks the planner when the plugin reports quest states.
- Live in-game acceptance of this release is still pending.
