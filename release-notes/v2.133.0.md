# OSRS Path 2.133.0

Three more quests join the Members route, so the Members quest-point total is now 134:

- **The Giant Dwarf** (Crafting 12, Firemaking 16, Magic 33, Thieving 14). The guide covers the trip to Keldagrim, the statue and Blasidar, and Vermundi's clothes (200 coins) with the book on costumes. It also covers the spinning machine, Dromund's boots (the right one with Telekinetic Grab), the Imcando axe with 3 sapphires and the Consortium tasks.
- **Hazeel Cult**. The guide covers Ceril Carnillean and Clivet, and the sewer valves (valve 1 left, then 2 to 5 right). On Ceril's side it covers Alomone (level 13) and the Carnillean armour, and Hazeel's side is also covered.
- **Troll Stronghold** (Agility 15, after Death Plateau). The guide covers the climbing boots from Tenzing, Dad (level 101) and a Troll General (level 113), both fought with Fire Bolt from a safespot (a staff of fire, 80 chaos and 240 air runes). It also covers the prison and cell keys and freeing Godric and Mad Eadgar.

Verified before delivery: 2,662 unit tests, the route gate (all 23 free-to-play quests and all 74 Members-route quests finish), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
