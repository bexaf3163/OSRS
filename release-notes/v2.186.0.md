# OSRS Path 2.186.0

## Members Hunter reaches chinchompas

The Members Hunter plan stopped at razor-backed kebbits, so from level 49 to 99 it offered one method that costs coins. It now has three more methods from the OSRS Wiki Hunter guide, checked on 25 November 2026:

- Embertailed jerboas at the Locus Oasis from 39 to 43, about 50,000 XP an hour with 3 box traps.
- Carnivorous chinchompas in the Tlati Rainforest from 63, about 61,000 to 72,000 XP an hour with 4 box traps.
- Carnivorous chinchompas with the fifth trap from 80, about 115,000 to 155,000 XP an hour.

Red chinchompas sell, so the plan counts the catch at live prices, and the method earns coins while it trains. All three need Eagles' Peak for box traps and Children of the Sun for Varlamore. Rates are without tick manipulation. The Locus Oasis is now on the map.

## The training card names a faster method behind quests

When a method that only undone quests lock would train at least a quarter faster than the current pick, the card says so: "Faster after a quest: Carnivorous chinchompas (5 traps) opens with Eagles' Peak and Children of the Sun." It stays silent for a smaller gain, or when a skill level is missing too.

## Costs name what they use

Mahogany Homes showed its planks and steel bars as "arrows", and falconry showed its fee the same way. Methods with an hourly material list now name it ("plank, steel bar") and a fixed fee shows as "fees".
