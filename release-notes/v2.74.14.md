# OSRS Path 2.74.14

## Your saves and updates

- **Two profile lists that disagree** no longer leave profile saving paused for good. The banner shows both lists and lets you merge them, which loses nothing, or keep either one. Both originals are kept as recovery copies first.
- **The safety copy made before the first save of a session** (`progress.bak.json`) used to fail silently. A failure now shows with the other copy errors in Settings and stays there after a restart until that copy succeeds.
- **An update download can be cancelled** from the update banner. A failed download stays in the banner with its reason and a **Try again** button. No partial file is ever left behind, including when you retry straight away.

## The guide

- **Owning the items counts.** A step that exists to get items no longer blocks the next quest when the game shows you have them. For example, a worn Anti-dragon shield now lets you start Elvarg even if "Get the Anti-dragon shield" was never ticked. Missing or unread items never undo a step you marked done.
- **Demon Slayer in three trips:** the coin and bucket first, then the 25 bones for Traiborn, then Silverlight and food for Delrith. The bones no longer block the start or crowd out your food.
- **Members tools on a free account:** a Dragon pickaxe or axe kept from an old membership is not counted as the tool on a free-to-play account or world. The app names it as "members only".
- **Ranged training** now says that one shot in five loses its arrow, so picking the rest up makes a stack last about five times longer.
- **Shopping:** Grand Exchange purchases sit under one heading and shop purchases under another, so "Buy at the Grand Exchange" is no longer repeated on every row. A batch larger than the exchange's 4-hour buy limit says how many rounds it takes.

## Under the hood

- The plugin's session log now records your frame rate and how long the guide's overlays take to draw each frame, so a real play session shows whether the guide costs FPS.
- The release check now confirms that the plugin inside the app is this version's build.
- Electron 44.9.3 and other dependency updates fix five published security advisories; `npm audit` reports none.

No gameplay automation or route reordering. Saves, route IDs and progress are unchanged.
