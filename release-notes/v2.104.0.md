# OSRS Path 2.104.0

- Two new Members quests: Plague City (1 QP) and Sheep Herder (4 QP). Both join the early Members stage of a Members account's route and never show in free-to-play mode. Their stages, tiles and texts come from Quest Helper and the Wiki through the quest pipeline.
- Plague City's Hangover cure is made, not bought: chocolate dust on a bucket of milk, then snape grass on the chocolatey milk. The app plans both steps and adds their time to the preparation. The picture from Edmond's house is picked up during the quest, and the card moves on once it is in the bag.
- Construction training for Members accounts, from the OSRS Wiki's Construction training guide: Mahogany Homes contracts for every level (beginner 35,000-50,000 XP/h, novice from 20 at 80,000-95,000, adept from 50 at 130,000-150,000, expert from 70 at 185,000-210,000). They need a player-owned house (1,000 coins at an estate agent), a hammer, a saw, planks and steel bars. Every Members skill now has a training method.
- Better time estimates: a quest that needs a made item no longer leaves its whole preparation time unknown. Turns with an unknown remaining time in the Members route gate dropped from 87 to 82.

Verified before delivery: 2,631 unit tests (new: a made item takes its recipe time in the preparation trip), the F2P route gate (23 of 23 quests, 0 of 211 turns with an unknown time), the Members route gate (39 of 39 quests, no check, 82 of 308 turns with an unknown time), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
