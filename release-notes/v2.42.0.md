**Skill leveling guides: pick a skill, track it, and the game leads you.**

- **Roadmaps for all 23 skills** (free and members), built from the wiki training guides and quest rewards: quest XP skips first (Knight's Sword to Smithing 29, Waterfall Quest to Attack and Strength 30, Sea Slug to Fishing 24, Tourist Trap, Witch's House, Monk's Friend) and then the current methods (Giants' Foundry, Guardians of the Rift, Camdozaal, Forestry, rooftop agility). A step that a finished quest already covers is skipped.
- **The Skills page is a hub:** every card has a "Leveling roadmap" button that opens a drawer with a level-bracket timeline (1–29, 29–40, 40–60, 60–99) and three filters: Fastest / Quest-Driven, AFK / Budget, F2P Only (a free account stops at its free cap, and members-only skills say so).
- **Track Skill Path** pins the skill to a card on the Path screen, in place of the quest route (one click goes back). In the game the arrow leads to the step's place, the rock, tree or NPC is highlighted, and the HUD shows a chip like "🎯 Iron ore (powermining) (Lvl 15 → 45)".
- **It advances by itself:** when the skill reaches the step's target level the game plays a soft chime, moves to the next step and points to the next place. The step is derived from your real level, so it survives restarts and level jumps (lamps).
- **Safe by design:** the app sends the path in the same snapshot as before (one optional new part); an old plugin ignores it. A bad path is refused whole. Stopping the track gives the quest step back from the same snapshot. Nothing is clicked for you.
- **Checked:** app tests (new `tests/skillsGuide.test.ts`: every guide against the schema, quest XP chains, quest-first order, every filter covered at every level, a StatChanged simulation), data checks, the production build, the browser interface checks and all plugin tests (new `SkillPathTest`). Not yet confirmed in the live game. Tiles come from the wiki maps and have not been walked; the objects are highlighted by name, not id, so a rock or tree at a slightly different spot is still found. The methods follow the wiki and will age as the game changes.
