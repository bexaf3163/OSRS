# OSRS Path 2.115.0

- 59 Members money methods are now reviewed by hand, up from 24. New this release: cleaning all 13 grimy herbs, all 12 unfinished potions, ultracompost, bird nests, unicorn horns, headless arrows, ruby bracelets, topaz rings, maple longbows, mahogany planks, silk to the Ardougne merchant, crystal keys and mole parts. Each herb method names its buy limit and the hourly cap it sets.
- Mahogany planks are planned at the Varrock sawmill on foot, about 1,200 an hour. The guide's 6,900 needs a plank sack and a ring of the elements, which the route does not plan.
- The two item-spawn methods the Members route uses, spades in Falador and monk's robes in Edgeville, are reviewed by hand too. Monk's robes now count pairs, as the guide does.
- Built guides no longer show empty "Bring: + recommended" lines.
- The Members route is shorter: 376 turns instead of 404, and the turns with an unknown remaining time fell from 6 to 4. The gate's limit is lowered to 4.

Verified before delivery: 2,643 unit tests, the F2P route gate (23 of 23 quests, 0 unknown turns), the Members route gate (51 of 51 quests, 4 of 376 turns unknown), the browser and Electron desktop checks, the plugin tests, typecheck/build, data/fixtures, structure, unused-code and dead-CSS gates. Nothing was tested in the real game.
