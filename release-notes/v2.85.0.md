# OSRS Path 2.85.0

- Quest fights now steer the plan instead of stopping it with a check. When the next quest's fight is unsafe for your live levels and worn kit:
  - The planner first offers the weapon or armour your coins cover, with the shop and the map arrow.
  - If better kit you can already wear costs more than you have, it earns the difference with the best Money method for your character.
  - Otherwise it trains the lowest of Attack, Strength and Defence toward the level at which even the high food estimate of the fight fits in your bag. The target drops by itself as your kit improves.
- Ernest the Chicken: its level 22 Skeleton is now a fight the app estimates, so the quest no longer waits on a "Verify combat readiness" check.
- Chickens and Wizards, which the training methods fight, now have combat numbers from the OSRS Wiki, so those training spots are assessed instead of asking you to review them.
- Live in-game acceptance of this release is still pending.
