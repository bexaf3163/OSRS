# OSRS Path 2.180.0

## The Barbarian rod has a source

The audit checked every item that the first method of every training plan needs, in 2,342 plans across both modes, and asked whether the route can get it. One could not. Barbarian fishing at Otto's Grotto, from Fishing 58 on Members, needs a Barbarian rod. You cannot buy or trade it, and the app knew no other source, so the route would have stopped with a check.

The rod is free: once you ask Otto Godblessed about barbarian fishing, you can take it from under the bed in his cabin, and the bed gives another if you lose it (OSRS Wiki). The route now sends you there to take it. The training sweep that runs with every test run now also fails when a plan needs an item the route cannot buy or take.

## Gear checked on 120 accounts

The audit also asked the Gear page for advice at every level from 1 to 99 in both modes, with no coins, 10,000, a million and 100 million. Every pick was wearable at those levels, allowed in that mode, one per slot and within the coins. With enough coins, level 40 brings a rune scimitar, an amulet of strength and full rune.

Verified before delivery: 2,870 unit tests, the free-to-play route gate, all five parts of the Members route gate, the browser checks, typecheck and build, structure, unused-code, dead-CSS and data checks. Nothing was tested in the real game.
