# OSRS Path 2.107.0

- New Members quest: Sea Slug (1 QP, needs Firemaking 30). It joins the early Members stage of a Members account's route and never shows in free-to-play mode. Every stage is on the card, from Caroline north of Witchaven to the fishing platform and back.
- New Members quest: Observatory Quest (2 QP, 2,250 Crafting XP). Every stage is on the card, from the professor to the dungeon and back. The level 42 goblin guard at the kitchen gate is estimated with your kit, and Trout x5 (or similar food) is a required item.
- Better time estimates for Members accounts: a fight that needs a level the quest itself requires (Prayer 43 in Monkey Madness I) is now judged at that level, so its preparation time is known while you train. Turns with an unknown remaining time in the Members route gate dropped from 7 to 6.
